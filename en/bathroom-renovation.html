Turnkey bathroom renovation in Kyiv

12-month warranty · estimate within ±5% when CAS renovates

The bathroom is the hardest room in any renovation: water, electrics, ventilation and tiling all have to be done flawlessly. We renovate bathrooms turnkey — from stripping out to the mirror and lighting, with a tile layout plan.

A bathroom with no leaks and no rework

Most bathroom problems come from cutting corners on hidden work: waterproofing, falls and ventilation. We do these stages by the book and send photo reports of each one, because once the tiles are on, nobody will see them again.

The tile layout is planned in advance: joints line up with niches, concealed frames and mirror edges, and cuts go into inconspicuous corners.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey bathroom renovation cost?

It depends on the size, tiles and sanitaryware. We give the exact price after measuring and fix it in the estimate; if CAS renovates, the total stays within ±5%. If the bathroom is part of a full apartment renovation, it is included in the overall price from $550/m². The price from $550/m² covers works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are priced separately in the estimate.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

We set the timeline in the contract after the survey — from 2 weeks; waterproofing and screed need time to dry, so we never rush those stages.

Do you renovate just the bathroom, without the rest of the apartment?

Yes, we take on bathrooms and WCs as separate projects.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.
