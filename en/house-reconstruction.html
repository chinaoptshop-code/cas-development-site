When reconstruction beats new construction

If the structure of the house is sound and you are happy with the plot and location, reconstruction gives you a modern house without building from scratch: a new layout, an extension, modern MEP systems and finishes. We decide after the survey — sometimes it is more honest to recommend new construction.

Since 2016 we have delivered 150+ projects — houses, apartments, offices, restaurants. We take on reconstruction, extensions, replacement of MEP systems and complete finishing.

One project for architecture, interior and engineering

In an old house decisions are interconnected: moving a wall changes the layout, a new boiler room changes the pipe routing, new windows change the facade. So we run architecture, interior and engineering in one project, and the works follow it, with technical supervision and photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

The cost of reconstruction is calculated per project after the survey. Benchmarks: new construction from $600/m², a turnkey house with finishes from $1,100/m², renovation from $550/m² (works and rough materials).

Questions and answers

How much does house reconstruction cost?

We calculate it per project after the survey: the cost depends on the condition of the structures and the scope of changes. Benchmarks — construction from $600/m², renovation from $550/m².

Do you add storeys or build extensions?

Yes, we take on reconstruction and extensions — after a structural survey and design.

Do you replace MEP systems?

Yes: electrics, heating and boiler room, water supply, drainage, ventilation.

Can we live in the house during the works?

It depends on the scope. With a full replacement of systems and finishes — no; with phased reconstruction we discuss the schedule.

Do you design the interior of the reconstructed house?

Yes, we run architecture and interior in one project. Design from $30/m² (minimum $2,500).

What warranty do you give?

12 months on completed works; price, timeline and payment stages are fixed in the contract.
