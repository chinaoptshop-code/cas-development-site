Renovating a two-level apartment of 104–179 m²

A two-level apartment of this size feels closer to a townhouse than to a regular apartment. So we start by distributing the levels: a day zone with a kitchen-living room and a guest WC, a night zone with bedrooms, wardrobes and bathrooms. We make the staircase the centrepiece rather than an obstacle.

On two levels the engineering is more complex: separate air conditioning for each floor, ventilation for every bathroom, underfloor heating, staircase lighting with motion sensors. We put sound insulation in the slab under the bedrooms so footsteps downstairs are not heard upstairs.

For large areas the fit-out is especially important: a lot of furniture, lighting and textiles is needed, and logistics are best handled by one contractor. Renovation rules in the building are usually set by the management company — we take them into account.

For reference: developer — Citex Development, address — 54 Yevhena Sverstiuka St. We do not represent the developer or the management company — we work directly with the apartment owner.

Questions and answers

How much does an apartment renovation in Rusaniv Residence cost?

Turnkey renovation starts from $550/m² (works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are extra, per the estimate), a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500), furnishing from $250/m². The calculation for a 140 m² apartment is in the budget guide above; we fix the exact amount in the estimate after the inspection.

Where does the renovation of a two-level apartment start?

With the design project: distribution of zones between levels, the staircase, engineering and sound insulation. Only then do we draw up the estimate and the work schedule.

Can you fully furnish the apartment?

Yes. Custom furniture and furniture from China, lighting, appliances, textiles and décor — until the apartment is ready to move in.

What warranty do you give?

We hand over the apartment with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Can I follow the renovation remotely?

Yes. We send photo reports from the site via Telegram, and the price, timeline and payment stages are fixed in the contract.
