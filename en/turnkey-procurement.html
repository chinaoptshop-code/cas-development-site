Interior procurement: how we work

Turnkey procurement is not “buying furniture”: it means assembling dozens of items from different manufacturers so that they match, fit the dimensions and arrive on time. We draw up a specification with item codes and prices, place the orders, check quality on arrival, deliver, assemble and arrange everything.

For restaurants, cafés and hotels (HoReCa) we choose furniture and lighting that withstand intensive use. For rental apartments — a package that is easy to maintain and looks good in photos. Procurement can be combined with renovation and a design project or ordered separately to your own design.

How much does interior procurement cost?

Full turnkey furnishing of an apartment costs from $250/m². Catalogue prices are indicative: the final price, availability and lead time are confirmed after your request. Delivery from China takes 35–70 days.

Questions and answers

What does turnkey procurement include?

Furniture, lighting, sanitaryware, textiles, decor and appliances if needed: specification, ordering, delivery, assembly, installation and final styling.

Do you equip restaurants, cafés and hotels?

Yes, we work with HoReCa: furniture for dining rooms and terraces, decorative and technical lighting, textiles, table settings, sanitaryware.

Can you furnish a rental apartment?

Yes. We choose hard-wearing furniture and textiles, appliances and lighting, and style the apartment for listing photos. Full apartment furnishing from $250/m².

Where do the furniture and lighting come from?

Some directly from factories in China (delivery 35–70 days), some we buy in Ukraine. The final price, availability and lead time are confirmed after your request.

Do you work with companies?

Yes: we equip offices, restaurants, hotels and rental apartments and invoice companies registered in Ukraine with closing documents; invoicing for EU companies — being set up. We work in Kyiv, across Ukraine, in Europe and worldwide.
