CAS Development referral programme

Reward of 5–15% of the works amount under the contract

Know someone planning interior design, a renovation or building a house? Recommend CAS Development: when the client signs a contract, you receive a reward of 5–15% of the works amount under the contract. The size depends on the scope and type of project; we agree the terms in writing.

Who can recommend

CAS clients

You have already done design, a renovation or a house with us and know how we work: tell your friends and acquaintances about us.

Partners

Suppliers, furniture and material manufacturers, related contractors who meet clients at the start of a project.

Designers and architects

Your client is looking for a team to carry out a renovation or construction to your design: recommend CAS for the implementation.

Estate agents

Buyers of a new apartment or house often plan design and renovation straight away: tell them who to contact.

How it works

  1. You recommendLeave your number in the form below and briefly describe who you are recommending and what they need, or message our manager on Telegram or Viber.
  2. We record the referralWe call you back, clarify the details and record the referral and the reward terms in writing.
  3. We work with the clientPhone consultation, measurement, estimate and contract are handled by our team.
  4. The client signs a contractThe reward is due once the recommended client has signed a contract with CAS.
  5. You receive the reward5–15% of the works amount under the contract: the size depends on the scope and type of project. Timing and method of payment by agreement.

Who you can recommend

Interior design

Apartments, houses, offices and restaurants: design project from $30/m², minimum $2,500.

Turnkey renovation

Apartment and house renovation by our own in-house team, from $550/m² for works and rough materials.

House construction

Shell from $600/m², turnkey house from $1,100/m²; timber-frame, modular, timber houses.

Commercial premises

Offices from $350/m², restaurants, hotels and turnkey business premises.

Questions and answers

How big is the reward?

5–15% of the works amount under the contract signed by the recommended client. The size depends on the scope and type of project; specific terms are agreed in writing.

When and how is the reward paid?

Timing and method of payment by agreement: we discuss them and record them in writing together with the reward terms.

Do I need to be a CAS client?

No. Clients, partners, designers, architects and estate agents can all recommend.

How will you know the client came from me?

You leave a request in the form or message our manager, and we record the referral. The client can also mention your name when contacting us.

Do I need to take part in negotiations?

No. A recommendation is enough; the consultation, measurement, estimate and contract are handled by our team.

When is no reward paid?

The reward is tied to the contract: if the recommended client does not sign a contract with CAS, no reward is due.
