Questions and answers

How much does a house design cost?

Design from $30/m², minimum project budget $2,500. We give the exact price after getting to know the plot and the brief.

How much does a turnkey house cost?

Construction with turnkey finishing — from $1,100/m². Furniture and furnishing are calculated separately; we can bring them directly from China.

Do you also do the interior and landscaping?

Yes — architecture, interior and landscaping in one project.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.
