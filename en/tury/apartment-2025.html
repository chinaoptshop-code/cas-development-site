About the tour

A 360° tour of a finished apartment in Kyiv: the interior after the works were completed, shot with a 360 camera. Walk through the rooms and look at the finishes, furniture and lighting the way you would on a site visit.

More about the property and the tour

In the tour: bedroom, living room, kitchen, entrance hall, bathroom — 8 viewpoints. Switch with the arrows on the photo, the ‹ › buttons or the «Rooms» menu; in a VR headset, use the VR button.

This is a completed project, not a visualisation: the photos show real walls, joints, plumbing and lighting. That is how a renovation should be judged — by the result, not by a render.

CAS renovates apartments with its own team: turnkey renovation in Kyiv — from $550/m² (labour and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate). During the works the client gets photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and a 24/7 online camera on site can be added for an extra fee. If there is no design yet, we start with apartment interior design.
