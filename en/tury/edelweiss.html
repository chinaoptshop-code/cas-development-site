About the tour

360 visualisations of the Edelweiss apartment design project, 108 m²: a light interior with soft textures, a walk-in wardrobe and graphic art. This is a 3D model of the project, not photos of a finished apartment.

More about the property and the tour

In the tour: hall, living room, kitchen, dining room, bedroom, guest bedroom, laundry, bathroom, shower room — 19 viewpoints. Switch with the arrows on the photo, the ‹ › buttons or the «Rooms» menu; in a VR headset, use the VR button.

The kitchen is combined with the living and dining area in one space, the walls behind the beds are finished with textile panels, and the walk-in wardrobe has glass fronts. The brief, solution and photos are on the Edelweiss project page.

Why a 360° tour: you can walk through a design project before the renovation and see how the rooms flow into one another. Layout, colours and furniture are easier to agree on the model — changes go into the project, not onto the site. The model is built to real dimensions: our specialist does a 3D scan of the space.
