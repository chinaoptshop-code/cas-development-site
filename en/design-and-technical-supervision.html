cost per estimate, depending on the property and visit frequency

Why supervision if there is a crew

The crew is responsible for execution, but not always for following the idea: without control, details get simplified, materials are swapped for “equivalents” and niche sizes stop matching the furniture. Designer supervision preserves the design; technical supervision preserves the quality of what will be hidden under the finishes.

The most important part is concealed works: electrics, plumbing, waterproofing, screed, the base for stone. We accept them before they are closed up and record them in photos, so that walls do not have to be opened a year later.

How it works at CAS

When CAS carries out the renovation to our design, technical and designer supervision are built into the process: the designer and engineer see the site at every stage, and every 1–2 weeks you get a photo report and sign stage certificates. Works carry a 12-month warranty.

If the design project is ours and another contractor does the renovation, designer supervision can be ordered separately. The format and frequency of visits, and the terms of technical supervision on someone else's site, are agreed individually.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between designer and technical supervision?

Designer supervision checks that the renovation follows the design: materials, dimensions, lighting, furniture. Technical supervision checks the quality and technology of the works: geometry, concealed works, engineering.

How much does supervision cost?

It depends on the area, duration of works and visit frequency — we quote after the brief. When CAS carries out the renovation, quality control is already part of our work.

Can I order designer supervision separately?

Yes, for our design project — even if another contractor does the renovation.

I am abroad — how do I control the renovation?

We send photo reports every 1–2 weeks, agree key decisions remotely and sign certificates for each stage.

What are concealed works and why accept them?

Everything that will later be covered by finishes: wiring, pipes, waterproofing, screed. Acceptance before closing up is the only way to check them without demolition.

What warranty do you give?

12 months on works carried out by CAS.
