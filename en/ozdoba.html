Furniture, lighting & decor from China for your project

Ozdoba is the procurement service of CAS Development, an interior design and fit-out studio from Kyiv. We supply furniture, lighting, sanitaryware, finishes and decor for hotels, restaurants, cafés, offices and residential developments. Items are made to order — colour, size, material and finish are tailored to your project; lead time from China is 30–60 days. We hand-picked many of them at trade fairs and factories in Guangzhou. Choose items or a ready-made package, download a quote as PDF — our manager will confirm prices, availability and lead time.

  1. SelectionAdd items to the cart or take a ready-made package for your space.

Terms

  • Minimum order — $500 (≈ 23 000 ₴); at least 10 pcs (units) of each item. We do not sell single pieces.
  • Made to order: colour (including RAL), size, material, upholstery and finish.
  • Lead time from China — 30–60 days.
  • Delivery and customs duties are not fixed — calculated individually depending on order volume.
  • Payment: by card (payment link from our manager), invoice for companies registered in Ukraine, cash (UAH, USD, EUR) or USDT. No instalments and no cash on delivery.
  • Volume discount on the total goods amount: from $5 000 — 3%, from $10 000 — 5%, from $20 000 — 7%. Designers and architects get a personal partner code — discounts are not combined, the larger one applies.
  • Invoices for companies registered in Ukraine with closing documents; invoices for EU companies are being set up.

Questions and answers

Can I order one or two pieces?

No, we do not sell single pieces: Ozdoba furnishes whole projects. The minimum order is $500 (≈ 23 000 ₴) and at least 10 pcs (units) of each item. Different items can be combined in one order.

Can the colour, size or material be changed?

Yes. Items are made to order at the factory, so colour (including RAL), dimensions, material, upholstery and finish can be adapted to your project. Lead time from China is 30–60 days.

Are there discounts?

Yes, a volume discount on the total goods amount, packages included: from $5 000 — 3%, from $10 000 — 5%, from $20 000 — 7%. The cart calculates it automatically. Designers and architects get a personal partner code — discounts are not combined, the larger one applies.

Are the catalogue prices final?

No, they are indicative and shown in Ukrainian hryvnia. The final proposal — in UAH, USD or EUR — follows after our manager confirms prices, availability and lead time.

How much is delivery?

Delivery and customs duties are calculated individually depending on order volume, as goods are shipped to order from China. Our manager quotes them together with the confirmed prices and lead time (30–60 days).

Do you work with companies from the EU?

Yes. CAS Development has worked on projects in Poland, Germany, France, Spain, the USA and Bali. Invoices for companies registered in Ukraine are available now; invoices for EU companies are being set up. Payment by card, cash (UAH, USD, EUR) or USDT is also possible.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Add items or a package to the cart and press “Download quote (PDF)” — enter your company or project name and you will get a PDF with photos, quantities, prices and terms. Send the quote number to our manager to confirm it.
