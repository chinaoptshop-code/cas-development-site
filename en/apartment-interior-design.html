How we work

  1. Introduction and measurementsWe look at the apartment and talk about how you live: who will live here, how many people, what matters to you.
  2. LayoutWe propose layout options — you choose one and we refine it.
  3. 3D and styleWe show how every room will look and make revisions until approval.
  4. Working drawingsDrawings and specifications that let the crew work without guesswork.
  5. EstimateWe price the renovation to the design. If we do the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.
  6. ImplementationIf you wish, we take on the renovation and furnishing with furniture direct from China.

Questions and answers

How much does an apartment design project in Kyiv cost?

From $30/m², minimum project budget $2,500. We give the exact price after seeing the apartment.

What does a design project include?

Measurements, layout options, 3D visualisations, working drawings, materials and furniture specification, renovation estimate.

Can you renovate to the design?

Yes, with our own team. If we carry out the renovation to our design, we guarantee the total will not deviate from the estimate by more than 5%.

Do you work with new-builds?

Yes — both new-build apartments from developers and older apartments needing a complete overhaul.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.
