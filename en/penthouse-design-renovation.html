How a penthouse differs from an ordinary apartment

A penthouse is the top floor with large glazed areas, terraces and often two levels. There are more decisions to make before the renovation: how the space near the windows heats and cools, where to hide indoor AC units, how to drain the terrace, what load the slab can take under a stone bath or heavy furniture.

So we start with measurements and layout, and put the engineering into the working drawings together with the interior. Works and material lifting are agreed with the building's management company; the house rules are built into the schedule and estimate.

How much penthouse design and renovation cost

Design project from $30/m², minimum $2,500; the full package with designer supervision from $45/m². Renovation from $550/m²: works and rough materials, while finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate. In penthouses the finishing part usually weighs more because of stone, large glazing and bespoke furniture, so the exact estimate is prepared after the design.

For 210 m² — the area of our Linden Penthouse design project — the base calculation is 210 m² × $30 for design and 210 m² × $550 for renovation. If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.

Questions and answers

Who does penthouse design and renovation in Kyiv?

CAS Development — a Kyiv studio that since 2016 has provided turnkey design, renovation and procurement; our design projects include the 210 m² Linden Penthouse. The renovation is carried out by our own salaried team.

How much does a penthouse renovation cost?

From $550/m² — works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate. Design project from $30/m², minimum $2,500.

How long does a penthouse renovation take?

The timeline is fixed in the contract after the design and estimate: it depends on area, engineering scope and long-lead materials. The shortest jobs at the studio take from 2 weeks, but a full penthouse renovation takes much longer.

Do you design the penthouse terrace?

Yes, together with the interior: waterproofing, surfacing, lighting, planting and outdoor furniture.

Do you take a new-build penthouse with no finishes?

Yes: from bare concrete to furniture, including layout and engineering. If there is an existing renovation, we demolish and rework it to the new design.
