Why turnkey renovation pays off

When design, works and supply are run by one company, there is no downtime between contractors and no arguments about who is to blame. We prepare the estimate before the start and keep to it: if CAS does the renovation, the total stays within ±5%.

Since 2016 we have completed more than 150 projects — apartments, private houses, offices, restaurants and salons. Furniture and materials come directly from China, so furnishing costs less.

A renovation from scratch: an 86 m² apartment in 19 stages

Scroll and the apartment goes from bare concrete to keys in the right order of works. Photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

  1. 3D scan of the apartmentOur specialist scans the apartment with a 3D scanner: the point cloud gives real sizes, heights, slab level differences and angles. The site manager checks it against the plan and service entries.
  2. Design project on the floorThe plan goes onto the concrete: zoning, furniture, tile layout, socket, lighting and plumbing drawings. From here everything follows the drawings, done by our in-house crews.
  3. Handover and preparationWe check the developer's shell: wall geometry, slab level differences, windows and service entries. Then we clean, dedust and prime the concrete.
  4. PartitionsWet areas get 100 mm aerated block on adhesive with reinforced courses; rooms get plasterboard on metal studs with mineral wool for sound insulation. Openings follow the project.
  5. Electrics and low voltageChases, back boxes, junction boxes and cables per drawing; dedicated lines for the kitchen, water heater and underfloor heating. A panel with breakers and RCDs; internet, TV and intercom run separately. Air-conditioning lines go in now too.
  6. Plumbing and manifoldFrom the metered entry to a manifold, then PPR or multilayer pipes to every point. Drains fall 2–3 % to the stack; a frame for the wall-hung WC.
  7. Waterproofing wet areasLiquid waterproofing over primer: reinforcing tape in corners and collars on pipe outlets, a 20 cm upstand on walls and higher around the bath and shower. The second coat goes on before tiling.
  8. Underfloor heating and screedEdge strip along the walls, insulation and heating cable where the floor is heated. Guide rails set with a laser level, then a semi-dry screed reinforced with fibre.
  9. Plaster to guide beadsPrimer, guide beads set with a laser level, then plaster: gypsum in rooms, cement in wet areas. Flat walls and square corners for tiles, paint and built-in furniture.
  10. Reveals and sillsWe check and adjust the developer's windows and add the vapour-barrier tape where it is missing. Then insulated reveals and window sills.
  11. CeilingsMetal grid on hangers, two layers of plasterboard, recesses for LED strips and curtain tracks. A bulkhead hides the hood duct and air-conditioning lines.
  12. TilingLayout per project, from the centre line, no thin cuts. Adhesive with a notched trowel over the second waterproofing coat, even joints on spacers, then grout and sealant in the corners.
  13. Filling and paintingPlasterboard joints get reinforcing tape. Base and finish filler, sanding under a work light, primer and two coats of paint.
  14. Flooring and skirtingParquet or SPC on a dry, flat screed over underlay, with an expansion gap at the walls. Skirting is flush-mounted or paintable.
  15. DoorsFlush doors level with the wall for the bathroom, WC and closet; standard doors with frames and casings for the rooms. A steel entrance door with seals.
  16. Built-in kitchen and wardrobesOur joiners fit it module by module: base units, worktop, wall units, the appliance tower; then the closet, hallway wardrobe and kids' room.
  17. Fixtures and lightingWe fit the bath, basin, wall-hung WC and mixers and connect the appliances. Lighting scenes: task light in the kitchen, warm evening light, LED in the recesses.
  18. Decor and cleaningTextiles, plants, art and small things per the design project. Then a professional post-renovation clean: no dust, film or mortar traces.
  19. Handover and a 12-month warrantyWe walk the apartment with you against a checklist, sign the acts and hand over the hidden-services drawings and the keys. Warranty: 12 months.

Questions and answers

What does turnkey renovation include?

Design project, estimate, demolition, rough and finishing works, electrics, plumbing, purchasing of materials, furniture, cleaning and handover.

How much does it cost?

Apartments and houses from $550 per m², offices from $350 per m². The price from $550/m² covers works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are priced separately in the estimate. If CAS does the renovation, the final amount stays within ±5% of the approved estimate.

Can I order renovation without design?

Yes, if you already have a project. Or we design it ourselves — from $30 per m².

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.
