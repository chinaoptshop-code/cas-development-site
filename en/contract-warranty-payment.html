A works contract is the basis of every renovation

We sign renovation and construction as a works contract under Chapter 61 of the Civil Code of Ukraine: the contractor carries out the work to the client’s brief, and the client accepts and pays for the result. Everything that matters to you — scope, price, timeline and warranty — goes into the contract and its annexes before work starts.

The most common problem with renovation contracts is a vague scope: “renovate the apartment” with no list of works. That is why our contract comes with an itemised estimate of works and materials and, for renovation to a design project, the drawings.

What the CAS contract covers

  1. Subject of the contractProperty, address, list and volume of works; the design project or drawings as an annex.
  2. EstimateWorks and materials item by item. If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.
  3. TimelineStart date, stage schedule and handover date. Timelines start from 2 weeks, depending on the scope.
  4. Payment termsThe schedule you chose: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages; you start with an advance from 10%.
  5. Stage acceptanceEach stage is handed over with a signed completion certificate, so you see what is done and what you pay for.
  6. ReportingPhoto reports every 1–2 weeks; for an extra fee, an online camera on site 24/7.
  7. Warranty36 months on completed works from the date of the final certificate; how defects are fixed during the warranty is set out in the contract.
  8. LiabilityWhat happens if deadlines or payments are missed, and how disputes are resolved.
  9. Force majeureCircumstances beyond the parties’ control (including martial law and power outages) and how they shift deadlines.
  10. TerminationConditions and procedure: settlement for works actually completed and accepted.

Payment schedules that suit you

50/50

Half at the start, half after handover with the completion certificate.

50/30/20

Start, an intermediate stage and the final payment after acceptance.

50/40/10

A small final payment stays until the handover certificate is signed.

Split by stages

Pay for each stage separately; start with an advance from 10%.

Ways to pay

Invoice

For companies registered in Ukraine, with closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies is being set up.

Card

Payment by bank card.

Cash

Cash by arrangement.

Who does the work

Our own crews, not subcontractors

Renovation is carried out by three in-house salaried CAS crews for different levels of complexity and types of work, with a site manager checking every stage.

Online control

Photo reports from site every 1–2 weeks; for an extra fee, a 24/7 online camera so you can see the site at any time.

Estimate within ±5%

The estimate guarantee applies only when CAS carries out the renovation: then the total stays within ±5% of it.

Guarantees

36-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 36-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

Three in-house salaried crews

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors: three crews for different levels of complexity and types of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages; you start with an advance from 10%.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

Can I see the contract before signing?

Yes. Leave a request and we will send a sample contract; for your property we prepare a contract with an estimate and schedule.

What is the warranty on renovation?

36 months on completed works from the date of the final certificate.

How can I pay?

On the schedule you choose: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages; you start with an advance from 10%. By invoice (for companies registered in Ukraine), card or cash.

Can the total grow during the renovation?

If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the approved estimate.

Who works on site?

Our own crews: three in-house salaried CAS crews for different complexity and types of work, no subcontracting.

How do I follow the works from abroad?

Photo reports every 1–2 weeks and, for an extra fee, an online camera on site 24/7.

How long does a renovation take?

The timeline is fixed in the contract and starts from 2 weeks, depending on the scope.
