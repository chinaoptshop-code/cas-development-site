Renovation contract, warranty and payment

How a renovation or construction contract with CAS Development works: what it covers, how we hand over each stage, what the warranty is and how you can pay. We will send a sample contract on request.

A works contract is the basis of every renovation

We sign renovation and construction as a works contract under Chapter 61 of the Civil Code of Ukraine: the contractor carries out the work to the client’s brief, and the client accepts and pays for the result. Everything that matters to you — scope, price, timeline and warranty — goes into the contract and its annexes before work starts.

The most common problem with renovation contracts is a vague scope: “renovate the apartment” with no list of works. That is why our contract comes with an itemised estimate of works and materials and, for renovation to a design project, the drawings.

What the CAS contract covers

  1. Subject of the contractProperty, address, list and volume of works; the design project or drawings as an annex.
  2. EstimateWorks and materials item by item. If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.
  3. TimelineStart date, stage schedule and handover date. Timelines start from 2 weeks, depending on the scope.
  4. Payment termsThe schedule you chose: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more. The deposit and the certificate each payment is tied to are fixed in the contract.
  5. Stage acceptanceEach stage is handed over with a signed completion certificate, so you see what is done and what you pay for.
  6. ReportingPhoto reports every 1–2 weeks; for an extra fee, an online camera on site 24/7.
  7. Warranty12 months on completed works from the day they are accepted under the final certificate. For construction, major renovation and reconstruction, the warranty on the building follows Article 884 of the Civil Code of Ukraine and the contract. How to report a defect and how fast we fix it is set out in the contract.
  8. LiabilityWhat happens if deadlines or payments are missed, and how disputes are resolved.
  9. Force majeureForce majeure (Article 617 of the Civil Code) means events that directly prevent a specific obligation from being performed — for example, shelling or damage to the property. The affected party notifies the other and confirms it with a Chamber of Commerce certificate or other documents; deadlines move for as long as the event lasts. Martial law or scheduled power cuts are not force majeure by themselves — how they affect deadlines is agreed separately in the contract.
  10. TerminationConditions and procedure: settlement for works actually completed and materials purchased for the property (Article 849 of the Civil Code).

Payment schedules that suit you

50/50

50% at the start, 50% after handover and signing of the final certificate.

50/30/20

50% at the start, 30% after an intermediate stage is accepted under a certificate, 20% after the final certificate is signed.

50/40/10

50% at the start, 40% after an intermediate stage, the last 10% after the final certificate is signed.

Split by stages

Pay for each stage separately; start with a deposit of 10% or more.

Ways to pay

Invoice

For companies registered in Ukraine — with or without VAT, individually — with closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies is being set up.

Other methods

Other payment methods are agreed in the contract.

Who does the work

Our own team

Renovation is carried out by the in-house CAS team of salaried staff for different levels of complexity and types of work; custom kitchens and wardrobes are made by our staff and partners. A site manager checks every stage.

Online control

Photo reports from site every 1–2 weeks; for an extra fee, a 24/7 online camera so you can see the site at any time.

Estimate within ±5%

The estimate guarantee applies only when CAS carries out the renovation: then the total stays within ±5% of it.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

Can I see the contract before signing?

Yes. Leave a request and we will send a sample contract; for your property we prepare a contract with an estimate and schedule.

What is the warranty on renovation?

12 months on completed works from the day they are accepted under the final certificate. For construction and reconstruction, the warranty on the building follows Article 884 of the Civil Code of Ukraine and the contract.

How can I pay?

On the schedule you choose: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more. By invoice (for companies registered in Ukraine — with or without VAT, individually) or another method agreed in the contract.

How can I pay from abroad?

By invoice in USD or EUR (SWIFT transfer), by card via an invoice link, or in USDT. The contract is in English; terms are agreed individually.

Can the total grow during the renovation?

If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the approved estimate.

Who works on site?

The in-house CAS team of salaried staff for different complexity and types of work; custom kitchens and wardrobes are made by our staff and partners.

How do I follow the works from abroad?

Photo reports every 1–2 weeks and, for an extra fee, an online camera on site 24/7.

How long does a renovation take?

The timeline is fixed in the contract and starts from 2 weeks, depending on the scope.
