How we work

  1. Site visitWe inspect the house and record the condition of structures and engineering.
  2. DesignWe create a design project or work to yours and prepare drawings.
  3. Estimate and contractIf we carry out the renovation to our design, amount and stages are fixed: the total stays within 5% of the estimate.
  4. EngineeringNetworks are laid before finishing — so nothing has to be chiselled out later.
  5. Finishing and furnitureFinishing works, with furnishing direct from China in parallel. Photo reports every 1–2 weeks via Telegram.
  6. HandoverCleaning, certificate of completion, warranty obligations under the contract.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey house renovation cost?

From $550/m². The exact amount depends on the condition of the house, the engineering and the finish level — we fix it in the estimate after inspection.

Do you work in the Kyiv region?

Yes: Kyiv and the region — Irpin, Bucha, Brovary, Boryspil, Obukhiv and other towns. We also work across Ukraine, in Europe and worldwide.

Can the estimate grow during the renovation?

If we carry out the renovation to our design, we guarantee the total will not deviate from the approved estimate by more than 5%. Extra works only with your written approval.

Can I follow the renovation without coming to the site?

Yes, we send photo reports every 1–2 weeks via Telegram.
