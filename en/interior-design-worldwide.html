Do you come to the site abroad?

Yes. The studio’s founder and team travel to the site in any country when the project interests us: to see the space, take measurements and meet local contractors and suppliers. Travel terms and costs are agreed individually.

We take the measurements ourselves during the visit, or a local surveyor does it using our checklist — and we check that nothing is missing.

Who builds the project?

In Ukraine and Europe renovation and construction are carried out by our own team — rates and terms are on the Renovation and construction in Europe page.

In other countries we organise the build on site: we find and vet local contractors and suppliers and manage them to our design — designer supervision and quality control, terms agreed individually. Local codes and permits are handled by the local engineers and contractors we bring in; we revise the drawings to their comments.

How much do design and delivery cost?

A design project costs from $30/m², with a minimum project of $2,500; packages: space planning from $6/m², full design with designer supervision from $45/m².

Properties in Europe are priced at European rates: design from $45/m² (minimum $4,000), renovation from $850/m², house shell from $900/m², turnkey from $1,700/m². In other countries the build is priced by estimate, because local labour and material costs depend on the country.

Can we work fully online?

Yes, it is one of the formats: you send plans, measurements, photos or a video walkthrough, we present the design on video calls and hand over a full set of drawings and specifications for your local contractor. The price is the same — from $30/m², minimum $2,500. More in the article Online interior design for a home abroad.

How do payment and the contract work?

For clients abroad: an invoice in dollars or euros (SWIFT), card payment via an invoice link, or USDT. The contract is in English and fixes the price, stages and timeline; terms are agreed individually. Details — Contract, warranty, payment.

Questions and answers

Do you come to the site in another country?

Yes, for the projects we take on. Travel terms and costs are agreed individually.

Who will build my project?

In Ukraine and Europe — our own team. In other countries — local contractors that we find, vet and manage to the design.

Can I order the design fully online?

Yes: from $30/m², minimum $2,500. We need plans or measurements, plus photos and videos of the rooms.

What is the minimum project?

$2,500 for a design project; $4,000 for properties in Europe.

Which currency do you invoice in?

Dollars or euros (SWIFT), card payment via an invoice link, or USDT; the contract is in English.

How long does it take?

It depends on the area and scope; the timeline is fixed in the contract.

Do you take on restaurants, hotels and resorts?

Yes: restaurant design, hotel design and fit-out, resort design.

How do you handle time zones?

We schedule calls at a time that suits both sides and use a messenger and email in between. The office is open Mon–Fri 10:00–20:00 Kyiv time; online 24/7.
