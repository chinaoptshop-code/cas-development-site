What renovations in residential complexes have in common

The rates are the same for all complexes: turnkey renovation from $550/m² (works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are extra, per the estimate), a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500), furnishing from $250/m². If CAS does the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of the estimate, and the works carry a 12-month warranty.

The difference is in the apartment format: two-level apartments need a staircase and separate engineering for each level, terraces need a waterproofing check, large areas need well-planned ventilation and air conditioning, compact ones need precise built-in furniture.

Questions and answers

My residential complex is not on the list — do you work there?

Yes, we renovate apartments in any residential complex in Kyiv and the Kyiv region. The list only shows the complexes for which we have prepared separate pages with advice.

How much does an apartment renovation in a residential complex cost?

Turnkey renovation from $550/m² (works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are extra, per the estimate), a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500), furnishing from $250/m². We fix the exact amount in the estimate after the inspection.

Who gets the renovation approved by the management company?

We do. As a rule, a list of works, a schedule and the crew's details are required; we also arrange the service lift and waste removal.

Can the design project start before I get the keys?

Yes, based on the developer's floor plan. Once we have access, we take control measurements and arrive on site with ready drawings.
