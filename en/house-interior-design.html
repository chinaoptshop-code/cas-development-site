A house interior starts with the layout

A house involves more decisions than an apartment: the staircase and upper floor, windows facing several directions, access to the terrace, guest rooms, the boiler room and service spaces. It works best when the designer joins before construction — then sockets, lighting, ventilation and built-in furniture are planned from the start, with no rework.

Our projects include Hodos Villa (500 m², Kyiv), Petrivtsi House (320 m², architecture and interior in one project), Pidgirland (260 m², Kyiv region), Hoda Estate (180 m², the Carpathians) and House 150 with a pool and terrace. You can walk through houses in 360° 3D tours: Triangle House, the estate in Yablunytsia, private house, concept 1 and concept 4.

A turnkey house in one pair of hands

If the house does not exist yet, we start with design: architecture, structure, engineering and interior in one project. Construction costs from $600/m², a turnkey house with finishes from $1,100/m². For an existing house — turnkey renovation from $550/m² (works and rough materials).

After the renovation — furnishing from $250/m² and landscape design from $50 per 100 m². One manager, one schedule, photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

How much house interior design costs

An interior design project costs from $30/m², minimum $2,500. The rate depends on the area, the number of rooms and the scope of documentation. For a 250 m² house the base calculation is 250 m² × $30 for design and 250 m² × $550 for renovation. We quote the exact price after the brief; a phone consultation is free.

Questions and answers

How much does house interior design cost?

From $30/m², minimum project fee $2,500. We quote the exact price after the brief — it depends on the area and project scope.

When is it best to order the design — before or after construction?

Ideally before the engineering works start. Then lighting, sockets, ventilation and built-in furniture are planned at once, with no rework.

Do you work from our architect's design?

Yes. We take the architect's drawings as a basis and develop the interior; if needed, we agree changes with the author.

Can we order the whole house turnkey?

Yes: design, construction from $600/m², turnkey with finishes from $1,100/m², furnishing and landscaping — by one team.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.

What warranty do you give?

12 months on works. If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.
