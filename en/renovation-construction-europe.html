Who this service is for

For Ukrainians and others who have bought an apartment or house in Europe and want the interior and renovation quality they know from Kyiv projects: a well-planned layout, working drawings, designer supervision and furnishing.

CAS projects have been and are in Spain, France, Germany and Poland, as well as outside Europe — the Kitana Beach complex in Bodrum (Turkey), properties in Bali, a house in the USA and a villa in Dubai (design project).

How we work in Europe

We run the design project online and travel for measurements and key stages. Renovation and construction are carried out by our salaried team; local regulations, permits and contractor requirements differ by country, so the format of works, logistics and team accommodation are agreed individually and fixed in the contract.

Furniture, lighting and decor can be supplied from China — delivery and customs duties are calculated individually depending on the volume. Invoicing for EU companies is being set up.

“From” prices

Rates in Europe are about 50% higher than in Kyiv: design project from $45/m² (minimum $4,000), turnkey renovation from $850/m² (works and rough materials), shell construction from $900/m², turnkey with finishes from $1,700/m². Logistics, site visits and local materials are priced separately — the exact amount will be in the estimate.

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Questions and answers

Which countries do you work in?

Projects have been and are in Spain, France, Germany and Poland, as well as Turkey, Bali, the USA and the UAE. Other countries are discussed individually.

Does your own team work in Europe?

Yes, renovation and construction are carried out by our salaried team. Travel and accommodation terms are individual, in the contract.

Can a European company get an invoice?

Invoicing for EU companies is being set up; ask the manager about current payment options.

Can I order only a design project for an apartment in Europe?

Yes, from $45/m², minimum $4,000: online meetings, 3D, working drawings and specifications.
