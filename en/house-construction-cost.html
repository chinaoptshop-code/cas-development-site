Turnkey house price in Kyiv and the region

shell from $600/m² · turnkey from $1,100/m²

From $1,100/m² with finishes and from $600/m² for the shell — that is what a turnkey house costs with CAS Development. This page explains what the price of a cottage is made of, what each rate includes, what is priced separately and how to get an exact estimate for your plot and design.

How much a turnkey cottage costs: a 150 m² example

For a 150 m² house the lower bound at the studio's base rates is: shell from $90,000 (150 × $600), turnkey house with finishes from $165,000 (150 × $1,100). This is a “from” benchmark: the exact total depends on the design, the plot's geology, materials and finish level.

The rate per m² is only a lower bound, because two houses of the same area can differ several times over: flat or pitched roof, panoramic glazing or standard windows, a pool, terraces, a garage. That is why we prepare the estimate from a design — ours or yours.

What the shell from $600/m² includes

The shell is the load-bearing part of the house: site preparation, foundation, load-bearing walls and partitions, floor slabs, rafters and roof, plus embedded routes for utilities. Most builders in Kyiv and the region price it this way, and so do we.

Windows, entrance doors, insulation of walls, roof and plinth, wind and vapour barriers, facade and gutters are usually a separate stage — the “thermal envelope”: after it the house is weather-tight and ready for MEP. We list it in the estimate as separate lines.

What turnkey from $1,100/m² includes

Turnkey means a house you can move into: shell and thermal envelope, internal MEP (electrics with a distribution board, heating with a boiler or heat pump, radiators or underfloor heating, water and drainage, ventilation), rough and fine finishing of walls, ceilings and floors, interior doors, installation of sanitaryware, sockets, switches and lighting.

Priced separately is what depends on the plot and your choices: connection to external utilities, a well and septic tank, furniture and appliances, landscaping. The finish class affects the total most — we agree it before the start.

Design and technology

The house design is priced separately from construction: from $12/m² for the concept (layouts, facades, 3D) and working drawings for architecture and structure; MEP sections and a site plan are added on request. The rate per m² is higher for small houses and lower for large ones. If you already have a design, we will review it and prepare an estimate.

We build timber-frame and modular houses, timber houses, aerated concrete and brick houses. The budget can be optimised at the design stage: a compact form, a simple roof, a layout without extra corridors and standard window sizes reduce the cost significantly. Price, timeline and payment stages are fixed in the contract; photo reports every 1–2 weeks; 12-month warranty on works.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey house cost in Kyiv?

From $1,100/m² with finishes; the shell from $600/m². For a 150 m² house that is from $165,000 turnkey or from $90,000 for the shell. The exact total comes from the project estimate.

What does the shell include?

Foundation, load-bearing walls and partitions, floor slabs, rafters and roof, routes for utilities. Windows, doors, insulation and facade are the thermal envelope, priced as a separate stage.

How much does an architectural design cost?

From $12/m²: concept and working drawings for architecture and structure. MEP sections and site plan on request; for small houses the rate per m² is higher.

What houses do you build?

Timber-frame, modular, timber, aerated concrete and brick — we choose the technology for the plot, budget and lifestyle.

What is included in the turnkey price?

Shell and thermal envelope, internal MEP, rough and fine finishing, interior doors, installation of sanitaryware and lighting. Furniture and appliances, utility connections, well, septic tank and landscaping are separate; the exact scope is in the estimate.

Can you build to my design?

Yes. We review the design, refine details if needed and prepare an estimate.

How long does construction take?

It depends on area, design and season and is fixed in the contract together with the payment stages.
