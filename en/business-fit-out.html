Design → renovation → furnishing → launch

Opening a venue is more than renovation. Guest and staff flows must be planned, engineering laid for equipment, furniture and lighting chosen to withstand daily use — and all of it ready by the opening date. We take the whole path on ourselves, with one manager, one schedule and photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

Procurement directly from factories in China

Furniture, lighting, sanitary ware, textiles and tableware for commercial projects come directly from factories in China. Delivery takes 35–70 days, so we order right after the specification is approved — in parallel with the renovation.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

What does “turnkey business” mean?

We take the premises from brief to opening: design, renovation, furnishing, delivery, installation and styling. You get a space ready to work and one person responsible for everything.

How much does it cost?

Design from $30/m² (minimum $2,500), office renovation from $350/m², furniture directly from factories in China. Cafés, restaurants and salons are priced per project.

Can I order just one stage?

Yes — design only, renovation to your project, or furnishing only.

Do you issue invoices to companies?

Yes, we invoice companies registered in Ukraine and provide closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies — being set up.
