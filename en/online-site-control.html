Why online control

Renovation and construction take weeks and months, and clients often live in another city or country. Online control removes the need to travel to the site and keep asking: stages, photos, documents and payments are visible in the client portal.

Your manager updates the portal: stage progress, photo reports every 1–2 weeks, acts and invoices. Work timelines are fixed in the contract, from 2 weeks depending on scope.

A 24/7 camera is installed on site at extra cost: once it is connected, the stream opens right from the portal. Only you see the link.

How we work

  1. ContractWe fix stages, timelines and the payment scheme, and the schedule in the portal is built from them.
  2. Portal loginSign in with Telegram, Google or your phone number, no passwords.
  3. Stages and paymentsThe manager adds the contract stages, the payment schedule and documents.
  4. Photo reportsEvery 1–2 weeks: site photos with a comment, linked to a stage.
  5. 24/7 cameraOn request and at extra cost: a camera on site streaming to the portal.
  6. HandoverStage acts in the portal; a 12-month warranty on the works.

Questions and answers

How much does online control cost?

Stages, photo reports every 1–2 weeks, documents and the payment schedule in the portal are part of CAS's work on your project. The 24/7 camera is an extra cost, price on request: it depends on the site and the equipment.

How often do photo reports appear?

Every 1–2 weeks. Each report has site photos, a date and a comment, and is linked to a work stage.

Who else can see my project?

Only you and CAS managers. The portal opens after signing in with Telegram, Google or your phone number; only you see the camera link.

How does the 24/7 camera work?

We install a camera on site and set up the stream. If it is on YouTube with link-only access, you watch it right in the portal; if it is in the camera's cloud, the portal opens its website or app in a new tab.

What if I have a different payment scheme?

The schedule in the portal is built from your contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or instalments, starting from a 10% advance.

Can I connect online control if the renovation is already underway?

Yes, if CAS is doing the works: the manager adds the stages, documents and payments, and photo reports start from the next week. Technical supervision of another contractor: terms and price on an individual basis.
