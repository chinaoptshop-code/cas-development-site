What you can and should not change

Most replanning means moving non-load-bearing partitions, joining the kitchen with the living room, adding wardrobes and extra bathrooms. Limits usually come from load-bearing walls and columns, ventilation shafts, gas equipment and placing wet areas above the neighbours' living rooms. So before the renovation we check the structures and engineering and show layout options within these limits.

Whether official approval is needed depends on the scope of changes and the building type. We do not promise outcomes on behalf of state authorities: we prepare drawings and support — if needed, terms individual.

How much replanning with renovation costs

The layout package costs from $6/m², a standard design project with 3D and working drawings from $30/m²; the minimum for any package is $2,500. Renovation from $550/m²: works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances separately, per estimate.

Replanning makes most sense together with a major renovation or in a new-build apartment, while the walls are not yet finished.

Questions and answers

Who does turnkey apartment replanning in Kyiv?

CAS Development — a Kyiv studio that since 2016 has done layouts, design and renovation of apartments with one team; the renovation is carried out by our own salaried team. An example of a completed apartment is Tetris Hall.

How much does a replanning design cost?

The layout package from $6/m², a standard design project from $30/m²; the minimum for any package is $2,500.

Can the kitchen be joined with the living room?

Often yes, if the wall is not load-bearing and there is no gas in the kitchen or it can be separated to code. We check structures and engineering at the survey and suggest options.

Do you help with approval?

We prepare drawings and support — if needed, terms individual. The result of approval depends on the authorities and the scope of changes, so we do not guarantee it.

Can a bathroom or kitchen be moved?

It depends on the risers, the drainage slope and what is below the apartment. As a rule, we do not move wet areas above the neighbours' living rooms.

How much does renovation after replanning cost?

From $550/m² — works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances separately. If CAS carries out the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.
