    Apartment renovation price per m²: what it is made of

    The price of a renovation per m² in Kyiv has three parts: works, rough materials and finishing materials. We price the first two at a rate from $550/m², and the final figure is refined by the estimate. The third part — tiles, flooring, doors, sanitaryware — depends entirely on your choice, so we price it separately from the specification.

    This gives an honest picture: how much the renovation itself costs and how much the materials you choose. For a 50 m² apartment the lower bound for works and rough materials is from $27,500; calculate a benchmark for your area in the calculator above.

    Renovation with materials: what it means at CAS

    When people look for “turnkey renovation with materials”, they usually want the contractor to buy and deliver everything. With us, rough materials are already in the rate, and finishing materials are selected together with you, priced in the estimate and purchased by us — you do not have to tour building markets.

    Furniture, lighting and decor can be ordered without intermediaries — directly from factories in China through turnkey procurement; delivery takes 35–70 days, so orders are planned in parallel with the renovation.

    Can a renovation be done inexpensively

    We do not promise the lowest price on the market — CAS is a premium-segment studio, and behind the rate from $550/m² stand an estimate accurate to ±5% (if we renovate), photo reports and a 12-month warranty. But saving without losing quality is realistic, and we show exactly where.

    First, a well-thought-out layout: fewer partitions and boxing mean less work. Second, finishing materials: the estimate gives several price options. Third, furniture direct from factories, without intermediary mark-ups. Fourth, a fixed estimate: the most expensive renovation is the one that “grew” along the way, while ours stays within ±5% if CAS does the work.

    Renovation estimate: how we calculate

    We prepare the estimate after a site survey (it is paid, its cost depends on the property) and from a design project or your brief. It contains scopes of work by room, the list of rough materials, the finishing specification and a stage payment schedule. A phone consultation is free: we will give a benchmark even before the visit.

    Questions and answers

    How much does an apartment renovation cost per m² in Kyiv?

    From $550/m² — works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate. The exact total comes from the estimate after the survey.

    How much does a one-room apartment renovation cost?

    For a 40 m² apartment the lower bound for works and rough materials is from $22,000 (40 × $550). Finishing materials are added to your choice; the exact total comes from the estimate.

    Is the survey free?

    No, the survey is paid, its cost depends on the property. A phone consultation is free: we will give a budget benchmark before the visit.

    Can the price grow during the renovation?

    If CAS carries out the renovation to the estimate, the total stays within ±5% of it. Price, timeline and payment stages are fixed in the contract.

    Do you invoice companies?

    Yes, we invoice companies registered in Ukraine — convenient for offices and commercial premises.
