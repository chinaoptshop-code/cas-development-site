A crew you do not need to babysit

The main problem with renovation crews is missed deadlines and a price that keeps growing. We work under a contract and a detailed estimate, and a site manager checks every stage. You get reports and pay for completed stages.

When needed, we bring in a designer and an engineer: drawings help the crew work precisely and without rework.

A renovation company, not a random crew

Looking for a renovation crew, it is easy to end up with “handymen from an ad” with no contract and no warranty. CAS Development has been a renovation company since 2016: an in-house team of salaried staff for different levels of complexity and types of work, plus a site manager, estimator and material supply. 150+ completed projects and a 12-month warranty on works.

As a renovation company we are responsible for the whole site: from demolition to turnkey renovation and design implementation, and, if needed, turnkey furnishing with furniture and lighting.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

What work does your crew do?

A full turnkey renovation or individual stages: demolition, electrics, plumbing, plastering, tiling, flooring, painting, ceilings, furniture assembly.

How much does a renovation crew cost?

Turnkey renovation from $550/m². The price from $550/m² covers works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are priced separately in the estimate. Individual works are priced after measuring; the price is fixed in the estimate, and if CAS renovates, the total stays within ±5%.

Do you sign a contract?

Always — with an estimate, schedule and payment stages.

Do you buy the materials yourselves?

We can purchase and deliver everything ourselves, including directly from China, or work with your materials.

Are you a crew or a renovation company?

A renovation company with our own team: we work under a contract, with an estimate, a site manager and a 12-month warranty on works.

Do you renovate to someone else’s design project?

Yes. First we check the drawings and prepare an estimate, then carry out the renovation and, if you wish, purchase materials and furniture.
