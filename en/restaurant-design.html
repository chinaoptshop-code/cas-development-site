Questions and answers

How much does a restaurant or café design project cost?

From $30/m², minimum project budget $2,500. We give the exact price after the brief and a site visit.

What does a venue design project include?

Concept, layout of dining room, bar and kitchen, 3D visualisations, working drawings, materials and furniture specification, renovation estimate.

Can you renovate and furnish the venue?

Yes, with our own team. Furniture, lighting and decor can be brought directly from China. If we do the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.

Where do you work?

A restaurant from scratch — 14 stages from shell to first guests

Scroll and an empty unit turns into a restaurant. Choose the size and style, switch on the X-ray to see ventilation, drainage with grease traps and the kitchen wiring.

  1. Survey and 3D scanOur specialist scans the space with a laser scanner, so the design rests on real dimensions, not old drawings.
  2. ShellColumns, walls course by course and the slab; openings for shopfronts and the entrance as designed.
  3. Facade and shopfrontsGlazing section by section, the entrance and a place for the sign.
  4. VentilationKitchen extract through a shaft to the roof and a supply network for the dining room: kitchen smells stay away from guests.
  5. Drainage and grease trapsFloor gullies in the kitchen, pipes laid to falls and grease traps after the sinks, before the floor is poured.
  6. Kitchen wiringA dedicated board, circuits for ranges, ovens and fridges, cable trays under the ceiling, sized for the equipment load.
  7. FloorScreed, waterproofing and slip-resistant tiles in the kitchen; a timber floor in the dining room.
  8. Dining room walls and ceilingFinishes in the chosen style, a slatted ceiling and access hatches to ventilation and wiring.
  9. BarThe bar counter, bottle shelves, a sink and fridges under the counter, bar stools.
  10. Open kitchenRanges, ovens, an island, a heated pass and the extract hood: guests see the cooking.
  11. Dining furnitureTables and chairs to the seating plan, with aisles for staff and guests.
  12. TerraceDecking board by board, planters, umbrellas and tables: a summer room in front of the windows.
  13. Lighting: day → eveningLighting scenes: a daytime room, evening pendants over the tables, lit bar, shopfronts and sign.
  14. GuestsThe venue opens: guests at tables and at the bar, waiters between the pass and the room.

Design from $30/m², minimum $2,500. Fit-out, kitchen equipment and furniture are priced per estimate.
