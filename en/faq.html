Prices and payment

How much does an apartment design project cost?

From $30/m², with a minimum project budget of $2,500.

How much does turnkey renovation cost?

From $550/m² for apartments and houses, from $350/m² for offices. The exact amount is fixed in the estimate before the start.

What is included in the price from $550/m²?

Works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are priced separately in the estimate, depending on the items chosen.

How much does a site measurement cost?

Measurement and inspection of the property are arranged individually, and the cost is calculated case by case.

And construction or furnishing?

Construction from $600/m², a turnkey house with finishing from $1,100/m², turnkey furnishing from $250/m².

Can you invoice a company?

Yes, we invoice companies registered in Ukraine — with or without VAT, individually — and provide closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies — being set up.

How can I pay for a renovation or construction?

We tailor the payment schedule to you and fix it in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more. Payment by invoice (for companies registered in Ukraine — with or without VAT, individually), card or cash. More on the contract, warranty and payment page.

How can I pay from abroad?

By invoice in USD or EUR (SWIFT transfer), by card via an invoice link, or in USDT. The contract is in English; terms are agreed individually.

Warranty and reliability

What is the warranty on works?

12 months.

Can the renovation cost grow during the process?

If CAS Development carries out the renovation to our project, the total does not deviate from the approved estimate by more than ±5%.

How many projects have you completed?

150+ projects since 2016.

Who does the work — your own crew or subcontractors?

Our own: an in-house team of salaried staff for different levels of complexity and types of work, with a site manager checking every stage.

Can I follow the works online?

Yes. We send photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and for an extra fee we install an online camera on site 24/7.
