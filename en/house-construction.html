A house from design to keys

A house is the most expensive purchase of a lifetime, so we start with the design and the estimate: you know in advance how much the house will cost and how it will look inside and out. During construction you receive photo reports from the site.

We build one- and two-storey houses, cottages, villas, A-frame houses and resorts. After the shell we take on interior finishing, furniture from China and landscaping — no need to look for new contractors.

What the shell from $600/m² includes

The shell is the load-bearing part of the house: site preparation, foundation, load-bearing walls and partitions, floor slabs, roof structure and roofing, plus sleeves for utilities. This is how most builders around Kyiv define it, and this is what our rate from $600/m² covers.

Windows, the entrance door, insulation of walls, roof and plinth, wind and vapour barriers, facade and gutters are usually a separate stage — the “weather-tight envelope”. Whether you need it straight away and what exactly it includes is listed line by line in the estimate.

What turnkey from $1,100/m² includes

Turnkey means a house you can move into: shell and envelope, internal utilities (electrics with a distribution board, heating with a boiler or heat pump, radiators or underfloor heating, water supply and drainage, ventilation), rough and finishing works on walls, ceilings and floors, interior doors, sanitary ware, sockets, switches and lighting installed.

Priced separately, depending on the plot and your choices: connection to external grids (power, gas, water, sewage), a well and septic tank, furniture and appliances, landscaping. The class of finishing materials affects the total most — we agree it before work starts.

Architectural design of a house — from $12/m²

The house design is priced separately from construction. As with most architecture firms, the base rate is from $12 per m²: concept design (layouts, facades, 3D) and working drawings with architectural and structural sections. Engineering sections — heating and ventilation, water and drainage, electrics — and the site plan are added as required; smaller houses have a higher rate per m².

Technologies: timber frame, modular, timber beam, aerated concrete and brick

We build timber-frame and modular houses, houses from glued or profiled timber beams, as well as aerated concrete and brick houses. The technology is chosen for the plot, the budget and how you will live in the house; timelines and costs are calculated from the design, not from an average rate. Our own in-house crews build, and works carry a 12-month warranty.

A house from scratch: the method in 19 stages

Scroll and the house comes together the way it does on site, from survey to keys. Switch the wall method and tap the layers to see each build-up. Photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

  1. Survey and setting outOur surveyor Mykola maps the terrain, sets out the house axes on the ground and builds batter boards that every later measurement starts from.
  2. ExcavationWe strip the topsoil and dig the pit for the slab to the design depth; the spoil is stockpiled, part of it goes back as backfill.
  3. Sand and gravel bedGeotextile on the bottom, then sand and crushed stone in compacted layers. The geotextile keeps the bed from mixing with the soil.
  4. Waterproofing and XPS under the slabA concrete blinding layer, torch-on waterproofing with overlaps and XPS boards: the slab stays dry and keeps heat out of the ground.
  5. Slab reinforcementTwo rebar meshes on chairs (concrete cover), edge cages and starter bars for cast-in-place parts. The site manager signs off the rebar before the pour.
  6. Pouring the slabA concrete pump places the mix, a poker vibrator compacts it; then curing: we keep it damp and covered while it gains strength.
  7. Ground-floor blockworkAerated block on thin-bed adhesive (2–3 mm joints), the first course on mortar over the damp-proof layer. Every 4th course is reinforced, lintels over openings.
  8. Ring beamFormwork, a rebar cage and concrete around the perimeter: the ring beam spreads the floor load and ties the walls together.
  9. Floor slabHollow-core planks by crane or a cast-in-place slab with two meshes — per the structural design. Mykhailo films the progress by drone.
  10. First floor, ring beam, wall plateFirst-floor walls the same way, topped with a second ring beam with cast-in studs that hold the timber wall plate.
  11. Rafters, membrane, counter-battensRafters on the wall plate, then a vapour-open roofing membrane, counter-battens for ventilation and battens for the roofing.
  12. Roof insulation and coveringMetal tiles or standing seam on battens, gutters; mineral wool between rafters, a vapour barrier below. Mykhailo films the roof by drone.
  13. Windows: sealed installationEnergy-efficient windows on anchors with a three-layer joint: a breathable tape outside, foam in the gap, a vapour-tight tape inside.
  14. Building servicesWiring from a panel with breakers and RCDs, water and sloped drainage, boiler and underfloor heating, balanced ventilation — per the MEP design.
  15. Screed and plasterScreed over the heating pipes with an edge strip, walls plastered to guide beads — a true base for the finishes.
  16. Ventilated facade: insulation and claddingMineral wool on anchors, a wind membrane, counter battens for a vented gap and cladding: boards, slats or fibre cement to suit the style.
  17. Interior, furniture, lightingFinishes from the design project, a kitchen and wardrobes by our joiners Serhii, Andrii and Oleh, light fittings — and the lights come on.
  18. LandscapingA perimeter path with drainage, walkways, lawn, trees and outdoor lighting — per the landscape design.
  19. Handover and 12-month warrantyWe inspect the house with you, sign the acts, hand over the as-built documents and the keys. Warranty: 12 months.

Questions and answers

How much does it cost to build a house near Kyiv?

Construction from $600 per m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100 per m². The exact cost depends on the design, materials and plot.

What does the shell include?

Foundation, load-bearing walls and partitions, floor slabs, roof structure and roofing, sleeves for utilities. Windows, doors, insulation and facade form the weather-tight envelope, priced as a separate stage.

How much does the architectural design of a house cost?

From $12 per m²: concept design and architectural and structural working drawings. Engineering sections and the site plan are added as required.

Do you design the house too?

Yes, architecture and interior in one project. Design from $30 per m², minimum project $2,500.

Do you build to someone else’s design?

Yes, we review the project and prepare an estimate.

Do you reconstruct old houses?

Yes: reconstruction, extensions, replacement of engineering networks and full finishing.
