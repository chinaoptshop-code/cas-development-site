A hotel is a business model in drawings

Every decision in a hotel affects revenue and running costs: how many rooms fit on a floor, how quickly housekeeping can turn a room, how durable the finishes are, how guest and staff flows are separated. So we start with the format and room mix, and only then with style.

We design standard rooms as a system: the same furniture, lighting, sanitaryware and textiles across the floor. This simplifies procurement, maintenance and replacement — and every guest gets the same quality.

Hospitality projects

Yablu Rest — a space for leisure, reception and conferences in a warm loft style, with a double-height hall, timber beams and a stove. Kitana Beach — concept and architecture of a beach complex in Bodrum, Turkey, with overwater cabins, pools and reception. Yacht Restaurant and Monpark — restaurants that can become the heart of a hotel.

FF&E sourced from China

We source furniture, lighting, textiles and tableware directly from factories in China. For hotels this works especially well: rooms are standardised, and factories produce in batches. Custom colours, sizes, materials and finishes are possible, and volume discounts apply to larger orders.

Delivery takes 35–70 days, so we order right after the specification is approved, in parallel with construction. Design costs from $30/m² (minimum $2,500); renovation is priced per project. We invoice companies registered in Ukraine; EU invoicing is being set up.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Questions and answers

How much does hotel design cost?

Design from $30/m², minimum project fee $2,500. Renovation is priced per project after a site survey and brief.

Do you work on mini-hotels and aparthotels?

Yes: mini-hotels, boutique hotels, aparthotels, rental apartments, resorts and country complexes.

Can you furnish rooms with FF&E from China?

Yes, directly from factories in China: custom manufacturing available. Delivery takes 35–70 days.

Do you deliver up to opening day?

Yes. Design, renovation, FF&E, installation and styling by one team, one manager and photo reports every 1–2 weeks.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland. For projects that interest us we come to the site, and in other countries we select and manage local contractors — see projects worldwide.
