How we work

  1. Site visit and measurementsWe inspect the premises and clarify the brief: headcount, zones, deadlines.
  2. Planning and designWe lay out workstations, meeting rooms and breakout areas and agree on the style.
  3. Estimate and contractAmount and stages are fixed. If we carry out the renovation to our design, the total stays within 5% of the estimate.
  4. RenovationPhoto reports every 1–2 weeks via Telegram. Noisy works at agreed times so as not to disturb neighbours in the business centre.
  5. Furniture and furnishingOrdered directly from China in parallel with the works, so furniture arrives before completion.
  6. HandoverCleaning, certificate of completion, warranty obligations under the contract.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey office renovation in Kyiv cost?

From $350/m² — works and basic materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per the estimate. The exact amount depends on the condition of the premises, engineering and finish level — we fix it in the estimate after measuring.

Can the estimate grow during the renovation?

If we carry out the renovation to our design, we guarantee the total will not deviate from the approved estimate by more than 5%. Any extra works only with your written approval.

Can the office be renovated without stopping work?

Yes. We split the site into phases and agree the hours for noisy works with you and the business-centre management; the works schedule is fixed in the contract.

Do you do only renovation, or furniture too?

The full cycle: design project, renovation, engineering, furniture, lighting and decor. Office furniture comes directly from factories in China, made to order: delivery in 35–70 days.

Can you invoice our company?

Yes, we invoice companies registered in Ukraine and provide closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies — being set up.

Where do you work?

Kyiv and all of Ukraine, Europe and worldwide — projects in Bali, Turkey, Spain, the USA, France, Germany and Poland.
