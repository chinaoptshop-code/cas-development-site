Turnkey implementation of your design project

renovation from $550/m² · furnishing from $250/m²

You already have a design project — by us or by another designer. We implement it in full: check the drawings, calculate the estimate, renovate, and purchase materials, furniture and lighting. You get the interior from the visualisations, not something “roughly similar”.

What's included

Project audit

  • review of drawings and specifications
  • details that are missing
  • measurement and check against the site
  • estimate before work starts

Renovation to the project

  • own team under a site manager
  • engineering: electrics, plumbing, ventilation
  • finishing exactly to the drawings
  • photo reports every 1–2 weeks

Purchasing and furnishing

  • materials to the specification or equivalents
  • made-to-measure furniture and furniture from China
  • lighting, sanitary ware, textiles, decor
  • delivery, installation and final styling

One contractor instead of a dozen

A design project most often “breaks” during renovation: the crew simplifies details, the supplier swaps items and the furniture does not fit the niches. We take on implementation turnkey — from rough works to arranged furniture and hung curtains — and are responsible for the result as one team.

We work with other designers’ projects: if you wish, we agree solutions with the author, and if designer supervision is not ordered, our architect makes sure every detail matches the drawings. Any changes are agreed with you in writing before they are made.

Guarantees

12-month warranty on works

We hand over the property with a signed acceptance certificate; all completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

Estimate within ±5% when CAS does the renovation

We prepare the estimate before work starts. If our own team carries out the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

In-house team

The work is done by our own salaried staff, not subcontractors; we assign people according to the complexity and type of work.

Contract and payment that suits you

Price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or split by stages, starting with a deposit of 10% or more.

Photo reports and a 24/7 camera

Photos from the site every 1–2 weeks via Telegram or WhatsApp; for an extra fee — an online camera on site 24/7.

Questions and answers

Do you implement projects by other designers?

Yes. First we audit the drawings and specifications, add the missing details and calculate the estimate.

How much does implementation cost?

Renovation from $550/m², offices from $350/m², furnishing from $250/m². If CAS does the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.

Can expensive items be replaced?

Yes, we offer equivalent alternatives — including furniture, lighting and materials directly from factories in China. Every replacement is agreed in writing.

What is the warranty?

12 months on completed works.
