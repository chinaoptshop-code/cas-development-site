Why aerated concrete

Autoclaved aerated concrete is one of the most common materials for private houses around Kyiv: large-format blocks are laid on a thin adhesive layer, walls go up faster than with brick, and the material holds heat well and is easy to work. At the same time it is brittle in bending and absorbs moisture, so reinforced courses, a monolithic ring beam under the slabs and the right facade matter.

We define the block grade and thickness, insulation and facade type in the house design: both thermal performance and the estimate depend on them.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey aerated concrete house cost?

From $1,100/m² with MEP and finishes; the shell from $600/m². The exact total comes from the project estimate.

Aerated concrete, brick or timber frame — which is better?

Aerated concrete is warm and quick to lay but needs reinforcement and a protective facade; brick is stronger and heavier; timber frame is the fastest option. We compare technologies for your plot and budget in the estimate.

Do aerated concrete walls need insulation?

It depends on block thickness and grade and how you will use the house. We decide in the thermal calculation of the design; roof and floor are always insulated.
