Apartment renovation without the hassle

Renovations most often drag on and get more expensive because of scattered contractors: the designer draws, the crew redoes, the supplier delivers the wrong thing. At CAS Development everything is in one pair of hands — design, estimate, works, materials and furniture. You deal with one manager and get photo reports from site every 1–2 weeks.

We fix the price before the start: after the survey and design we prepare a detailed estimate, and if CAS does the renovation, the final total stays within ±5% of it. Furniture, lighting and finishing materials can be brought directly from China — noticeably cheaper than buying retail.

Which apartments we renovate

New-build apartments from the developer, resale housing with demolition and replanning, penthouses and serviced apartments. We work in all districts of Kyiv and in the Kyiv region, as well as across Ukraine, in Europe and worldwide.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey apartment renovation cost in Kyiv?

From $550/m² — works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate. The exact total depends on the apartment's condition, scope and materials; we fix it in the estimate, and if CAS renovates, the final price stays within ±5% of it.

What is included in the price from $550/m²?

Works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate: their cost depends on the chosen items.

How long does an apartment renovation take?

It depends on area and complexity. The timeline is in the contract, from 2 weeks, with a stage schedule after the survey and estimate.

Do I need a design project?

Preferably: with a design and drawings the crew works without rework, and you see the result in 3D in advance. Design from $30/m².

Do you do cosmetic renovation?

Yes. We take on both major turnkey renovation and individual works — we assess after the survey.

Where do you work?

All districts of Kyiv and the Kyiv region, as well as across Ukraine, in Europe and worldwide.
