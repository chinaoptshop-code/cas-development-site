Kitchens and wardrobes together with the interior

Custom cabinet furniture is best designed together with the renovation: then sockets, water outlets, ventilation and lighting are exactly where the kitchen and wardrobes need them, and the fronts match the doors and floors. If you do a kitchen renovation or an apartment design project with us, the furniture goes into the same project.

You can also order a kitchen or wardrobes separately, without renovation: we survey, design the furniture with dimensions and materials and, after approval, make and install it.

Price of a custom kitchen

We calculate the price of custom kitchens and wardrobes per project estimate: it depends on dimensions, carcass and front materials, worktop, hardware, lighting and built-in appliances. We do not quote a fixed rate per running metre — two projects of the same length can differ several times over.

Furniture, lighting and decor for the whole apartment can be selected and brought directly from China — see turnkey furnishing.

Questions and answers

How much does a custom kitchen cost in Kyiv?

Per project estimate: the price depends on dimensions, front and carcass materials, worktop, hardware and appliances.

Who makes the furniture?

Part of the work is done by CAS Development's own craftsmen, part by trusted partner workshops. Design, control and installation are on us.

Do you make custom wardrobes and walk-in closets?

Yes: built-in, sliding and hinged wardrobes, walk-in closet systems, floor-to-ceiling panels and shelving.

Can I order a kitchen without a renovation?

Yes. We survey, design the furniture and prepare an estimate; after approval we make and install it.

Is the survey free?

No, the survey is paid, its cost is individual. A phone consultation is free.
