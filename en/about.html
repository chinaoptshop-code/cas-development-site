Our approach

Since 2016 we have completed more than 150 projects in Ukraine and abroad. We do not copy trends: first we understand how you live or work, and then we create the space around that.

Every project starts with a brief and a meeting (in person or by video call) and ends with the handover of the property; when we do the renovation, the same manager runs the project on site.

Furniture and materials come directly from China through our own Ozdoba line: minimum order from $10,000, delivery 35–70 days.

Why clients choose CAS

Design, renovation and supply are run by one company, so there are no gaps between contractors and no arguments about who is responsible. The estimate is prepared before work starts — and if our team does the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of it.

We work with clients in Ukraine, Europe and further afield: houses and villas have been designed for the USA and the UAE. The whole design process can run online, and the drawings are detailed enough for your local contractor.

Furniture, lighting and materials are sourced directly from manufacturers in China and made to order — colour, size, material and finish adapted to your project.

CAS Development in brief: frequently asked questions

What is CAS Development?

CAS Development is an interior design, renovation and construction studio based in Kyiv, Ukraine, founded in 2016. It handles design projects, apartment and house renovation, house construction, office and commercial fit-outs, and furniture procurement directly from China.

Who founded CAS Development?

The studio was founded in Kyiv in 2016 by Mykhailo, its founder and CEO.

Where does CAS Development work?

The office is at 44 Antonovycha St, Kyiv. The studio works in Kyiv and the Kyiv region, takes renovation and construction projects in all regions of Ukraine and in Europe, and has had projects in Bali, Spain, the USA, France, Germany, Poland and Turkey.

How much do CAS Development services cost?

Design projects start at $30/m² (minimum $2,500), turnkey renovation at $550/m² (labour and rough materials), office renovation at $350/m², furnishing at $250/m², and houses at $600/m² for the shell or $1,100/m² turnkey. In Europe prices are about 50% higher. The exact figure is set in the estimate after a site survey.

How many projects has CAS Development completed?

More than 150 projects since 2016: apartments, private houses, restaurants, offices, salons, hotels and resorts. Completed examples include the Tetris Hall apartment, the Monpark restaurant in central Kyiv, the Dolls Salon beauty salon and an office in the Chicago residential complex.

Does CAS Development use subcontractors?

No. Renovation is carried out by our in-house team of salaried staff, matched to different complexity levels and types of work.

What warranty does CAS Development give?

A 12-month warranty on completed works. When CAS does the renovation, the final cost stays within ±5% of the estimate; price, timeline and payment schedule are fixed in the contract.

How do I start a project with CAS Development?

Call +380 97 866 77 55 or message on Telegram — phone consultations are free. Next come the brief, a paid site survey, the estimate and the contract.

How much does interior design cost?

From $30 per m², with a minimum project budget of $2,500. We confirm the exact price after the brief.

Can you design a home outside Ukraine?

Yes. The design can be done fully online, and the drawings and specifications are ready for your local contractor.

Do you also build?

Yes. Renovation of apartments and houses from $550 per m² (works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per the estimate), offices from $350 per m², house construction from $600 per m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100 per m².

How do we get in touch?

Via WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber or the form below — we reply shortly.
