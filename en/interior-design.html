A design studio that thinks about implementation

A beautiful picture is only half the job. We design with real prices for materials, works and furniture in mind, so the interior from the 3D fits the budget. The drawings are made for builders: where the sockets go, how the lighting runs, which tile and where the joint is.

We work in different styles — modern minimalism, neoclassical, loft, Scandinavian, Japandi — and our portfolio includes penthouses, private houses, villas, restaurants and offices in Kyiv and abroad.

For clients outside Ukraine the whole process can run online: you send plans, measurements and photos or a video walkthrough, and receive a full set of documents your local contractor can follow. We also take on projects worldwide: for projects that interest us we come to the site, and in other countries we find and manage local contractors.

Try a style on a real interior

Change walls, floors, fabrics, stone and metal in our projects and see the result instantly. Japandi, Modern classic, Dark luxury or Warm minimal: choose yours and get a project in that style.

Questions and answers

How much does interior design cost?

It depends on the package: layout, standard design project or full with designer supervision — prices and scope are in the package comparison above; the exact fee is fixed in the contract.

What does a design project include?

Layouts, 3D visualisations, working drawings (electrics, plumbing, ceilings, floors), material and furniture specifications and an estimate.

How long does it take?

It depends on the area and number of revisions; the timeline is fixed in the contract.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, we design properties in other cities and countries online. We also take on projects worldwide with site visits.

Do you implement the project as well?

Yes: renovation from $550/m², purchasing of materials, furniture and decor. If our team does the renovation, the estimate is kept within ±5%.
