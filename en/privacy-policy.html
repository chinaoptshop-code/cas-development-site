Privacy Policy

Updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

cas-develop.com is operated by CAS Development, a design and architecture studio at 44 Antonovycha St, Kyiv, Ukraine. For any question about your data, message or call +380 97 866 77 55 (Telegram, Viber, WhatsApp).

What we collect

From the request form: your name, phone number, floor area, comment and the page you sent it from. The form is optional — you can simply call us.

Your account and orders: you can sign in with Telegram, Google or your phone number — the number is confirmed in our Telegram bot. We receive your name and, depending on how you sign in, your Telegram @username and profile photo or your Google email and profile photo; your phone number if you entered or confirmed it. Your account stores the data you add yourself: delivery addresses, company details for invoices, saved products, orders and projects. To keep you signed in, the site sets a technical session cookie.

Analytics: Microsoft Clarity collects anonymised usage data — clicks, scrolling, device type, approximate country and session recordings. Clarity masks and does not record what you type into form fields. Clarity uses cookies.

Technical data: our host Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser data to deliver and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from our own site.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no advertising cookies. Necessary cookies are used only in the client account: the sign-in session (cas_s), a temporary cas_pl while you sign in with a phone number, and the account language (cas_lang).

In browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) the site keeps your analytics choice (cas_ck), a note that a scene hint has already been shown, account favourites and settings, and, for the duration of the visit, the domain of the site you came from (cas_rh). If you write in the website chat, your browser keeps the conversation identifier and key (cas_chat) so that you can continue it after reloading the page; the chat sets no cookies.

Analytics is Microsoft Clarity: it records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, navigation) and sets its own cookies; the data is processed by Microsoft. Clarity starts after your first action on the site. You can opt out with the “Decline” button in the cookie notice (shown to visitors outside Ukraine, as told by the device’s time zone) or with the “Cookies” link at the bottom of the page: Clarity is then no longer loaded and its cookies are deleted.

From the request form we keep what you sent, the address of the page it was sent from, and the domain of the site you came from (for example google.com, without the page address). We do not sell this data or pass it to third parties other than the processors listed below.

Website chat. In the chat you are answered by the studio’s AI assistant and, when needed, by a manager. We store the messages, files and voice messages you send, the language and the page where you started, and the domain of the site you came from; they are seen by the studio staff who handle conversations. Your name and phone number are stored only if you leave them yourself: the conversation then becomes an enquiry and is kept with it. We do not store your IP address, only its hash to protect against spam. A conversation without an enquiry is deleted 30 days after the last message, files without an enquiry after 14 days. Files of a conversation with an enquiry are deleted after 180 days. If you send a voice message, the assistant may reply by voice: to do so, the text of the reply is processed by a third-party speech synthesis service (Google). Estimates in the chat are preliminary; the exact price comes after measurements and a detailed quote.

Why

To contact you about your request, estimate the cost and prepare a proposal.

So that you can sign in to your account, place orders and track them.

To see which pages work well and improve the site.

Where it is stored and who receives it

Requests, orders and account data are stored in the Cloudflare D1 database. Requests also go to our automation server and to the CAS Development team chat. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. Processors: Microsoft (Clarity), Cloudflare (hosting and D1 database); Telegram and Google — only if you sign in with them.

How long we keep it

Request, order and account data is kept as long as needed to work with you, or until you ask us to delete it. Clarity data is kept under Microsoft’s retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. Message or call +380 97 866 77 55 and we will reply within 7 days. You can disable cookies in your browser; the site works without them (signing in to your account requires the session cookie).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
