Kropyvnytskyi: an old merchant city and a private sector

Kropyvnytskyi lies in the very centre of Ukraine, with a 19th-century merchant centre, apartment-block estates and a large private sector along the Inhul. There are fewer new complexes than in bigger regional centres, so most requests are about updating existing homes.

For such homes an accurate estimate matters most: what to replace in the services, whether to level the walls, which structures to keep. We start with it, so the budget does not drift halfway through the renovation.

Interior design in Kropyvnytskyi

For apartments in older buildings — replanning within the structure, new bathrooms and kitchens, built-in furniture; for houses — room for a large family, a terrace, a boiler room and a pantry. Packages from planning to full. Design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500); packages are on the interior design studio page.

Turnkey renovation and house construction

We renovate apartments and houses, from major renovation to cosmetic. Near Kropyvnytskyi we build aerated-block, brick and timber-frame houses, from the architectural design to turnkey finishing.

Renovation is carried out by our salaried team: from $550/m² for works and rough materials, with finishing materials, furniture and appliances priced separately per estimate. Scope, schedule and payment plan (50/50, 50/30/20 or in stages, from a 10% advance) are fixed in the contract; team travel is on individual terms. Architectural design from $12/m², house shell from $600/m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100/m² — see turnkey renovation and house construction.

Logistics, control and reports

Design and approvals run online; we travel for measurements and key stages on the schedule set in the contract, and the terms and costs of travel and team accommodation are individual. Between visits you get photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and an online camera on site 24/7 is available for an extra fee.
