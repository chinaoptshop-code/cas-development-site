Renovating in Ukraine remotely: contract, payment from abroad and control

CAS Development · 23 November 2026

The apartment or house is in Ukraine, while you live in another country and visit once every few months. A remote renovation is possible in this situation if every decision is fixed on paper and the progress of the works is visible without travelling. Here is how to organise the contract, payment from abroad and control of the site.

What should you prepare before a remote renovation starts?

A remote renovation needs a detailed design before it starts, because from a distance you cannot drop in and point to where a socket should go. That is why a remote renovation rests on the design: layout, electrics, plumbing, wall elevations and a materials specification. The more detailed the design, the fewer questions arise in the middle of the works. The design project itself can also be done online — see the page on design and renovation across Ukraine. How to follow the works without travelling is shown on the online control of renovation and construction page.

Before the start, also settle three practical matters:

  • who will hand over the keys and give access to the property
  • who makes decisions on your side when you are out of reach, and within what limits
  • which communication channel is the main one and at what hours you reply, given the time difference

What should the contract say if you are not around?

The less often you are on site, the more has to be in the contract and its annexes. The minimum set:

  • a list of works and an estimate with quantities item by item
  • the design project or drawings as an annex
  • a stage schedule with dates
  • a payment schedule tied to stage certificates
  • how often and in what form you receive reports
  • who accepts concealed works and how: electrics, pipes, waterproofing
  • the warranty and the procedure for remedying defects
  • how changes are agreed: in writing, with a new price and deadline

CAS formalises renovation and construction with a contract for works; for foreign clients we prepare the contract in English. It can also be signed remotely, online, without visiting the office. How the document is structured is described on the contract, warranty and payment page.

How do you pay for a renovation from abroad?

From abroad, the renovation is paid in parts under the schedule in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10, or a stage-by-stage split starting from a 10% advance. Payments are tied to stage certificates, so you can always see exactly what you are paying for.

There are three ways to pay from abroad:

  • against an invoice in dollars or euros by SWIFT bank transfer
  • by card via a payment link
  • in USDT

We agree the payment terms individually and write them into the contract. If a company registered in Ukraine is paying, we issue an invoice with or without VAT, on individual terms.

How do you control a renovation online?

A renovation is controlled online through photo reports, stage certificates and the online client account on the website, which every CAS client receives. You sign in with Telegram, Google or a phone number; the account works in ten languages. Inside:

  • work stages with statuses and a completion percentage
  • photo reports every 1–2 weeks with a date and a comment
  • the contract, the estimate and the stage certificates
  • the payment schedule: what is planned, what is due, what has been paid

If you want to see the site at any moment, for an extra fee we install a 24/7 camera; only you have the link to the stream. The demo on the online site control page shows what the account looks like.

A useful habit is to check every photo report against the schedule: is the stage under way the one in the contract, and does the progress match the payment that is coming up.

How are materials and furniture agreed without you on site?

We agree finishing materials from a specification: name, product code, photo, quantity, price. Things that are hard to judge on a screen — stone, fabrics, the shade of a paint — are better seen in person during one of your visits, or entrusted to someone you trust. Every approval should be recorded in writing, so that a month later nobody has to remember which shade was chosen.

Furniture, lighting and decor can be ordered directly from factories in China. Delivery takes 35–70 days, so the order is placed in parallel with the renovation. Made-to-order kitchens and wardrobes are produced by our own staff and partners, and installation takes place without your involvement.

How are the works accepted from a distance, and what is the warranty?

We hand over stages against certificates, and accept concealed works before they are covered, recording them in photos. The final acceptance is best done in person: come for a day or two, walk through the apartment with the design in hand and sign the certificate. If you cannot come, your representative can accept the works. The renovation handover checklist tells you what to check. The works carry a 12-month warranty.

How CAS works with clients abroad

We take on renovation and construction in Kyiv, the Kyiv region and every region of Ukraine, as well as projects in Europe. We start with a free phone consultation: you tell us about the property, and we give you a guide figure. Renovation costs from $550/m² (works and rough materials), a design project from $30/m², minimum $2,500; the site survey is paid, and its cost depends on the property. Next come the design, the estimate and the contract. One manager runs the project; working hours are Mon–Fri from 10:00 to 20:00 Kyiv time, and online enquiries are accepted around the clock. If our team does the renovation, the final total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%.

Questions and answers

Can I renovate in Ukraine without travelling there?

Yes, if there is a detailed design, a contract with an estimate and a schedule, and regular reports. It is advisable to come at least for the final acceptance, or to appoint a representative.

How do I pay for a renovation from abroad?

Against an invoice in dollars or euros via SWIFT, by card via a payment link, or in USDT. We agree the terms individually and fix them in the contract.

How do I follow the renovation from a distance?

Through photo reports every 1–2 weeks and stage certificates; the online client account, which every CAS client receives, shows the stages with a completion percentage, photos, documents and the payment schedule. A 24/7 camera on site is available for an extra fee.

Can the contract be drawn up in English?

Yes, for foreign clients we prepare the contract in English. You can sign it remotely, online, without visiting the office.
