Turnkey apartment fit-out: furniture, lighting, textiles — order and budget

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

A turnkey apartment fit-out is a sequence of purchases in which furniture, lighting, sanitaryware and textiles arrive on site at the right moment — not “go to a showroom and buy a sofa”. Here is where to start, where to finish and how to calculate the budget.

What is a turnkey apartment fit-out?

A fit-out is the selection, ordering, delivery and placement of everything that fills the interior after the renovation: furniture, light fittings, sanitaryware, textiles and decor. “Turnkey” means one team does this to an approved specification, rather than you dealing with dozens of shops and manufacturers yourself.

The main difficulty is not the choice itself but the order. Some things must be known before finishing works start, others can be bought almost at the end. A mistake in the sequence means either idle tradesmen or furniture that does not fit the room.

What should you buy first?

The first decisions are those that affect services and rough works. Without them the renovation will have to be redone.

  • large furniture and the kitchen — sockets, water and drainage outlets and lighting positions depend on them;
  • sanitaryware: bath, shower, WC, mixers — exact sizes are needed before waterproofing and tiling;
  • light fittings and the lighting plan — the type and number of points go into the electrical design;
  • built-in appliances — the dimensions of their niches are fixed before the furniture is made.

These should be bought to the design specification, with exact dimensions, colours and configuration. Then each item already has its place on the drawing.

What do you order after the rough works?

Once rough works are complete, items that can be fitted precisely to the finished walls are ordered: upholstered furniture, tables, chairs, wardrobes, mirrors. Fabrics and finishes are approved on samples, not from photos on a screen. Delivery dates are put into the renovation schedule so that installation does not stand idle.

What should you buy last?

Textiles and decor are left for the end, when the furniture is in place and the real light in the rooms is visible. These are curtains, rugs, bed linen, cushions, throws, pictures, vases, candles, plants.

  • curtains — after the rails and furniture are installed, when height and width are known;
  • rugs — when sofas, tables and beds are in place;
  • decor — last of all: it is easy to add later and hard to correct if bought in advance;
  • small household items — after you start living in the space and understand what is missing.

This order protects the budget: decor is bought with what is left over, not at the expense of the essentials.

How much does an apartment fit-out cost?

At CAS Development turnkey furnishing starts from $250/m². To estimate the budget, multiply the rate by the area. For example, for a 70 m² apartment the guide calculation is 70 × $250 = $17,500. For 50 m² it is 50 × $250 = $12,500.

This is a guide, not the final sum. The exact cost comes from the estimate based on the specification: it depends on the number of items and the chosen models. It is sensible to split the budget into groups in advance — furniture, lighting, sanitaryware, textiles, decor — and keep a separate part for unforeseen costs.

If you are planning both the renovation and the fit-out, consider them together: a turnkey renovation starts from $550/m², a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500). Then there will be no gap between what is in the design and what you can actually buy.

Furniture and lighting directly from China

Furniture and lighting can be ordered directly from factories in China. We clarify the details, calculate the full cost and only then agree the order; availability and price are confirmed by the manager.

We order in batches, not single pieces. So it makes most sense when you are furnishing the whole apartment or several rooms at once. Production and delivery times depend on the items, and we agree them separately for each batch.

What mistakes are made in a fit-out?

  • buying furniture before the layout is approved;
  • not checking door and lift widths for large items;
  • ordering lighting separately from the lighting plan;
  • buying decor first and spending the budget meant for furniture;
  • not building a delivery margin into the schedule.

Questions and answers

Where should an apartment fit-out start?

With large furniture, the kitchen, sanitaryware and the lighting plan: the services work depends on their dimensions. Leave textiles and decor for the end.

How much does furnishing an apartment cost?

At CAS Development furnishing starts from $250/m². For 70 m², roughly 70 × $250 = $17,500; the exact sum comes from the estimate based on the specification.

Can furniture and lighting from China be ordered as single items?

We order from China in batches, so it pays off when furnishing the whole apartment or several rooms. Individual items are selected within turnkey furnishing.

Is a design project needed for a fit-out?

It is not mandatory, but with a project specification there is less risk of mistakes with sizes, colours and quantities.
