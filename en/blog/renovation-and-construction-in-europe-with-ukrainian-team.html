Here is how we work abroad: what we do, what it costs, and how logistics, local rules and remote control are handled.

Who it is for

Our clients abroad are mostly people who have already lived in Kyiv interiors built to a design project and want the same level in their new home: a thought-out layout, working drawings, designer supervision, furnishing and a renovation where the project's decisions do not get lost on site. It also often matters to them to speak one language with the team and have one party responsible for the result.

CAS projects have been and are in Spain, France, Germany and Poland, and outside Europe — the Kitana Beach complex in Bodrum (Turkey), properties in Bali, a house in the USA and a villa in Dubai (design project). The service is described on the renovation and construction in Europe page.

  • interior design projects for apartments and houses — online, with trips for the survey and key stages
  • turnkey renovation in new builds, resale apartments and houses
  • house construction — shell or turnkey with finishes
  • an architectural house project as the basis for local permit documents
  • furnishing, bespoke kitchens and wardrobes
  • designer supervision when a local contractor does the renovation

Renovation and construction in Europe

Ukrainian team or local contractor: an honest comparison

A local contractor knows local rules and suppliers and does not spend money on travel. A full-cycle Ukrainian team is strong elsewhere: design, estimate and execution follow the same drawings, decisions are made fast and one party is responsible for the result. The choice depends on the task.

  • a complex interior with many custom solutions — the advantage is with the team that both designs and builds
  • a small, standard scope — often a project from us and execution by a local crew under our supervision is more rational
  • many local approvals — we plan the work with local specialists from day one
  • communication in Ukrainian and one manager for the whole project — that is our standard format

Prices in Europe and why they are higher than in Kyiv

Rates in Europe are about 50% higher than in Kyiv. The difference comes not only from local material prices: there is team travel and accommodation, logistics, longer preparation of documents for local requirements and more manager time for coordination. Our base "from" rates:

  • design project — from $45/m², minimum $4,000
  • turnkey renovation — from $850/m² (works and rough materials)
  • house shell — from $900/m²
  • turnkey house with finishes — from $1,700/m²
  • architectural house project — from $18/m²
  • furnishing — from $400/m²

For reference, a 90 m² apartment: design project 90 m² × $45 = $4,050, renovation 90 m² × $850 = $76,500 for works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate; logistics, trips and local materials are included in the estimate. The exact sum always depends on the property, the country and the scope.

How the process works

  1. A free phone or online consultation: country, property, tasks, budget.
  2. Input data: plans, photos and video, building or manager rules, plot documents for construction.
  3. Survey on site during a trip; the cost of the trip is calculated individually.
  4. Design or architectural project: online meetings, 3D, working drawings, specifications.
  5. Estimate and contract: scope, crew format, logistics, schedule and payment scheme.
  6. Works by our own team with photo reports and online meetings at key stages.
  7. Handover, signed acceptance and a 12-month warranty on works.

Local rules, permits and contractor requirements

Each country has its own rules; there is no single scheme for all of Europe. Renovating a flat in an apartment block often requires approval from the manager or owners' association, quiet hours and rules for lifts and common areas. Replanning, window replacement or facade changes may need separate approvals. For a house, permit documents follow national rules: usually a local licensed architect signs the permit project, and our architectural project becomes the basis for it. Some works — utility connections, gas, electrical sign-off — require local certified specialists in many countries.

  • what needs approval for your property — we find out before the estimate
  • who prepares documents and engages local specialists — set in the contract
  • the crew's working format in the country — agreed individually under local requirements
  • electrical fittings, sockets and equipment follow the country's standard, not "as in Kyiv"

Logistics: crew, materials, furniture

Renovation and construction are carried out by our salaried team. Travel and accommodation are agreed individually and fixed in the contract. Work is planned in blocks so that each trip closes a complete stage, with materials on site before the team arrives. Rough materials are usually cheaper to buy locally and meet local standards; finishing materials are chosen to the project, locally or delivered when price and timing justify it. Long-lead items — furniture, lighting, bespoke pieces — are ordered in parallel with the renovation, not after it. Construction waste sorting and removal, which in many cities follows separate rules, is included in the estimate in advance.

Timing

Timelines are set in the contract after the project and estimate. Abroad they depend on the same factors as in Kyiv — area, complexity, engineering — plus approval times with the manager or authorities, delivery times, the crew's travel schedule and restrictions on working hours. Preparation saves the most time: an approved project before the start, long-lead items ordered early and a clear approval schedule.

Remote supervision and communication

  • one manager responsible for the property and communication
  • photo reports every 1–2 weeks, including concealed works before they are closed
  • online site control and a 24/7 camera on site — at extra cost
  • online meetings at key stages: after rough engineering, before finishing, before furniture installation
  • designer supervision with site visits at key moments

Contract, payment and invoices

Scope, estimate, timing and payment scheme are fixed in the contract before the start. Payment schemes are tailored: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or more instalments, starting from a 10% advance — see paying for a renovation in instalments. We invoice companies registered in Ukraine; invoices for EU companies are being set up, so ask the manager about current options. Works carry a 12-month warranty; terms are on the contract, warranty, payment page.

Typical risks and how we remove them

  • inaccurate measurements from someone else's drawings — we do our own survey on a trip
  • local requirements discovered mid-works — we check them before the estimate
  • material delays — long-lead items are ordered in advance
  • vague agreements — scope, timing and payment are fixed in the contract
  • loss of control at a distance — one manager, photo reports and online meetings

How to start

Call or leave a request: tell us the country, property and task, and we will give an approximate cost at base rates and the next steps. After the survey and project you receive an estimate broken down into works, materials and logistics, and make your decision with it in hand. CAS Development has worked since 2016 — more than 150 projects in Ukraine and abroad. We take on renovation and construction in all regions of Ukraine and in Europe.

Questions and answers

Which European countries do you work in?

Projects have been and are in Spain, France, Germany and Poland. For other countries we discuss the format individually, taking local requirements and logistics into account.

How much does a renovation in Europe cost?

Turnkey renovation from $850/m² for works and rough materials, design project from $45/m² (minimum $4,000), house shell from $900/m², turnkey house from $1,700/m². Logistics, trips and local materials are estimated separately.

Does your own team work abroad?

Yes, renovation and construction are done by our salaried team. Travel, accommodation and the working format under local requirements are agreed individually and fixed in the contract.

Can I order only an online design project?

Yes. The project is run online with trips for the survey and key stages, from $45/m², minimum $4,000. A local contractor can then do the renovation under our supervision.

How can I control the renovation if I live elsewhere?

One manager runs the property, photo reports come every 1–2 weeks, and there are online meetings at key stages. A 24/7 site camera is available at extra cost.
