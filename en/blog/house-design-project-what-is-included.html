House design project: what it includes and how much it costs

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

House design in Kyiv is the first step towards construction, and it determines both how comfortable life will be and the budget. Here is what a house project consists of, why you need it and how much it costs.

What is a house project?

A house project is a set of decisions and drawings that the construction team works from on the plot. It answers where everything goes, what it is built from, how the services run and how it will all look. Without a project each of these decisions is made “on the go”, and that is almost always more expensive.

What does a house project include?

  • layout: the position of the house on the plot, floors, rooms, entrances, stairs, boiler room, wardrobes and pantries
  • facades: form, materials, windows, roof — together with a 3D visualisation so you can see the house before work starts
  • structure: foundation, walls, floors, roof structure
  • services: heating, water supply, drainage, electrics, ventilation
  • interior: positions of sockets, lights, sanitaryware and furniture, so finished walls do not have to be reworked

The components depend on the brief: a small house needs one scope, a two-storey house with underfloor heating, heat-recovery ventilation and backup power another. We agree the scope of the project with you at the start.

Why do you need a house project?

  1. you see the result before the foundation is poured
  2. you get a basis for an accurate estimate — without a project a price “per square metre” guarantees nothing
  3. builders work from drawings, not from verbal agreements
  4. services are planned in advance rather than cut into finished walls

What do you lose by building without a project?

Without a project people most often lose money and nerves. A layout that was not thought through becomes an inconvenient house: the boiler room ends up in the wrong place and there is no wardrobe at all. Services that were not built into the structure have to be cut through finished walls. And an estimate without drawings is just an assumption that changes along with the decisions during construction.

A mistake on paper is easy to fix. A mistake in concrete and brick is expensive. That is why the project is ordered before construction, not in parallel with it.

How much does a house project cost?

An architectural house project at CAS Development starts from $12/m²: concept and working design with the architecture and structural sections; heating and ventilation, water and drainage, electrics and the site plan are added to the brief. For a 150 m² house the guide is: 150 m² × $12 = from $1,800. In Europe an architectural project starts from $18/m². The exact sum depends on the area, the complexity of the house and the set of sections, so we name the final cost after discussing the brief and the plot.

Interior design is a separate service: from $30/m², with a minimum budget of $2,500 (for 150 m² — from $4,500). It can be ordered together with the architectural project or later, once the shell is up.

To compare the scale: house construction starts from $600/m², construction with a turnkey renovation from $1,100/m². For the same 150 m² that is from $90,000 and from $165,000. These are guides; the exact sum comes from an estimate drawn up from the project.

Why keep design and construction in one team?

When one team designs and another builds, decisions get lost between them: the drawings do not take the builders' capabilities into account, and the builders ask about what should have been in the project. We handle both stages, so the project is written from the start with an understanding of how to build it. More about the service on the house design page.

What should you prepare before design starts?

  • information about the plot: boundaries, terrain, access, the possibility of connecting electricity, gas and water
  • the family's wishes: how many bedrooms, whether you need a study, a terrace, a carport
  • an approximate budget for the project and construction
  • examples of houses you like

That is enough to begin: we clarify the rest at the consultation and when inspecting the plot.

How we work

We start with the project, then draw up an estimate and agree it with you. Next comes house construction with one manager on site, regular reports and stage payments. The works carry a 12-month warranty.

Questions and answers

What does a house project include?

Layout, facades with a 3D visualisation, structure, building services and interior solutions. We agree the scope with you at the start, based on the brief.

How much does a house project cost?

An architectural project starts from $12/m² (concept and working design, architecture and structure; services and site plan to the brief) — from $1,800 for 150 m². Interior design is a separate service: from $30/m², minimum $2,500.

Can you build without a project?

You can, but then decisions are made on the go and the estimate stays an assumption. Fixing mistakes in what has been built is more expensive than on drawings.

Do you build to your own project?

Yes. We handle both design and construction, so the project takes into account from the start how it will be built.
