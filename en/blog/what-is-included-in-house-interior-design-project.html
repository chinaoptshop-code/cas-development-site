What is included in a house interior design project: scope, stages and price

CAS Development · 16 November 2026

A house is more complex than an apartment: stairs, double-height spaces, a fireplace, a boiler room, a terrace, several bathrooms. Here is what a house interior design project includes, how it differs from the architectural project and which documents builders and joiners need to work without guessing.

Design project vs architectural project

The architectural project answers what the house will be outside and in its structure: walls, floors, roof, windows, facade, position on the plot. The interior design project starts inside: how you live within these walls, where the furniture stands, where the light comes from, which materials go on floors and walls.

It works best when both projects are done in parallel, or at least before construction starts. Then sockets, water outlets, wardrobe niches and light positions are built into the structure from the outset. If the house is already standing, the designer works with what is there and some decisions have to fit existing walls. The architectural part is covered separately — see house design.

Survey, brief and floor plans

Work starts with a brief: who lives in the house, whether you work from home, whether guests stay overnight, where laundry dries, where seasonal things and garden tools are stored. For a house these questions matter more than for an apartment, because there is more space and it is easy to lay it out badly.

Next comes a survey, or a check of the drawings if the house is not built yet. The designer proposes layouts for each floor: a day zone downstairs, a private zone upstairs, a guest bedroom, wardrobes, technical rooms. Routes are thought through separately: from the entrance to the kitchen with shopping bags, from the bedroom to the bathroom at night, from the terrace into the house with wet feet.

  • a measured plan of each floor with heights and openings
  • 2–3 layout options with furniture
  • zoning: day, night, guest and technical areas
  • places for the boiler room, laundry, pantry and wardrobes

Interior design from $30/m² (min. $2,500), space planning from $6/m². Phone consultation is free.

Concept and 3D of every room

Once the layout is approved, the designer builds the concept: style, palette, materials, character of light. Then come 3D visualisations — not only of the living room but also of stairs, corridors, bathrooms and bedrooms. In a house, transitions between spaces often look weakest if they were not drawn in advance.

3D shows clearly how a double-height space works, whether the chandelier hangs too high, how the fireplace reads from the kitchen. Changes at this stage cost an hour of the designer's time, not demolition.

Working drawings: what is specific to a house

The basic drawing set is the same as for an apartment: demolition and new partitions, electrics, plumbing, ceilings, floors, wall elevations. It is described in What is included in an interior design project. A house adds details an apartment usually lacks:

  • stairs: structure, balustrade, step lighting, finishes
  • fireplace or stove: cladding, clearances, position relative to furniture
  • double-height ceilings and beams, fixing heavy light fittings
  • entrance, vestibule and space for coats and shoes
  • terrace and exit to the garden: surfacing, lighting, link with the interior
  • technical rooms: boiler room, laundry, switch room — sized for the equipment

Another feature is coordination with engineering. Underfloor heating, radiators, ventilation grilles, air conditioners and smart-home sensors must sit where furniture will not block them and where they will not spoil a panelled wall. So the designer's plans are checked against the engineering sections of the house project.

Lighting, bespoke furniture and specification

A house lighting plan is usually more complex: several scenes for the living room, night lighting on stairs and in corridors, niche lighting, facade and garden lighting if the interior continues onto the plot. Each group has a fitting, height and switch.

Kitchens, wardrobes, under-stair cupboards and study shelving are drawn separately with dimensions, fronts and hardware. Joiners work from these drawings; we make bespoke kitchens and wardrobes with our own staff and partners.

The specification puts everything in one list: finishing materials, sanitary ware, light fittings, furniture, doors — with item codes, quantities and location in the house. The estimate is built on it. If CAS carries out the works, the total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%.

Checklist: what a house design project should contain

  • an approved furnished layout of every floor
  • 3D of all rooms, including stairs and bathrooms
  • drawings of stairs, fireplace and double-height ceilings
  • electrical and lighting plans with groups and scenes
  • plumbing, underfloor heating and radiator plans
  • drawings of the kitchen, wardrobes and built-in furniture
  • a specification with item codes and an estimate

How much a house design project costs

CAS Development has three formats. A space plan from $6/m² when only layout and zoning are needed. A full design project from $30/m². A full project with designer supervision from $45/m². The minimum for every format is $2,500.

For a 200 m² house a full project costs from 200 m² × $30 = $6,000, and with supervision from 200 m² × $45 = $9,000. We name the exact sum after the brief: it depends on the number of floors, bathrooms, bespoke furniture and the complexity of the stairs. Supervision is especially useful for a house: there is more work, it lasts longer and questions on site come up more often — see the article on designer supervision.

Questions and answers

How does a house design project differ from an apartment project?

In the drawing set: a house adds stairs, a fireplace, double-height ceilings, the entrance, a terrace and technical rooms, and the plans must be checked against the engineering sections of the house project.

When should I order a house design project?

Ideally before construction or together with the architectural project, so that service outlets, niches and light positions are built into the structure.

How much does a house design project cost?

A full project from $30/m², with designer supervision from $45/m², a space plan from $6/m². The minimum is $2,500.

Can I order only the design and build with another contractor?

Yes. The project contains all drawings and the specification that any crew can work from. Technical and designer supervision are then discussed separately.
