Interior design prices in Kyiv: cost per m² and what each package includes

CAS Development · 31 October 2026

Interior design in Kyiv costs different amounts depending on how much documentation you need: from planning at $6/m² to a full project with designer supervision from $45/m². Design is priced per m², so we explain what each package includes and work out figures for 40, 80 and 120 m² apartments.

What the price of design depends on

A design project is priced per square metre, but the rate depends on the scope of work, not on the "style". Some clients only need a good layout so they can run the renovation themselves. Others need a full set of drawings for the crew. Others also want the designer to accompany the renovation so that the result matches the visualisations.

That is why CAS Development has three standard packages. The scope can be tailored, but the packages show the order of figures at once and let you compare offers from different studios on the same set of documents.

Three packages and their prices

  • Planning — from $6/m²: measured survey, 2–3 layout options, furniture layout, room schedule with areas.
  • Basic — from $30/m², minimum $2,500: everything in Planning, 3D visualisations of all rooms, working drawings (demolition and new walls, electrics, plumbing, ceilings, floors, tile layout), specifications of materials and furniture.
  • Full with designer supervision — from $45/m²: everything in Basic, elevations of all walls, bespoke furniture drawings, designer supervision during the renovation with visits on a schedule set in the contract, selection of furniture, lighting and decor.

More on the packages on the interior design page. Purchasing and delivery of furniture and materials are not part of the design price — that is a separate furnishing service.

Interior design from $30/m² (min. $2,500), space planning from $6/m². Phone consultation is free.

Planning package: when it is enough

A layout is the foundation of any interior. It decides where the kitchen, wardrobes and bed stand, how much storage there is and how people move around the flat. This package suits a simple renovation where you are ready to pick materials and furniture yourself but do not want to get the walls and zones wrong.

What it lacks: visualisations and working drawings. The crew will have to make some decisions on its own — for example, where exactly to put sockets or how to lay the tiles.

Basic package: drawings for the crew

The basic package is a full design project: you see the interior in 3D before the renovation starts, and the crew gets dimensioned drawings. The renovation estimate is built from the working drawings and specifications, so the basic project also tells you the budget in advance, not just how the flat will look.

The minimum price of a design project is $2,500, so for small apartments the total is set by that minimum rather than the per-metre rate.

Full package with designer supervision

Even the best drawings do not guarantee the result if nobody checks the work against the project during the renovation. Designer supervision means regular site visits on a schedule in the contract: the designer answers the crew's questions, checks that the work matches the drawings and helps when something has to change on site.

The full package also includes elevations of all walls, bespoke furniture drawings and selection of furniture, lighting and decor. If another contractor does the renovation, supervision terms and cost are agreed individually — see designer and technical supervision.

Examples: 40, 80 and 120 m² apartments

Below is a calculation at the studio's base rates. It is the lower bound: approximate, the exact figure comes from the estimate.

  • 40 m²: planning from 40 × $6 = $240; basic from $2,500 (40 × $30 = $1,200, the minimum applies); full 40 × $45 = $1,800 at the rate, but not less than the $2,500 design project minimum.
  • 80 m²: planning from 80 × $6 = $480; basic from $2,500 (80 × $30 = $2,400, the minimum applies); full from 80 × $45 = $3,600.
  • 120 m²: planning from 120 × $6 = $720; basic from 120 × $30 = $3,600; full from 120 × $45 = $5,400.

The total depends on the number of rooms, the complexity of the layout, the amount of bespoke furniture and the supervision schedule. More examples on the interior design cost in Kyiv page.

What to budget for separately

  • renovation — from $550/m²: works and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are estimated separately
  • furnishing — from $250/m²
  • measurement on site — paid, the cost depends on the property
  • purchasing and delivery of furniture and decor, if needed as part of furnishing

Questions and answers

How much does apartment interior design cost in Kyiv?

At CAS Development from $6/m² for planning, from $30/m² for the basic package (minimum $2,500) and from $45/m² for the full package with designer supervision. The exact sum comes from the estimate.

What is included in a basic design project?

A layout, 3D visualisations of all rooms, working drawings for the crew and specifications of materials and furniture.

Does the design price include buying furniture?

No. Selection of furniture, lighting and decor is part of the full package; purchasing and delivery are separate, as part of furnishing.

Is the on-site measurement paid?

Yes, measurement is paid and its cost depends on the property. A phone consultation is free.
