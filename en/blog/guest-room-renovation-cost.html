Guest room renovation cost: what drives it and how to estimate

CAS Development · 23 November 2026

Guest room renovation cost comes down to four things: the floor area, the condition of the room, how much work it needs and what furniture goes in. Below is how to estimate it yourself, where a design project saves money and what our own rates look like as a reference point.

What decides the cost of a guest room renovation?

The scope of work decides it more than anything else. A refresh with paint, new lighting and textiles is one budget; stripping the room back to bare walls, moving sockets and adding an en-suite shower is a completely different one. Area matters too, because most labour and materials are priced per square metre, but two rooms of the same size can differ several times in cost depending on what is behind the walls. If the property is outside Ukraine, the formats are described on the interior design and turnkey projects worldwide page.

  • floor area and ceiling height
  • condition: whether walls, floor and wiring can stay or need replacing
  • scope: cosmetic refresh, full renovation or a room with its own bathroom
  • furniture, lighting and textiles
  • where the property is: labour and materials are priced locally

How do you estimate the cost yourself?

Start with a list of works, not with a total. Measure the room, then go surface by surface: floor, walls, ceiling, electrics, windows and doors. For each one note whether it stays, gets refreshed or gets replaced. That list is what a contractor needs to quote, and it is the only way to compare quotes from different builders on equal terms.

  1. measure the room: length, width, ceiling height, window and door openings
  2. list each surface and decide: keep, refresh or replace
  3. add electrics and lighting: number of sockets, switches and light points
  4. list furniture and textiles separately from construction work
  5. ask two or three local contractors to price the same list of works
  6. keep a reserve for surprises found once old finishes come off

Separate the renovation itself from furniture and decor. Builders quote the first; the second you can buy in stages, and it is the part of the budget that is easiest to control. For a quick guide figure for your area, use the renovation cost calculator.

What does a design project add to a guest room?

A design project turns a wish list into drawings a builder can price and follow: furniture layout, lighting plan, socket positions, finishes and a specification of every item. It prevents the most expensive kind of mistake — rework, when a bed does not fit between the window and the door, or a wall light ends up behind the wardrobe.

We design interiors in any country and can run the whole project online: you send plans, measurements and photos or a video walkthrough, and you receive drawings and specifications for your local contractor. For projects that interest us we also come to the site. A design project costs from $30/m², with a minimum project of $2,500, so for a single small guest room the minimum applies; it pays off best when the guest room is part of a larger home project.

What are CAS rates, as a reference?

Our own rates are a reference point, not a forecast for your city. Interior design: from $30/m², minimum $2,500. Turnkey renovation by our team in Ukraine: from $550/m² (labour and rough materials; finishing materials and furniture are priced separately). Renovation in Europe: from $850/m². For a 14 m² guest room that is 14 m² × $550 = $7,700 in Ukraine and 14 m² × $850 = $11,900 in Europe, before finishing materials and furniture.

We do not quote local labour prices in the USA or other countries outside Ukraine and Europe. There the price comes from a local contractor, who quotes from the scope of works and the specification in our project; we find and vet contractors and manage them to the design, with terms agreed individually.

Where does a guest room budget usually go over?

  • hidden problems under old finishes: uneven walls, damp, outdated wiring
  • changing decisions after works have started
  • buying furniture before the layout is final
  • adding a bathroom without checking drainage and ventilation first

Most of these are avoided by fixing the layout and the list of works before anyone starts demolition, and by keeping changes on paper rather than on site.

How does CAS work on a guest room or a whole home?

Since 2016 we have completed more than 150 projects. Each one has a single manager, a stage schedule and a design that a builder can price line by line. In Ukraine and Europe our own team carries out the renovation, with photo reports every 1–2 weeks and a 12-month warranty on works; in other countries we bring in local contractors and supervise them to our drawings. A phone consultation is free — tell us about the room and the property, and we will suggest a format.

Questions and answers

How much does a guest room renovation cost?

It depends on the area, condition, scope of works and furniture. As a reference, CAS renovation in Ukraine starts from $550/m² and in Europe from $850/m², before finishing materials and furniture.

Do I need a designer for one guest room?

Not always. For a simple refresh a clear list of works is enough; a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500) pays off when the room is part of a larger project or needs a new layout or a bathroom.

Can you design a guest room remotely?

Yes. You send plans, measurements and photos or a video, and we deliver drawings and specifications for your local contractor.

Do you quote renovation prices in the USA?

No. Outside Ukraine and Europe the price comes from a local contractor, who quotes from the scope of works in our project.
