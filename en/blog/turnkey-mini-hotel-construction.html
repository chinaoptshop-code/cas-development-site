Turnkey mini-hotel construction: from concept to opening

CAS Development · 16 November 2026

A mini-hotel is a business in which every decision in the drawings later affects revenue: how many rooms fit, how quickly they can be cleaned, how durable the finishes are. Here is how turnkey mini-hotel construction goes — from concept and design to procurement and the first guests.

Which guest is the mini-hotel built for?

Building a mini-hotel starts with the question of the guest. Business trips in the city, weekends out of town, families with children, event guests — each has their own expectations of the room, breakfast, parking and lounge areas. This determines the format: a boutique hotel in the centre, an aparthotel with kitchens in the rooms, a country hotel with a restaurant and terrace.

At this stage it is also worth settling the budget, the opening date and who will run the hotel. An operator or manager often has their own requirements for the reception, laundry and storage layout, and it is better to hear them before the design, not after.

  • guest type and length of stay
  • number and types of rooms: standard, suites, family
  • whether there will be a restaurant, bar or breakfast area
  • extra services: spa, pool, conference room, terrace
  • budget and desired opening date

Build from scratch or convert an existing building?

A mini-hotel can be built from scratch or converted from an existing building. A new build gives freedom in planning: rooms, shafts, bathrooms and back-of-house areas are placed to suit hotel operations. A conversion is faster at the start, but the building's structure, heights and services limit how many rooms fit and where bathrooms can go.

In both cases you first check that the plot or building can be used as a hotel and which requirements apply to such a property. This is done before purchase or before the design starts — later, any mismatch becomes expensive.

Hotel design and turnkey fit-out

Where does a mini-hotel design start?

We start a hotel design with function; style comes later. First you determine how many rooms fit on a floor and how they sit relative to shafts and corridors. Typical rooms are designed as a system: the same furniture, lighting, sanitaryware and textiles across the floor. This simplifies purchasing, repairs and replacement, and guests get the same quality in every room.

Guest and staff flows are separated: a service entrance, the housekeeper's route with linen, food deliveries to the kitchen, waste. If these routes cross the lobby, the hotel looks untidy even with a beautiful interior.

  • sound insulation between rooms and from the corridor
  • hard-wearing floor, wall and furniture finishes
  • bathrooms that are quick to clean
  • linen storage and laundry on each floor or centralised
  • a lobby and reception with a view of the entrance

You can see how a country complex with several buildings is planned in the interactive “Holiday complex” scene on the turnkey holiday resort page: master plan, stage-by-stage construction of each building and a price panel.

How do hotel building services differ from a house?

A hotel differs from a residential house above all in its services: many bathrooms, hot water at peak hours, ventilation for every room, a restaurant kitchen, a security system. These sections are designed together with the architecture so that shafts and risers do not have to be squeezed into finished walls.

We cost hotel construction and renovation from the design: the price depends on the structure, services, number of bathrooms and equipment, so a single rate per metre says nothing here. We draw up the estimate before work starts; if our team carries out the works, the total does not deviate from it by more than ±5%.

How are rooms and public areas furnished?

Furniture, lighting, textiles, sanitaryware and tableware for a hotel are best ordered as a batch, because the rooms are typical. They can be ordered directly from factories in China, including made to order — colour, size, material. Delivery takes 35–70 days, so we order right after the specification is approved, in parallel with construction.

What exactly goes into fitting out a room, lobby and restaurant is covered in our article on hotel procurement.

Why does a mini-hotel need a restaurant, and how does it open?

A restaurant or breakfast area often becomes the heart of a small hotel and also serves guests from the city. Its kitchen, ventilation and seating are designed together with the hotel. An example of a completed restaurant is Monpark in central Kyiv: design and implementation by CAS.

Before opening we install furniture and textiles, set up the lighting, style the rooms and public areas, inspect the property together with you and sign the certificates.

How much does a turnkey mini-hotel cost?

A hotel design project starts from $30/m², with a minimum price of $2,500. Construction, renovation and procurement are costed from the design. For companies there is a contract; for companies registered in Ukraine, invoicing with or without VAT, individually. Invoicing for EU companies is being set up.

We take a mini-hotel from concept to opening with one team: design, estimate, construction, procurement and launch. You have one manager, photo reports every 1–2 weeks and a 12-month warranty on the works. More about formats on the hotel design and renovation page and in the article how to plan a holiday resort.

Questions and answers

How much does it cost to build a mini-hotel?

A design project starts from $30/m², minimum $2,500. Construction and renovation are costed from the design: the price depends on the structure, services, number of bathrooms and equipment.

Is it better to build from scratch or convert a building?

From scratch gives more freedom in planning the rooms and services. A conversion is faster at the start, but the building's structure limits the number of rooms and where bathrooms go.

Can the rooms be furnished from China?

Yes, we order furniture, lighting and textiles as a batch directly from factories in China; delivery takes 35–70 days. Made-to-order items are possible: colour, size, material.

Do you run the project through to opening?

Yes. Design, construction, procurement, installation and styling — by one team with one manager.
