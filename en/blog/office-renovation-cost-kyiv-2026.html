How much an office renovation costs in Kyiv in 2026: what the price per m² consists of

CAS Development · 26 October 2026

How much an office renovation costs in Kyiv is the question almost every conversation with a company starts with. We explain what the price per m² consists of, what the base rate includes, what is costed separately and how to estimate a budget roughly before measuring.

Base price: from $350/m²

At CAS a turnkey office renovation starts from $350/m². This rate covers works and rough materials — everything without which the premises will not become a working office. We cost this part of the budget first: it determines how quickly the team can move in.

  • demolition of old finishes and replanning
  • partitions, walls, ceilings, screed and plaster
  • electrics, low-voltage systems, network for workstations
  • installation of building services
  • rough materials for all these works

What is costed separately

The second part of the budget is what every employee sees and touches. Here the price depends most on your decisions, so we put these items into separate sections of the estimate.

  • finishing materials: floor covering, tiles, paint, doors
  • furniture, light fittings and decor
  • appliances and equipment
  • a design project — from $30/m², minimum price $2,500

This split is convenient for a finance director: you see how much goes on the base and how much on the choice of materials and furniture. If the budget is limited, it is better to save on decor rather than on services.

How much an office renovation costs: example calculations

As a guide, at the base rate of $350/m² (works and rough materials):

  • a 100 m² office — from $35,000 (100 × $350)
  • a 200 m² office — from $70,000 (200 × $350)
  • a 400 m² office — from $140,000 (400 × $350)
  • a design project for a 200 m² office — from $6,000 (200 × $30)

This is a “from” guide, not the price of your property. The exact sum comes from the estimate after measuring, with each item on a separate line. You can quickly sketch a budget on the office renovation cost page or in the cost calculator.

What affects the price per m²

  • the condition of the premises: bare walls from the developer or an old fit-out to demolish
  • the scope of replanning: open space or many offices and partitions
  • services: ventilation, air conditioning, number of workstations and network points
  • the level of finishing materials in client areas
  • the business centre's requirements for works and approvals

An open space is usually simpler to build than a cellular layout: fewer partitions and doors. But it needs more attention to acoustics and lighting. How to choose the format is covered in the article open space or private offices.

How to stay on budget

  • start with a design project: drawings remove most “surprises” on site
  • approve an itemised estimate rather than a single sum
  • include the business centre's requirements before the start, not during the works
  • order furniture right after the specification is approved — delivery from China takes 35–70 days
  • fix the work schedule and handover date in the contract

If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%. Any extra works only after your written approval.

An office in a business centre: what is added to the estimate

In a rented office some conditions are set by the building: design approval, connection to the business centre's services, relocation of fire alarm detectors, hours for noisy works, the goods lift and waste removal. All this affects both the schedule and the sum. That is why we clarify these requirements before costing and include them in the estimate as separate lines rather than adding them during the works.

It is useful to ask the landlord for the rules on works before signing the lease.

Payment, documents and timing

We split payments to suit the company: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or stage payments; the start is from a 10% advance. For companies registered in Ukraine we issue invoices with or without VAT and provide closing documents. Timing and the work schedule are fixed in the contract — from 2 weeks depending on area and scope.
