How to choose an interior design studio in Kyiv: 10 questions to ask before signing

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

An interior design studio is not just beautiful visualisations — it is also working drawings, support during the renovation and a clear contract. Below are 10 questions to ask a studio before signing, with an explanation of why each one matters — whether you live in Kyiv or are commissioning the project from abroad.

Why do questions to a studio matter more than its portfolio?

A portfolio shows a studio's taste, but not how it works with clients: what exactly you will receive, who is responsible for mistakes and what happens when the design reaches the renovation stage. That is why, when choosing a design studio, it is worth talking not only about style but about process. The ten questions below help you do that calmly and to the point.

Our work is collected in the studio portfolio (in Ukrainian) — look not only at the images but at whether there are completed projects behind them.

Questions 1–3: what does the design project include?

  1. What exactly does the design project include? Why it matters: design can mean anything from a few pictures to a full set of documentation. Ask for a list: space planning, 3D visualisations, drawings for the crew, lighting and socket plans, specifications for materials and furniture.
  2. Can the renovation be done from the project without guesswork? Why it matters: crews work from drawings, not visualisations. If the documentation is incomplete, tradespeople fill in the details themselves and the result differs from the intent.
  3. How many layout options and how many revisions are included? Why it matters: it is better to agree this in advance. You will not find out mid-project that the second revision is chargeable, and the studio does not lose time on endless rework.

Interior design from $30/m² (min. $2,500), space planning from $6/m². Phone consultation is free.

Questions 4–5: what happens after the design?

  1. Is designer supervision offered, and what does it include? Why it matters: these are the designer's site visits, checks against the drawings and resolution of issues that arise during the renovation. Without supervision even a good design can drift on site. Visit terms should be written into the contract.
  2. Does the studio implement the project itself? Why it matters: when one team designs and another renovates, responsibility gets blurred. When design and renovation are handled by one company, the question of whose mistake it is disappears. At CAS Development design, renovation and furniture are one process: the design, renovation by our own team and furniture sourced from China.

In-house implementation is not necessarily the best option for every client. But you should know whether it is available and how the studio works if another crew does the renovation. If your property is abroad, also ask how the studio supports a local contractor remotely.

Questions 6–7: what do design and execution cost?

  1. How is the design fee calculated? Why it matters: design is most often priced per square metre. At CAS a design project costs from $30/m², with a minimum fee of $2,500. That means the minimum applies to a small apartment, while for larger areas the fee is the rate multiplied by the area. For 100 m², for example, the benchmark is $3,000 at the rate of $30/m². The exact amount depends on the scope of work.
  2. What is not included in the design fee? Why it matters: ask for a clear list. Renovation, furniture and material purchases are separate budgets. Knowing the boundaries, you will not mistake ordinary additional services for hidden charges.

Benchmarks for the next stage if the project moves to implementation with CAS: turnkey renovation from $550/m², furnishing from $250/m². These are starting rates; the final amount comes from the estimate after studying the design.

Questions 8–10: what should the contract say?

  1. What does the contract say? Why it matters: the contract should state the documentation scope, stages, payment terms, number of revisions, supervision terms and the parties' responsibilities. Anything agreed verbally but not written down is not a guarantee.
  2. Who is my contact and how will I see progress? Why it matters: it is best when one manager is responsible for the project and the client receives regular reports instead of hunting for someone to call. Agree on the communication channel and report format before starting — especially important if you are in another time zone.
  3. What warranty is given and what does it cover? Why it matters: a warranty should be in writing and apply to specific works. At CAS completed works carry a 12-month warranty.

What should you check before signing?

  • a list of project documents — with names and formats
  • the rate per m², the minimum fee and what it excludes
  • designer supervision terms: what the designer checks and how often
  • who carries out the renovation and who is responsible for the result
  • the procedure for changes and revisions
  • warranty obligations in writing
  • the manager's name and the reporting format
  • how invoices are issued — at CAS, for companies registered in Ukraine; EU invoicing is being set up

If a studio answers these questions directly and without evasion, that is a good sign. If the answers are vague, clarify again before signing — not after.

Questions and answers

How much does a design project cost at a Kyiv design studio?

At CAS Development interior design costs from $30/m², with a minimum fee of $2,500. The exact amount depends on the area and scope of work.

Do I have to order the renovation from the same studio?

No. But when one team does both design and renovation, one party is responsible for the result and the design is implemented without losses at the handover between contractors.

Do I need designer supervision?

It is especially useful when the design has complex solutions and bespoke furniture, or when a third-party crew does the renovation. Supervision terms should be written into the contract.

What should I look for in a contract with a design studio?

The documentation scope, stages and payment terms, the number of revisions, supervision terms, the warranty and the parties' responsibilities.
