Major or cosmetic renovation: which one your apartment needs

CAS Development · 1 November 2026

A cosmetic refresh is faster and cheaper, while a major renovation solves problems for years ahead — and this choice is where most resale-apartment enquiries start. We explain how they differ, how to tell that a refresh will no longer help, and how not to overpay in either case.

How does a major renovation differ from a cosmetic one?

The key difference is whether the renovation touches what is hidden in the walls and floor — not the budget or how good the result will look. A cosmetic renovation refreshes surfaces: walls, ceilings, floor coverings, doors, light fittings. A major renovation goes deeper: it replaces the services, levels the bases and changes the layout, wet zones and electrics.

So the choice is first of all a technical decision. If the services are in good condition and the layout suits you, a cosmetic refresh may be enough. If not, fresh paint only hides problems that in a year or two will have to be solved together with tearing out the new finishes.

What does a cosmetic renovation include?

A cosmetic apartment renovation is a refresh without touching the services or structure. A typical list of works:

  • skim-coating and painting walls and ceilings or hanging wallpaper
  • replacing floor coverings without re-pouring the screed
  • replacing interior doors, skirting, sockets and switches in the same places
  • replacing light fittings and sanitaryware without moving the outlets
  • minor tile repairs, regrouting, renewing sealants
  • cleaning and preparation for moving in or letting

Cosmetic work does well when the base is sound: walls are even, wiring and pipes are fairly new and the layout fits the way you live. It is faster, less noisy and lets you refresh the home with minimal intervention. Another plus is less dust and noise for neighbours and fewer approvals with the building management. The minus is that it changes nothing in how the apartment works: if you lack sockets, storage or light, it will not fix that.

What does a major renovation include?

A major apartment renovation starts with stripping old finishes and often partitions. Then the apartment goes through the full cycle:

  • removal of finishes, old sanitaryware, wiring and, if needed, partitions
  • re-planning to the design and building new partitions
  • new electrics: cables, distribution board, breakers, points for appliances and light
  • new plumbing: water and drainage runs, concealed frames, waterproofing of wet zones
  • ventilation and air conditioning, underfloor heating — per the design
  • levelling walls and ceilings, new screed
  • finishing, installation of doors, sanitaryware and light fittings

A major renovation almost always needs a design project with working drawings: without them you cannot route the services correctly or draw up an accurate estimate. The difference between rough and finishing stages is described in detail in the article “Rough and finishing renovation: the difference”.

Comparison: a checklist of key parameters

  • Services: cosmetic — stay as they are; major — replaced fully or partly
  • Layout: cosmetic — unchanged; major — zoning can change and the kitchen or bathroom can move within what is permitted
  • Bases: cosmetic — local fixes; major — levelling of walls, ceilings and floors
  • Design: cosmetic — a plan and choice of materials are enough; major — a full design project with drawings
  • Timing: cosmetic — a shorter cycle; major — the full sequence of stages with technological pauses
  • Living in: cosmetic — sometimes possible in stages; major — you cannot live in the apartment during the works
  • Service life: cosmetic — until the next surface refresh; major — lays the foundation for many years

When is a cosmetic renovation enough?

  • the apartment had a good-quality renovation fairly recently and only needs refreshing
  • the services have already been replaced and you know when and how
  • the layout suits you and there is no need to move the kitchen or bathroom
  • the home is being prepared for sale or letting on a limited budget
  • you need to move in quickly and plan a bigger renovation later

Even in these cases an inspection is worthwhile: check the distribution board, the pipes under the sinks, the bathroom ventilation and the humidity in the corners. It takes little time but protects you from new tiles covering a leak.

When is a major renovation needed?

A major renovation is justified when the problem is not how the apartment looks but how it works. Typical situations: an apartment in an old building with original wiring and pipes, a new bare-concrete apartment with no finishes, or the wish to change the layout, move the kitchen, merge rooms or add a walk-in wardrobe.

Signs that a cosmetic refresh will no longer help

  • the board has old breakers or fuses, and the power trips when appliances are switched on
  • sockets get warm, the wiring is aluminium or of unknown age
  • pipes are rusty, water pressure is low, the neighbours below have leak marks
  • cracks in walls and ceilings come back after skim-coating
  • the floor creaks, has level differences, tiles sound hollow
  • the bathroom has no effective ventilation and there is mould in the corners
  • the layout does not match how the family lives

If you see two or three of these points, a cosmetic refresh will be a temporary fix. It is wiser to plan a major renovation at least for the services from the start, even if the finishes stay simple.

A bare-concrete new build

In a new apartment without finishes the question “cosmetic or major” does not really arise: services, screed, plaster and partitions have to be done from scratch. This is a full renovation cycle, and the most important thing is to start with the design so that sockets, water outlets and ventilation end up where they are needed. More on the specifics on the new-build renovation page.

The in-between option

Sometimes a full major renovation is not needed, but part of the work should go deeper: replace the wiring and board, redo the bathroom and only refresh the rest of the apartment. Such a mixed scope can also be priced in one estimate; the main thing is to separate honestly which zones get new services and which get only finishes. You can also order rough renovation only if you plan the finishing later.

How to decide: inspecting the apartment step by step

  1. Check the board: type of breakers, presence of an RCD, labelling of circuits.
  2. Look at sockets and switches: any signs of heating, are they firmly fixed in the wall.
  3. Open access to the pipes under the sinks and in the bathroom: material, rust, leak marks.
  4. Tap the tiles and floor: a hollow sound and level differences point to a problem base.
  5. Check ventilation in the bathroom and kitchen and humidity in the room corners.
  6. Write down what does not suit you in the layout: a cramped kitchen, no walk-in wardrobe, an awkward bathroom.

After such an inspection the choice usually becomes obvious. If the technical points are fine and the layout suits you — cosmetic renovation. If there are problems with the services or layout — a major or mixed scope. When in doubt, invite a specialist to inspect: measurement and inspection at CAS are paid, the cost is set individually, but it is cheaper than redoing a fresh renovation.

How much do major and cosmetic renovations cost?

A major renovation at CAS Development starts from $550/m² — works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per the estimate. For a 65 m² apartment the guide figure is 65 m² × $550 = $35,750. The cost of a cosmetic renovation depends on the list of works and the surface area, so it is set by the estimate after an inspection.

At first glance a cosmetic refresh always looks cheaper. But if in a few years the wiring or pipes have to be replaced, the new renovation will start by tearing out the finishes just done, and the total will be higher than one major renovation at once. So compare not today's price but the cost over the whole time you plan to live in the apartment.

A design project for a major renovation starts from $30/m², with a minimum budget of $2,500. You can estimate a rough figure for your area in the renovation calculator; the exact number comes from the estimate after the inspection and design. We keep the estimate within ±5% if we carry out the renovation to our design.

What mistakes do people make when choosing?

  • doing a cosmetic refresh over old wiring and then chasing the new walls
  • replacing bathroom tiles without checking the waterproofing and pipes
  • deciding “major or not” without an inspection — from photos or impressions
  • planning a major renovation without a design and adding works on the go
  • saving on services for the sake of more expensive finishing materials

Questions and answers

How does a major renovation differ from a cosmetic one?

A cosmetic renovation refreshes surfaces without touching the services. A major one replaces electrics and plumbing, levels the bases and can change the layout.

How much does a major apartment renovation cost?

At CAS — from $550/m² for works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are priced separately per the estimate.

Can a cosmetic renovation be done in an old apartment?

Yes, if the services have already been replaced and are in good condition. If the wiring and pipes are original, cosmetic work will be a temporary fix.

Does a cosmetic renovation need a design project?

A full project is not required: a plan and choice of materials are often enough. A major renovation needs a project with drawings to route the services correctly.

Can major and cosmetic renovation be combined?

Yes. For example, fully redo the bathroom and electrics and only refresh the rest of the apartment. The scope of each zone is fixed in one estimate.
