Office furniture from China: how to plan the budget and lead times

CAS Development · 26 October 2026

Office furniture from China is a way to equip workstations, meeting rooms and reception to your own design rather than from whatever is in stock. We explain how to calculate the budget, what lead times to expect and how to fit delivery into the renovation schedule.

What furniture can you order for an office?

Directly from factories in China we supply everything a typical office needs: workstations, ergonomic and executive chairs, meeting tables, reception desks, acoustic phone booths and panels, lighting and decor. Items are made to order, so colour, size, material and finish can be adapted to your design and brand.

  • workstations and executive desks
  • office chairs
  • meeting tables
  • reception desks
  • acoustic booths and panels
  • light fittings and technical lighting

How are quantities and the specification set?

Because production is made to order, furniture is planned by specification rather than bought piece by piece. For an office this is natural: you usually need a series of identical chairs and workstations, plus meeting tables and reception in a matching style. We put the quantities with a margin for team growth straight into the specification, and the manager confirms the price and production details for each item once it is agreed.

Turnkey furnishing from $250/m²; custom furniture, lighting and decor from China. Phone consultation is free.

Turnkey procurement

How do you budget for office furniture from China?

  1. count the workstations: today and with a margin for growth
  2. add meeting rooms, reception, kitchen and break-out area
  3. list the items and quantities — this is the specification
  4. choose models with the manager: prices are confirmed after the specification is agreed
  5. add delivery and customs duties — they are calculated individually depending on the volume

It is convenient when the furniture specification is part of the design project: then desk sizes, sockets and network points for workstations match. If there is no design yet, turnkey procurement can help.

How do you choose office furniture models?

Office furniture works eight hours a day or more, so we look at more than appearance. A chair must adjust to the person, a desk needs room for cables and enough depth for a monitor, and a meeting table needs a cable tray for chargers and equipment. Reception needs scratch-resistant surfaces, and an open-plan office needs acoustic screens between workstations.

  • chair adjustments: height, armrests, headrest
  • cable trays and space for power supplies
  • materials that are easy to clean
  • one colour palette with the design and the brand
  • spare items for team growth

Fabric and colour samples are agreed before production starts so the furniture matches the design.

How long does furniture from China take, and how does it fit the renovation?

Delivery from China takes 35–70 days. So that the office does not stand empty after the renovation, we order the furniture as soon as the specification is approved, and delivery runs in parallel with the building works. By handover the furniture is already on site.

That is why the furniture specification is best agreed as early as possible — at the design stage. What else to allow for in the schedule we covered in the article an office in a business centre.

How do payment and the contract work?

Payment for furniture and works follows the schedule fixed in the contract. Companies registered in Ukraine can pay by invoice with or without VAT, by individual arrangement. Foreign clients can pay by invoice in $ or € (SWIFT), by card via a payment link, or in USDT; the contract can be drawn up in English. All terms are agreed individually.

How CAS works

You can order furniture on its own or together with a turnkey office renovation — then one manager handles both the building work and the delivery. We produce the design, prepare an itemised estimate and take the office from demolition to furniture. You have one manager, photo reports every 1–2 weeks, an online camera 24/7 for an extra fee and a 12-month warranty on works. The work is done by our permanent team, not subcontractors. If CAS carries out the renovation, the final total stays within ±5% of the estimate. Behind us are 150+ projects since 2016; a phone consultation is free.
