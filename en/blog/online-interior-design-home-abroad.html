Online interior design for a home abroad: Europe, UAE, USA

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

Online interior design is a perfectly workable format if you have bought a villa in the UAE, an apartment in Europe or a house in the USA and want a design team with a clear process, full drawings and one point of contact. Here is how remote work runs, what we need from you and how the project is adapted to another country.

Who the online format is for

Our studio is based in Kyiv, but our projects go well beyond it: Kyiv and the rest of Ukraine, Europe, the UAE, the USA and other countries. Clients choose the remote format when they have bought property abroad and want an interior of the same standard they would expect at home — with clear communication, technical drawings and specifications. One of our projects is a villa in Dubai, and it showed us that distance is not a problem when the process is set up properly.

  • owners of villas and apartments in the UAE
  • families who have relocated to Europe and are setting up a new home
  • buyers of houses in the USA who want a structured design process
  • investors preparing a property abroad for rental

How remote interior design works

An online project has the same stages as an in-person one: brief, space planning, concept, visualisations, working drawings and specifications. The difference is in how we communicate and collect the initial data.

  1. video call and brief: how you live, preferred style, budget range, timeline
  2. initial data: developer floor plans or a measured survey, photos and videos of the rooms
  3. space planning and concept, approved at an online presentation
  4. 3D visualisations of the key rooms
  5. working drawings and specifications for finishes, furniture and lighting
  6. support during the build: answering the contractor's questions and reviewing site photos

You receive everything digitally, and every approval is recorded in writing, so at any moment it is clear which decision is current. It works best when one person on your side makes the decisions and one project manager on ours runs the schedule, collects your comments and makes sure every change ends up in the drawings.

Communication and time zones

A time difference is not an issue once we agree on a rhythm. We usually schedule regular video meetings at a time that suits both sides and use a messenger and email in between. Every presentation is recorded or followed by a short summary: what was approved, what changes and what comes next. Decisions do not get lost, even when several family members in different countries are involved.

What we need from you

The most important thing is accurate initial data: it decides whether the furniture will actually fit. For a new build there is usually a developer plan with dimensions, window positions and services. For an older or already renovated property we need a measured survey — a local surveyor can do it using our checklist, and we check that nothing is missing. Add photos and videos of every room so we can see the real details: sill heights, wall condition, views from the windows.

  • floor plans or a measured survey with dimensions and ceiling heights
  • photos and videos of the rooms, facades and views
  • information on services: utility entry points, electrical panels, risers
  • any building or community restrictions
  • your budget range and target move-in date

Adapting the design to your country

An interior abroad is not a copy of a Kyiv apartment. Climate, building traditions, connection standards and available materials differ, and the project has to reflect that.

In the UAE the main challenges are sun and heat: solar shading, air conditioning, fade-resistant materials, terraces and shaded zones all matter. In Europe we more often deal with compact floor areas, historic buildings and the rules of management companies. In the USA there is a different unit system and its own electrical and plumbing standards, so we prepare drawings that a local contractor can read without mental conversion.

  • socket types, voltage and plumbing connection standards in your country
  • climate: sun protection, humidity, heating or cooling
  • materials and furniture that can actually be bought and delivered locally
  • building, community or management company rules for works

Specifications and procurement

For a property abroad we write the specification around what can realistically be purchased and delivered there. Each item lists dimensions, material, colour and key characteristics, so a local supplier can offer an equivalent replacement without losing the design intent. For key items — lighting, sanitary ware, fabrics — we recommend approving physical samples, either in a local showroom or sent by post.

Some furniture, lighting and decor can also be selected from our own catalogue, Ozdoba (in Ukrainian). Whether delivery to your country is possible, and on what terms, we discuss separately for each project.

What the design package includes

  • space planning with furniture layouts
  • 3D visualisations of the key rooms
  • demolition and construction plans, electrical, lighting and plumbing drawings
  • ceiling and floor plans, wall elevations and details
  • specifications for finishes, furniture, lighting and sanitary ware with dimensions

We agree the scope at the start: for a small apartment in Europe it is more compact, for a villa with a terrace and pool it is considerably larger.

Construction and design supervision

Works on site are carried out by a local contractor, and we support the build remotely: answering questions on the drawings, reviewing photos and videos of key stages, approving material substitutions. The most important check is concealed work before walls and ceilings are closed — wiring, plumbing, lighting preparation. Any deviation from the design is far easier to fix at this point than after the finishes go on.

If the property is in Ukraine — in Kyiv or the surrounding region — we can also take on the build itself: renovation, procurement and furniture installation with one manager, regular reports and a 12-month warranty on works.

If the property is meant for rental

Some clients buy property abroad as an investment and rent it out. The brief then changes: the interior has to look good in listing photos, withstand a steady flow of guests and be easy for a management company to maintain. We focus on durable materials, clear storage, good lighting and a neutral palette with a few memorable accents. Furniture and textiles are chosen so they can be replaced or reordered without redoing the whole interior.

Mistakes to avoid

  • starting works before the working drawings are finished
  • choosing furniture from photos without checking dimensions and delivery
  • ignoring climate: sun, humidity, temperature swings
  • changing decisions on site without the designer's approval

These mistakes get more expensive with distance: fixing them abroad is harder than at home. That is why we insist on a full set of drawings and written approvals.

How much online interior design costs

An online project is priced the same way as an in-person one: from $30 per m², with a minimum project fee of $2,500. For a 250 m² villa that is from $30 × 250 m² = from $7,500. We confirm the exact fee after the brief and a review of the initial data, because the scope of drawings and supervision depends on the property. More on what the project includes — on our interior design page.

How CAS works

Since 2016 we have completed more than 150 projects. Every online project has one manager, a clear stage schedule, regular presentations and payment by stages. You receive a full set of drawings and specifications that any contractor can work from, and we stay in touch until the build is finished. Invoices are issued for companies registered in Ukraine; EU invoicing is being set up. Tell us where your property is and what stage it is at — we will suggest a format and quote the design fee.

Questions and answers

Can you design my interior without visiting the property?

Yes. We need accurate floor plans or a measured survey plus photos and videos of the rooms. A local surveyor can take the measurements using our checklist.

How much does online interior design cost?

From $30 per m², with a minimum project fee of $2,500. For a 250 m² villa that is from $7,500.

Who carries out the renovation abroad?

A local contractor working from our drawings. We support the build online: we answer questions and check key stages from photos and videos.

Do you take local standards into account?

Yes. Sockets, voltage, plumbing connections, climate and locally available materials are reflected in the drawings and specifications for your specific country.
