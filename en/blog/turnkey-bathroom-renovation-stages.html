Turnkey bathroom renovation: stages, price and common mistakes

CAS Development · 22 October 2026

A turnkey bathroom renovation takes 8 stages: from measuring and checking the risers to installing sanitaryware, furniture and lights. Here is how a turnkey bathroom renovation goes, what affects the price and which mistakes to avoid.

What are the stages of a turnkey bathroom renovation?

  1. measuring, checking the risers and ventilation
  2. design: tile layout, plumbing and lighting plan
  3. removing old tiles and sanitaryware
  4. water, drainage and electrical routing
  5. waterproofing the floor and wet zones
  6. screed with falls and underfloor heating
  7. tiles, ceiling, doors
  8. installing sanitaryware, furniture and lights

What affects the price of a bathroom renovation?

  • the bathroom area and the amount of tiling
  • concealed frames, a walk-in shower without a tray, lit niches
  • moving sanitaryware and risers
  • tile format and cost
  • vanity units and mirrors

We give the exact cost of a bathroom renovation after measuring and fix it in an estimate with a ±5% guarantee if we do the renovation. If the bathroom is part of an apartment renovation, it is included in the overall price from $550/m² (works and rough materials).

What must you not economise on in a bathroom?

Do not economise on waterproofing, falls, inspection hatches and ventilation: the most expensive bathroom mistakes are hidden here. Once the tiles are on you cannot see them, and fixing them means tearing everything out. That is why we do these stages strictly by the technology and make a photo report at each one.

Why prepare the tile layout in advance?

We prepare the tile layout before work starts: joints line up with niches, concealed frames and the edges of the mirror, and cuts go into inconspicuous corners. The bathroom looks neat, and tiles are not ordered “with a double margin just in case”.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

The exact timing depends on the property: we set it in the contract after measuring. Waterproofing and screed need time to dry, so we do not rush these stages — that is what keeps the bathroom leak-free for years.

Why do ventilation and lighting matter so much?

A bathroom without proper ventilation means condensation, mouldy joints and odours. We check the draught in the shaft, fit a fan with a timer or humidity sensor, and in small bathrooms a non-return valve. Lighting is planned in several scenes — general, at the mirror and in the niches — with the right moisture protection rating.

How do you choose bathroom tiles?

  • for the floor — with an anti-slip surface
  • large format on the walls — fewer joints and easier cleaning
  • highly moisture-resistant grout in the shower zone
  • a 5–10% margin for cuts, not “twice as much just in case”

Which furniture and sanitaryware to choose?

Wall-hung vanities and concealed frames visually free up space and make cleaning easier. We put furniture sizes into the design before tiling so that water outlets and sockets end up exactly where they are needed. Some sanitaryware, furniture and lighting can be brought directly from China — this makes turnkey procurement cheaper.

Why do the bathroom with the apartment design?

When the bathroom is part of the apartment design project, all decisions are coordinated: the tiles match the hallway floor, the lighting matches the other scenes, and the crew works from drawings without rework. A design project starts from $30/m², with a minimum budget of $2,500.

What mistakes are made in bathroom renovation?

  • laying tiles without a layout and ending up with cuts in plain sight
  • forgetting inspection hatches to meters and valves
  • installing a shower without a fall or with the drain in the wrong place
  • choosing mixers after the walls are closed

We fix all these decisions in the design before work starts so that the crew does not decide them on the go. You see in advance how the bathroom will look and know the exact estimate.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey bathroom renovation cost?

It depends on the area, tiles and sanitaryware. We name the sum after measuring and fix it in an estimate with a ±5% guarantee if we do the renovation.

Do you renovate only a bathroom without the rest of the apartment?

Yes, we take on bathrooms and WCs separately.

Can you make a shower without a tray?

Yes, if the floor height and the fall to the drain allow it; we check this when measuring.
