Turnkey landscape design: the stages of developing a plot

CAS Development · 18 November 2026

Turnkey landscape design means one team takes the plot from the survey and design all the way to the lawn, lighting and a set-up irrigation system. We explain the stages of the work, why the order of works matters more than the choice of plants and how the cost is calculated.

What does turnkey landscape design mean?

Turnkey means design and delivery in one pair of hands. You approve the site plan and estimate, and from then on you do not have to find separate diggers, drainage installers, irrigation fitters, an electrician and a gardener. One team is responsible for the interfaces between these jobs, and if something does not fit, the issue is not passed from contractor to contractor.

  • site inspection, topographic survey and design
  • an estimate listing works and materials
  • grading, drainage and water management
  • automatic irrigation, cables and light fittings
  • path paving, terrace, edging
  • topsoil, lawn, trees, shrubs and flowerbeds
  • setting up irrigation and lighting, handing over the plot

We wrote in detail about where to start and what the design itself includes in the article landscape design for your plot: where to start. Here we cover what happens after the design.

Stage 1. Where does landscaping a plot start?

Everything starts with a site visit. We look at the terrain, the soil, existing trees, where water collects and how shade falls from the house and neighbouring buildings. Our surveyor records boundaries, levels and utilities, and for large plots we do aerial photography. On this basis we prepare the site plan, zoning, grading plan, planting plan and service layouts.

We agree the design with you before work starts: where the terrace will be, how the paths will run, which plants go where and where the lighting goes. Changing a line on a drawing costs nothing; redoing finished paving is expensive.

Tell us about your property and we will give you a rough cost. Phone consultation is free.

Stage 2. Why are earthworks and grading needed?

The plot is cleared, the topsoil is stripped and stored if it is usable. Then the terrain is shaped: falls away from the house, level terraces, positions for retaining walls. This stage determines where rainwater will go, so it is not skipped even on a plot that looks flat.

Stage 3. What goes underground before the paving?

Drainage, irrigation pipes, lighting cables and ducts for future services are laid before paving and lawn. This is the main rule of landscaping: the underground part is done once and properly, because afterwards you cannot reach it without lifting the paths.

  • drainage trenches and chambers according to the grading plan
  • irrigation mains and zones, a place for the controller
  • cables to light fittings, outdoor sockets and gates in protective ducts
  • spare ducts under paths — in case another line is needed later

You can see what this looks like step by step in the “Landscape from scratch” animation on the landscape design page: switch on X-ray mode and drainage, irrigation and cables appear under the lawn.

Stage 4. When are paving, terraces and structures built?

Once the services are in the ground, the base for paths and hard areas is built, edging is set, slabs or stone are laid, and the terrace, retaining walls, pergolas and barbecue area are built. Edging is not just for looks: it holds the edge of the lawn and stops the paving from spreading.

Stage 5. When do the lawn and plants go in?

Plants are the last to arrive. Topsoil is brought in and levelled, trees and large shrubs are planted, then flowerbeds and edging plants, and the lawn goes in at the very end so it is not trampled during the works. After planting, irrigation is set up by zone: lawn, shrubs and trees need different amounts of water.

We hand over the plot with irrigation and lighting set up, a services plan and care recommendations for the first season.

How much does turnkey landscape design cost?

A landscape design project at CAS Development starts from $50 per 100 m². For a 1 500 m² plot the design costs from 15 × $50 = $750. Implementation is costed separately in the estimate once the design is approved: the cost depends on the area, terrain, the amount of paving, the services and the plants.

The estimate is split into stages, so the work can be done in phases: first everything underground and the paving by the house, then the rest of the plot. The payment schedule is tailored to the client and the terms are fixed in the contract.

How CAS works

Our own team takes the plot from survey to handover: design, estimate, earthworks, services, paving and planting. If the house is still being built, we plan the landscape together with the house construction so the garden does not have to be dug up again. You have one manager, photo reports every 1–2 weeks and a 12-month warranty on works. We work in Kyiv and the region, in all regions of Ukraine and in Europe.

Questions and answers

What does turnkey landscape design include?

Inspection and survey, design, estimate, earthworks, drainage, automatic irrigation, lighting, paving, lawn and planting, and at the end setting up irrigation and lighting.

How much does turnkey landscape design cost?

The design starts from $50 per 100 m². Implementation is costed in the estimate after the design is approved, as it depends on the area, terrain and materials.

Can a plot be developed in phases?

Yes. The estimate is split into stages: first the underground services and paving by the house, then the rest of the plot and planting.

Do you give a warranty on the works?

Yes, the warranty on works is 12 months; the terms are fixed in the contract.
