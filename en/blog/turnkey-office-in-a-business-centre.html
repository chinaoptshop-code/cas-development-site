A turnkey office in a business centre: approvals, building services and furniture from China

CAS Development · 27 October 2026

A turnkey office in a business centre is not just design and renovation: there is a landlord, a management company and building rules. Here is what to check before you start, how services are agreed and how to furnish the office with furniture and lighting from China.

What should you check with the landlord and management company?

In a business centre the renovation does not depend on you alone: the building has its own rules for works. So the first questions for the landlord and management company should be not about wall colours but about the conditions you will work under. It is better to get them in writing before the design project is signed off.

  • the building's rules for renovation works and when noisy works are allowed
  • site access: passes for the crew, entry hours, parking for delivery vehicles
  • which lifts can be used for materials and furniture and whether booking is needed
  • where to store construction waste and how to remove it
  • whether the landlord's consent is needed to change the layout, partitions and service points
  • what restrictions apply to the entrance, doors and common corridors

The answers affect the layout, the budget and the schedule. That is why we collect them at the start and build them into the design rather than finding out on site.

How do building services approvals usually go?

In an office, most depends on the services: ventilation, air conditioning, electrics, lighting, low-voltage networks. In a business centre the building already has some of these systems, and connecting to them usually has to be agreed with the management company.

In general terms, the order is: we survey the premises, record the existing connection points, build the solutions into the design and submit it to the landlord or management company for approval. Which documents and permits are needed depends on the building and the scope of changes, so we draw up a checklist for the specific business centre. The approval outcome depends on the landlord's decision, and nobody can guarantee it in advance.

What to discuss at this stage:

  • power supply capacity and a place for the distribution board
  • ventilation and air conditioning: whether outdoor units are allowed and where to run the lines
  • a server room or technical cabinet and its cooling
  • fire alarms and emergency exit lighting
  • acoustics of meeting rooms and the open space

Why keep design, renovation and procurement in one team?

When design, construction and furniture are handled by different contractors, things go wrong at the interfaces: sockets are not where the desks stand, the lighting does not match the plan. That is why we take the office from start to finish. A design project at CAS starts from $30/m² (minimum project $2,500), office renovation from $350/m² and includes works and rough materials.

As a guide: a 200 m² office is from $70,000 for the renovation (200 × $350) and from $6,000 for the design project (200 × $30). These are starting rates; the exact sum comes from the estimate after measuring and design. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are separate, per estimate. If CAS carries out the renovation, the total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%.

Our own team works on site — in-house staff for different levels of complexity. You follow progress through photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and payment can be split as 50/50, 50/30/20 or 50/40/10, starting from a 10% advance. More about the renovation on the turnkey office renovation in Kyiv page.

Furniture and lighting from China in batches

An office needs dozens of identical items: chairs, desks, shelving, light fittings. For such volumes it is convenient to order in batches directly from factories in China. Made-to-order production is possible: colour, size, material, finish.

  • delivery from China takes 35–70 days, so it is worth ordering right after the design is approved
  • delivery and customs costs are not fixed; they are calculated individually depending on the order volume
  • we put furniture sizes into the drawings so that sockets and network points are where the workstations will stand

Also check how furniture and lighting will be brought into the building: dimensions affect the choice of lift and the delivery time, and that is again a question for the management company.

How do you accept the office after the works?

At the end of the works you accept the office together with the manager. Check not only how it looks but also how the systems work.

  1. compare what has been done with the design and estimate item by item
  2. check all sockets, network points and switches
  3. switch on the lighting in all zones and check there is no glare on monitors
  4. test ventilation and air conditioning in working modes
  5. inspect doors, hardware and furniture for chips and scratches
  6. record remarks in the certificate and agree when they will be fixed

The works carry a 12-month warranty. Report anything discovered after moving in to the manager: we will record the problem and fix it under the warranty.

Questions and answers

What should be clarified with a business centre before an office renovation?

The rules for works, access for the crew and materials, use of lifts and the requirements for approving layout and service changes. It is better to get this in writing before the design starts.

Do you help with approving building services?

Yes, we build the solutions into the design and prepare them for approval by the landlord or management company. The outcome depends on the building's decision, so we do not promise legal guarantees.
