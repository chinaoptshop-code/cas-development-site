Furnishing an apartment for rent: checklist and budget

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

Turnkey furnishing of a rental apartment with CAS costs from $250/m², and what matters is the right set rather than expensive furniture: everything needed for daily life, nothing superfluous, easy to maintain and photogenic in the listing. Here is a room-by-room checklist and how to calculate the budget.

How does the rental format affect furnishing?

The rental format determines the set, the materials and the budget, so before writing a furniture list, decide who will rent the apartment. A long-term let for a couple or family, short stays for tourists and business travellers, and corporate housing for a company's staff are all different usage scenarios.

  • long-term let: more storage, a workspace, a full kitchen, a neutral style
  • short-term let: comfortable beds, hotel-grade textiles, minimal decor that is easy to clean around
  • corporate housing: workspaces, sturdy furniture, simple cleaning-friendly solutions

Tenants choose an apartment from photos, so the interior has to look good in the listing. But they live in it every day, so furniture must be comfortable and durable. A calm base works best — light walls, warm wood tones, plain fabrics — plus a few accents that stand out in the photos.

What furniture do the kitchen and living room need?

In the kitchen and living room, invest in the sofa, the dining set and the lighting: they are the hero shots of a listing and get used every day.

  • a sofa or sofa bed with a removable, washable cover
  • a coffee table and a TV unit
  • a dining table and chairs to match the number of beds
  • kitchen appliances: fridge, hob, oven or microwave, cooker hood, kettle
  • crockery, cutlery, pots and pans for the number of guests
  • curtains or blinds, a rug, cushions
  • general lighting plus a floor or table lamp for the evening

What does a rental bedroom need?

The bedroom should be simple, but with no compromise on the bed and mattress — tenants mention them in reviews more than anything else. One good bed is better than several places to sleep that are uncomfortable.

  • a bed with a quality mattress and mattress protector
  • two bedside tables and lighting on both sides of the bed
  • a wardrobe or open hanging system with hangers
  • blackout curtains
  • two sets of bed linen, duvets and pillows
  • a bedspread and a throw
  • a full-length mirror

What do the entrance hall and bathroom need?

  • a wardrobe or coat rack, a shoe shelf, a bench
  • a mirror and lighting by the entrance
  • in the bathroom: a backlit mirror, shelves or a vanity unit, hooks, a towel holder
  • towels: two sets per guest
  • washing machine, drying rack, iron and ironing board
  • a vacuum cleaner and basic cleaning kit

Small things like hooks, shelves and a mirror are often forgotten, yet they are what makes an apartment convenient. A tenant who has somewhere to hang a towel and put their shoes treats the rest of the space with more care.

Which materials survive renting?

High-durability fabrics and furniture with removable covers hold up best: in a rental apartment furniture works harder than in your own home. So what matters is not only how it looks but how the material will survive several tenants.

  • high-durability fabrics and removable sofa covers
  • worktops and surfaces resistant to moisture, scratches and stains
  • furniture on sturdy frames without fragile details
  • identical sets of textiles and crockery that are easy to reorder
  • neutral colours that show less wear

Light fabrics are fine if they have a protective finish or removable covers. For short-term lets, hotel textiles are convenient — white towels and linen that are easy to wash and replace.

How much does it cost to furnish a rental apartment?

Turnkey furnishing of an apartment with us costs from $250/m². For a 40 m² apartment that is from 40 × $250 = $10,000, for a 60 m² apartment from 60 × $250 = $15,000. This covers selection, specification, ordering, delivery, assembly and placement; the exact estimate depends on the rental format and the items chosen.

Some furniture, lighting and textiles are worth sourcing directly from factories in China. Delivery takes 35–70 days, so if the apartment is still being renovated, order in parallel with the works. How to order furniture and lighting from China without risk is covered in a separate article.

A tip for owners: do not economise on the mattress, lighting and storage — these are what reviews mention most. Decor can be added gradually, once you see how the apartment performs as a rental. Where saving makes sense and where it does not — see how not to overpay for a luxury interior.

In what order should you furnish a rental apartment?

First a furniture plan with dimensions, then the specification, and only then the order: in this sequence furnishing is faster and cheaper. That way furniture is guaranteed to fit its place and the sofa will go through the door and into the lift.

  1. measuring the apartment and photographing its current state
  2. furniture and lighting plan with dimensions
  3. specification: every item with product code, colour and price
  4. budget approval and ordering — from China and locally
  5. delivery, assembly and placement
  6. styling, a photo shoot and handing over the keys

If renovation is still under way, make the furniture plan before finishing: sockets, the TV outlet and bedside lighting then end up exactly where the furniture will stand. Moving a socket after wallpapering is an avoidable extra cost.

How do you prepare the apartment for listing photos?

Once the furniture is in place, styling remains: a made bed, cushions, a throw, a few books, plants, a laid table. These are inexpensive details, but they turn a furnished apartment into one people want to live in. After styling comes a photo shoot in daylight with the lights on.
