Luxury apartment renovation: what a premium budget consists of

CAS Development · 31 October 2026

A luxury apartment renovation in Kyiv is not just expensive materials but complex details, building services and control of every concealed stage. We explain what parts a premium renovation budget consists of, what the rate from $550/m² includes and what is costed separately.

How a premium renovation differs from a standard one

In a premium interior the cost of a mistake is high. A natural stone slab cannot be redone, concealed doors need perfect wall geometry, and lighting in niches has to be planned at the rough-works stage. That is why such a renovation is carried out only from working drawings, and every concealed stage — electrics, plumbing, waterproofing — is accepted before it is closed.

Services are a separate topic. In a large apartment or penthouse this means heat-recovery ventilation, several air-conditioning zones, a smart home, water treatment and underfloor heating. These systems are designed together with the interior so that technical cabinets and inspection hatches do not end up in the middle of the living room.

What a luxury apartment renovation budget consists of

It is convenient to divide the budget into several independent parts — that way you see what exactly you are paying for and where there is room to manoeuvre:

  • design project — from $30/m², minimum $2,500; the full package with designer supervision — from $45/m²;
  • renovation — from $550/m²: works and rough materials;
  • finishing materials — separately, per the design specification and estimate;
  • furniture and appliances — separately, per estimate; furnishing from $250/m²;
  • optional extras of your choice — for example, an online camera on site 24/7.

In the premium segment it is the finishing materials, furniture and appliances that affect the total most. So the rate per metre should be seen as the lower limit for the works, not as the price of a “turnkey apartment”.

What the rate from $550/m² includes

The rate from $550/m² covers works and rough materials: demolition and installation of partitions, building services, levelling walls and floors, preparation for finishing and the finishing works themselves. Finishing materials — stone, tiles, parquet, doors, sanitaryware, light fittings — are not included, because in a premium interior their cost depends on the specific items in the specification.

As a guide: a 150 m² apartment at $550/m² is from 150 × $550 = $82,500 for works and rough materials. This is a calculation at the base rate, not an estimate: the exact sum comes from the estimate after paid measuring and working drawings. More on the premium renovation page.

How to keep the budget within the estimate

The main risk of an expensive renovation is not the rate but the decisions made on the go. To keep the total from “drifting”, it is important to:

  1. start with a design project and working drawings — without them the estimate will be approximate;
  2. approve the specification of finishing materials before work starts;
  3. fix the estimate, schedule and payment scheme in the contract;
  4. accept each stage with a certificate, and concealed works before they are closed.

If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total does not deviate from the approved estimate by more than ±5%. This condition applies only when CAS carries out the renovation and is written into the contract.

Who does the works

The renovation is carried out by CAS's own team — in-house staff for different levels of complexity and types of work. Kitchens and built-in wardrobes are made by our own staff and partners. If our studio did the design, designer supervision checks the work against the drawings; if another contractor is doing the renovation, the terms and cost of designer and technical supervision are discussed individually.

Control, reports and an online camera

Owners of luxury apartments often have no time to visit the site, so reporting must be regular and clear. We send photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and each stage is handed over with a certificate — you see what has been done and what you are paying for. For an extra fee an online camera can be installed on site 24/7 so you can watch the work at any moment.

Contract, payment and warranty

The scope of works, estimate, timing, payment scheme and warranty are fixed in the contract and its annexes. Timing starts from 2 weeks depending on scope. Payment can be split as 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10 or by stages, starting from a 10% advance. The warranty on completed works is 12 months. More: contract, warranty and payment.
