What a turnkey renovation includes: the full list of works

CAS Development · 24 October 2026

A turnkey renovation in Kyiv means one contractor takes you from the design to a finished apartment. We go through the full list of works: what exactly such a renovation includes and where the contractor's responsibility ends.

What does a turnkey renovation mean?

A turnkey renovation in Kyiv is a format in which one renovation company takes on the whole path from measurements to handing over a finished apartment. You do not look for electricians, tilers and painters separately, do not drive around shops with a list of materials and do not coordinate the work of different crews. Below is the full list of what such a renovation usually includes.

A turnkey renovation at CAS Development costs from $550/m² (works and rough materials). This is a guide: the exact sum comes from the estimate after the site visit and design approval.

Where does a turnkey renovation start?

Work starts not with demolition but with documents. Without them a renovation becomes a series of improvisations that you end up paying for twice.

  • measurements of the premises and a space plan
  • a design project: layout, visualisations, selection of materials
  • working drawings: positions of sockets, lights, plumbing, underfloor heating
  • an estimate broken down into works and materials
  • a contract with the order of stages and payments

A design project can be ordered separately: from $30/m², with a minimum price of $2,500.

Turnkey renovation

What do demolition and preparation include?

At the first stage everything that will not stay in the new interior is removed. The premises are then prepared for rough works.

  • removal of old finishes, partitions, sanitaryware and doors
  • removal of construction waste
  • building new partitions according to the plan
  • checking walls and floor, protecting what stays

Why are building services installed before finishing?

Services are laid before finishing, because afterwards any change means opening up finished walls and floors.

  • electrics: cable routing, the distribution board, sockets and switches, lighting
  • water supply and drainage
  • heating and, as per the design, underfloor heating
  • ventilation and air conditioning
  • low-voltage networks: internet, TV, intercom

What do rough works include?

This is preparing the base for finishing. Its quality determines how the finishes will look.

  • plastering or levelling the walls
  • floor screed, waterproofing of wet zones
  • skimming and priming
  • installing windows and preparing door openings, if the design provides for it

What do finishing works include?

Here the interior takes on the look set out in the design project. All materials come from the approved list.

  • tiling in the bathroom and kitchen
  • floor covering: parquet, laminate, tiles or another option per the design
  • painting or decorative wall finishes, wallpaper
  • installing interior doors and skirting
  • fitting sanitaryware, light fittings, sockets and switches

When should furniture and procurement be planned?

If you need turnkey furnishing, it is worth planning it together with the renovation: the sizes of the kitchen, wardrobes and appliances affect where the service points go. Furnishing as a separate service starts from $250/m². It covers selecting and ordering furniture, textiles and decor, and installing them.

How does the handover work?

The last stage is checking and handing over the apartment.

  • final cleaning after construction work
  • checking the electrics, plumbing and heating
  • inspecting the property together with you and fixing any remarks
  • handing over documents and a 12-month warranty

How much does a turnkey renovation of a 60 m² apartment cost?

For a 60 m² apartment, a turnkey renovation from $550/m² gives roughly 60 × $550 = $33,000. Furnishing the same area from $250/m² is 60 × $250 = $15,000. This is a calculation at base rates, not an offer: the final sum is set by the estimate depending on the materials and complexity of the project.

How it is organised at CAS Development

We work to a clear scheme: first the design and estimate, then work to the approved plan. One manager is responsible for your property and stays in touch throughout the renovation. You get progress reports, and after handover a 12-month warranty. Answers to frequently asked questions about prices, timing and the warranty are collected separately. If you want to understand which items on this list you actually need, leave a request — we will clarify the details and estimate the approximate cost for your apartment.

Is furniture included in a turnkey renovation?
