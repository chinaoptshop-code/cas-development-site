A renovation estimate: how to read it and what to check

CAS Development · 31 October 2026

An apartment renovation estimate is the main document that shows what exactly you are paying for. We explain which sections it consists of, how to read the items, what to look at before signing the contract and how to link the estimate to stage payments.

Why do you need a renovation estimate?

An estimate breaks the renovation sum down into specific works and materials with quantities and prices, because the phrase “an apartment renovation for so much” says nothing about what will be done. It lets you compare offers from different contractors, see where a margin has been built in and know in advance what you will have to pay for separately.

A good estimate is drawn up from a design project or at least from a plan with dimensions. Without drawings, some quantities will have to be clarified during the works, and the total will be less accurate.

What does an apartment renovation estimate consist of?

An estimate is usually divided into sections by type of work or by room. Each section has items, and each item has the same set of fields:

  • the name of the work or material — specific, not “finishing works in general”;
  • the unit of measurement — m², linear metre, piece, point;
  • the quantity — how many of these units your property has;
  • the unit price and the total for the item;
  • a note if the item depends on a decision that has not yet been made.

Works and materials should be separated, and among the materials, rough and finishing ones. That way you can immediately see which part of the budget depends on your choice of tiles, parquet or sanitaryware.

How do you read the items and what should you check?

  1. Compare quantities with the plan: the area of walls, floors and ceilings should match the drawings, and the number of sockets and lights the electrical plan.
  2. Look for vague lines such as “other works” or “additional materials” without a quantity — ask for them to be itemised.
  3. Check that demolition, waste removal, carrying materials upstairs and cleaning are included — otherwise they will appear later.
  4. See which items are marked as approximate and ask what their final price depends on.
  5. Clarify what the estimate does not include: finishing materials, furniture, appliances.

What does the renovation rate include and what is extra?

At CAS Development an apartment renovation starts from $550/m²: that is works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are calculated separately, per estimate. For an 80 m² apartment the base-rate calculation is from 80 × $550 = $44,000 for works and rough materials; the exact sum comes from the estimate. Should the estimate be part of the contract?

Yes, the estimate should be an annex to the construction contract, not a separate message in a messenger. Together with it the contract sets the scope of works, timing, payment scheme and warranty. Work timing starts from 2 weeks, depending on the scope.

If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%. This condition is written into the contract. More about the contract on the contract, warranty and payment page.

How do you link the estimate to payments?

It is most convenient when each payment is tied to a stage accepted with a certificate of completed works. Then you see what you are paying for, and the estimate works as a checklist. At CAS we choose the payment scheme to suit the client:

  • 50/50 — half at the start, the rest after handover and the final certificate;
  • 50/30/20 — start, an interim stage and the final certificate;
  • 50/40/10 — the last 10% after the final certificate is signed;
  • stage by stage — you pay for each stage separately, starting from a 10% advance.

The size of the advance and the link between each payment and its stage certificate are fixed in the contract.

How do you compare estimates from different contractors?

Compare identical items rather than the totals. Put the estimates into one table by section and see where one contractor has works that the other does not. A cheaper estimate often simply leaves out part of the works or assumes cheaper rough materials. If the quantities differ noticeably between estimates, ask each contractor to explain how they calculated them — this quickly shows who studied the drawings carefully.

Questions and answers

What should an apartment renovation estimate contain?

Sections by type of work or room, items with a unit, quantity, price and total, and works, rough materials and finishing materials listed separately.

Are finishing materials included in the rate from $550/m²?

No. The rate from $550/m² covers works and rough materials. Finishing materials, furniture and appliances are calculated separately, per estimate.

How much can the total differ from the estimate?

If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%.

How do you pay for a renovation under an estimate?

On a 50/50, 50/30/20 or 50/40/10 scheme or stage by stage; the start is from a 10% advance. Each payment is tied to a stage certificate in the contract.
