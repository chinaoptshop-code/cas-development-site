Home staging: how to prepare an apartment for sale or rent

CAS Development · 16 November 2026

A buyer or tenant decides whether to call within seconds of looking at the photos. Home staging means preparing an apartment for sale or rent so it looks cared for and easy to read in photos and at viewings. Here is where to start, what to fix, which furniture and textiles to add and when it is simpler to order turnkey furnishing.

What is home staging and why do you need it?

Home staging is preparing a home for viewings: removing clutter, fixing small defects, arranging furniture so it is clear how to live in each room, and adding light and textiles. The aim is for people to see the space rather than someone else's belongings or unfinished details.

For a sale, it helps present the apartment neutrally so that the buyer can picture themselves in it. For a rental, it means furnishing the apartment so that it photographs well and stands up to daily use. The approaches are similar, but renting puts more weight on practicality and durability.

Step 1: what should you clear before viewings?

The first and cheapest step is to remove personal items, surplus furniture and things on surfaces. Empty worktops, clear walkways and a minimum of items on shelves make a room look bigger. Seasonal clothes, boxes and appliances nobody uses are best taken away for a while.

  • remove photos, magnets, small decor and things from windowsills
  • leave free space in wardrobes — buyers will open them
  • clean the windows, tiles, sanitaryware and light fittings
  • deal with odours: air the rooms, clean textiles, the kitchen and bathroom

Step 2: which minor repairs are worth doing?

Scratches, marks on walls, cracked tiles, a dripping tap, doors that do not close — all of this is noticed at viewings and factored into haggling. Often it is enough to refresh the walls in a neutral light colour, replace worn sockets and switches, fix the hardware and renew the silicone in the bathroom.

If the apartment needs more — new flooring, sanitaryware or doors — it is worth costing a cosmetic renovation. A major renovation before a sale is usually not needed: buyers often want to do it their own way.

Step 3: how should you arrange the furniture?

Each room should have a clear function. An empty room looks smaller in photos than it is, and a room crammed with old furniture looks more cramped. The minimum set: a sofa and coffee table in the living room, a bed with bedside tables in the bedroom, a dining table with chairs. Furniture is placed so that walkways stay clear and the windows are visible.

For a rental, furniture should be not only attractive but durable: removable covers, easy-clean fabrics, scratch-resistant worktops. What exactly to buy and in which order is covered in our checklist for furnishing a rental apartment.

Step 4: what do light, textiles and decor add?

Light does more than you might think. Add a floor or table lamp to dark corners, replace cold bulbs with warm ones and make sure every room has working fittings. Photos are best taken in daylight with the lights on.

  • a rug in the living room and by the bed — the space feels warmer
  • cushions and a throw in neutral tones
  • plain bed linen and towels in the bathroom
  • a mirror to bring more light into a hallway or bedroom
  • a few live plants instead of small decor

What should you check before photos and viewings?

  • personal items removed, surfaces clear
  • walls refreshed, small defects fixed
  • all lights work, bulbs are the same warm tone
  • every room has a clear function
  • textiles are clean, the bed is made
  • windows are clean, curtains open
  • the bathroom and kitchen sparkle, towels match

Questions and answers

How much does home staging an apartment cost?

Decluttering and small decor can be done on your own. Turnkey furnishing at CAS starts from $250/m²; for a 60 m² apartment, from $15,000. The exact sum comes from the estimate.

Is it worth renovating before a sale?

A cosmetic refresh is usually enough: repaint the walls, fix the plumbing and hardware, replace worn sockets. Buyers often want to do a major renovation their own way.

How does staging for rent differ from staging for sale?

For a sale the apartment is shown neutrally so the buyer can picture themselves in it. For a rental, practical and durable furniture and textiles matter more, because they will be used every day.

Can I order furniture from China for one apartment?

We order furniture from China in batches, so it pays off when you furnish several apartments at once. For a single apartment we select furniture within turnkey furnishing.
