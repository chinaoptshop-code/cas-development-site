How much a new-build apartment renovation costs in Kyiv in 2026

CAS Development · 21 October 2026

How much does a new-build renovation cost in Kyiv?

At CAS Development a turnkey renovation of a new-build apartment starts from $550/m² (works and rough materials). We fix the exact sum in the estimate after inspecting the apartment and the design project; if we do the renovation, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate. Guide figures by area — simply area times the rate:

  • one-bedroom 45 m² — from $24,750
  • two-bedroom 60 m² — from $33,000
  • three-bedroom 90 m² — from $49,500

A design project starts from $30/m², with a minimum project budget of $2,500: for a small apartment it is the minimum that applies. Built-in furniture and turnkey procurement are costed separately (furnishing from $250/m²): part can be brought directly from China, which is noticeably cheaper than retail.

What does a new-build renovation estimate consist of?

An estimate is not one figure but a list of works and materials with quantities. It is easiest to read it by blocks:

  • rough works: screed, plaster, partitions, plasterboard
  • services: electrics, plumbing, underfloor heating, air conditioning
  • finishing works: tiles, flooring, painting, ceilings, doors
  • materials: rough and finishing
  • furniture, lighting, appliances

When comparing offers, check that all blocks are included and that quantities are stated in metres rather than as a “package”. The cheapest estimate often simply leaves out part of the works, which then appear during the renovation as “extras”.

What affects the renovation price most?

  • the developer's handover state: bare concrete, screed or “white shell”
  • replanning and moving wet zones
  • the number of bathrooms and the tiled area
  • the level of materials and appliances
  • built-in furniture
  • complex ceilings, concealed doors, non-standard lighting

The budget grows most not because of expensive materials but because of changes mid-renovation. That is why all key decisions should be made at the design stage. Moving a socket on a drawing costs nothing; after plastering it means a new chase, new skim and new paint.

Which is better value: bare concrete or a “white shell”?

A “white shell” seems more economical, but the developer's plaster, screed and wiring often have to be redone for your design. Before starting, check the evenness of walls and floors and the quality of the wiring — sometimes it is cheaper to redo than to adapt. We decide after the inspection: if the base is good and fits the design, we keep it, and that genuinely saves budget.

What to check in an apartment from the developer:

  • the geometry of walls and corners with a straightedge and level
  • the screed: cracks, hollows, height differences
  • the position of risers and ventilation ducts
  • windows and sills: tight fit, reveals
  • the distribution board and incoming cable

How long does a new-build renovation take?

It depends on the area and scope of works. The longest stages are screed and plaster, which need time to dry, and the manufacture of built-in furniture. We set the timing in the contract with a stage schedule after the estimate.

Also allow for the specifics of a new building: while neighbours are renovating too, there may be restrictions on noisy works and use of the lift. Clarify this with the building manager before you start, along with the procedure for removing construction waste and connecting permanent power.

What mistakes do new-build owners make?

  • starting the renovation without a design to “move in sooner”
  • keeping the developer's wiring without checking it against your furniture and appliances
  • saving on waterproofing in the bathrooms and kitchen
  • ordering the kitchen and wardrobes only after the renovation is finished
  • not reserving budget for furniture, lighting and appliances

What should you do before you get the keys?

The period between buying the apartment and receiving the keys can be put to good use. Then the renovation starts straight away rather than after months of deliberation.

  1. get the apartment plan with dimensions and riser positions from the developer
  2. decide on the style, number of bedrooms and storage
  3. order the design project and an estimate based on the plan
  4. make a list of appliances and sanitaryware so the services can be prepared for them
  5. find out the building's rules for renovation works
  6. after receiving the keys — inspect the apartment and refine the estimate

How do you keep the renovation on budget?

  • start with a design project and a detailed estimate
  • agree changes in writing before they are made
  • check concealed works before they are closed: waterproofing, electrics, pipes
  • order furniture in parallel with the renovation, not after it
  • prepare the design and estimate before you receive the keys

Questions and answers

How much does it cost to renovate a one-bedroom new-build apartment?

It is calculated by area: a turnkey renovation at CAS starts from $550/m², so for 45 m² it is from $24,750. We name the exact sum after the inspection and design.

Can the renovation be prepared before the building is completed?

The design project and estimate can be prepared from the developer's apartment plan, so works start right after you receive the keys.

Which is cheaper: bare concrete or a white shell?

On paper, the white shell, but it is often redone. We assess the condition during the inspection and advise what is more economical in your apartment.

Is furniture included in the price of a turnkey renovation?

No, furniture and procurement are costed separately: furnishing from $250/m². They can be ordered together with the renovation so you move into a finished apartment.
