Turnkey apartment renovation stages: the order of works from design to cleaning

CAS Development · 21 October 2026

The stages of a turnkey apartment renovation follow a strict sequence: each step relies on the previous one. We go through the order of works, what to check at every step and which mistakes most often force rework.

What are the stages of a turnkey apartment renovation?

A turnkey renovation is not a set of separate jobs but a chain in which the order decides everything. Swap the stages and you will have to chase finished walls, re-lay tiles or remake furniture. The overall scheme looks like this:

  1. design and estimate
  2. demolition and preparation
  3. building services
  4. rough works
  5. finishing works
  6. furniture and procurement
  7. cleaning, system checks and handover

What should you prepare before the start?

Before the crew arrives, a few organisational questions are worth settling — you will find some of the answers in our FAQ. This saves time and nerves at every following stage.

  • an approved design project with working drawings
  • an estimate broken down into works and materials
  • a contract with a stage schedule and payment order
  • an agreement with the building management about noisy works and waste removal
  • a list of appliances and sanitaryware with dimensions — the services are prepared for them

What do the preparatory stages, design and demolition, involve?

1. Design and estimate

Without a design project and an estimate, a renovation turns into a series of improvisations. The drawings define where the walls, sockets, lights and plumbing will be, and the estimate defines how much it will cost. This is the stage where the decisions that are most expensive to change later are made: replanning, the location of wet zones, lighting scenes, places for appliances.

A design project at CAS Development starts from $30/m², with a minimum budget of $2,500. A turnkey renovation starts from $550/m²: for a 70 m² apartment that is roughly from $38,500. The exact sum comes from the estimate after the site visit and design.

2. Demolition and preparation

Removing partitions according to the design, stripping old finishes, taking out waste, protecting windows and the entrance door. Hidden problems often surface at this stage — old wiring, a weak floor base, cracks. They are best solved straight away while the walls are open, not after finishing. If a discovery affects the estimate, the changes are recorded in writing before work continues.

What do building services and rough works involve?

3. Building services

Services are installed before any finishing because afterwards everything is hidden in the walls, floor and ceiling.

  • electrics: chases, cables, back boxes, the distribution board
  • plumbing: water and drainage routing, concealed frames
  • ventilation and air conditioning: ducts before finishing
  • underfloor heating, low-voltage networks, internet — as per the design

Before closing up, check the installed routes and photograph them with a tape measure: this will come in handy when you want to hang a shelf or a picture.

4. Rough works

Partitions, plastering, screed, waterproofing of wet zones. The quality of the geometry here determines how tiles and flooring will lie and how built-in furniture will fit. Screed and plaster need time to dry — do not rush into finishing, or the coverings may deform. Before moving on, check the walls and floor with a straightedge: this is when deviations are easiest to fix.

What do finishing, furniture and handover involve?

5. Finishing works

Tiles, floors, ceilings, painting, installing doors, sanitaryware and light fittings. The order matters: dirty work first, then the work that fears dust. That is why painting and fitting lights usually come towards the end, and the floor covering is protected while other work continues.

6. Furniture and procurement

Kitchen, wardrobes, upholstered furniture, lighting, decor. Built-in furniture should be measured after the finishing works, when walls and floor are in their final geometry. Part of the turnkey procurement can be brought directly from China — it is cheaper, but delivery time has to be planned in. Furnishing at CAS starts from $250/m².

7. Cleaning and handover

A deep clean, a check of all systems, the acceptance certificate. Do not sign the certificate in a hurry: it is better to inspect every room carefully once than to look for someone to fix small things later. During acceptance, walk through the apartment with a checklist:

  • all sockets and switches work, the breakers in the board are labelled
  • taps, mixers and drains do not leak, water drains away quickly
  • doors and fronts open easily and do not catch
  • tiles have no hollows or chips, joints are even
  • you have as-built documentation: electrical and plumbing diagrams

What mistakes are made during a renovation?

  • starting a renovation without a design and “deciding on site”
  • moving sockets and plumbing after plastering
  • measuring furniture before finishing works are complete
  • closing concealed works without checks and photos
  • agreeing changes verbally rather than in writing

How do you keep control of an apartment renovation?

Ask for photo reports after each stage, check concealed works before they are closed (electrics, plumbing, waterproofing) and compare with the drawings. At CAS Development we first do the design and a detailed estimate, then one manager runs the renovation, and the client gets photo reports every 1–2 weeks. The estimate is guaranteed to within ±5% if we carry out the renovation to our design. We give a 12-month warranty on the works; we have completed 150+ projects since 2016.

Questions and answers

How long does a turnkey apartment renovation take?

It depends on the area and complexity; we draw up the exact stage schedule together with the estimate and put it in the contract (from 2 weeks).

Can you live in the apartment during the renovation?

Not during a major renovation: there is dust and the water and electricity are switched off. It is better to plan to move out for that time.

Where should an apartment renovation start?

With a design project and an estimate. They define the location of services, the scope of works and the budget, so the remaining stages go without rework.

What does a turnkey renovation include?

All stages from design to cleaning: demolition, services, rough and finishing works and, if you wish, furniture and procurement. After handover the apartment is ready to move into.
