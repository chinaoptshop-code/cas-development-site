How much a turnkey apartment costs in Kyiv: design, renovation and furniture

CAS Development · 23 November 2026

Everyone quotes a renovation rate per square metre, but you cannot move in for that sum alone: the design, finishing materials, furniture and appliances come on top. We break down, part by part, how much a turnkey apartment costs in Kyiv, with examples for 45, 60 and 90 m² at CAS rates.

What makes up the budget of a turnkey apartment?

The budget of a turnkey apartment has four parts, and the place to start is that “turnkey” means different things to different people. For a contractor it is usually a finished renovation; for the owner it is an apartment you can live in, with a kitchen, wardrobes, lighting and appliances. That is why it helps to split the full budget into four parts.

  • design project — from $30/m², with a minimum project fee of $2,500
  • renovation — from $550/m²: all works and rough materials
  • finishing materials — tiles, flooring, doors, sanitaryware, paint: priced in the estimate, depending on your choices
  • furniture and appliances — furnishing from $250/m², with household appliances included in that rate; the exact sum follows the specification

Three of the four parts can be estimated from the floor area before any design is done. The fourth, finishing materials, can only be priced honestly from a specification: tiles or flooring of the same format can differ in price several times over.

How much does a turnkey apartment of 45, 60 or 90 m² cost?

For 45, 60 and 90 m² the lower bound is the area multiplied by CAS rates, separately for design, renovation and furnishing. The exact sum comes from the estimate after the site survey and the design. How the renovation price is built up is explained on the apartment renovation cost in Kyiv page.

  • 45 m²: design — $2,500 (the minimum project fee applies), renovation — from $24,750 (45 × $550 = $24,750), furnishing — from $11,250 (45 × $250 = $11,250)
  • 60 m²: design — $2,500 (the minimum again), renovation — from $33,000 (60 × $550 = $33,000), furnishing — from $15,000 (60 × $250 = $15,000)
  • 90 m²: design — from $2,700 (90 × $30 = $2,700), renovation — from $49,500 (90 × $550 = $49,500), furnishing — from $22,500 (90 × $250 = $22,500)

Each line is calculated separately, and none of these figures includes finishing materials. Those come on top, and they are usually what separates a restrained budget from a generous one for the same floor area.

Apartment renovation cost

What does the renovation rate cover, and what does it not?

The rate from $550/m² covers all works, from demolition and building services to final finishes, plus rough materials: cables, pipes, mixes, waterproofing. You choose the tiles, flooring, doors and sanitaryware yourself, so they appear in the estimate as a separate section. You can get a guide figure for your own area in the renovation calculator.

The rate is a lower bound. It goes up with:

  • replacing services and changing the layout
  • complex details: stone, concealed doors, multi-level ceilings
  • several bathrooms and a lot of building services: air conditioning, ventilation, underfloor heating
  • the condition of the apartment: a developer-finished shell needs less work, while older buildings add demolition

Why does a design project for a small apartment cost at least $2,500?

Design costs from $30/m², but no less than $2,500 per project. So for apartments up to roughly 83 m² the project costs the same, and above that it is priced by the metre. If you also need designer supervision, there is a full package from $45/m².

The design affects the money directly. Quantities of works and materials are taken off the drawings, so the estimate is prepared before the start and is not revised along the way. Without a design, a turnkey figure can only be given with a large margin.

When should you order furniture and appliances?

Furniture and appliances are ordered during the renovation, not after it; furnishing starts from $250/m², and household appliances are included in that rate. The kitchen and wardrobes are made to order, and their dimensions depend on water outlets, sockets and niches. So the furniture drawings must be ready before the electrical and plumbing work begins. Some of the furniture, lighting and decor can be shipped directly from factories in China: delivery takes 35–70 days, so they need to be ordered in the middle of the renovation, not after it. How do you pay for a turnkey apartment?

A turnkey apartment is paid for in parts, not all at once. The design project is paid for separately from the renovation. The renovation is paid in parts under the schedule in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10, or a stage-by-stage split starting from a 10% advance. Payments are tied to stage acceptance certificates, so you can see exactly what you are paying for. We covered how to choose a schedule in the article on paying for a renovation in instalments.

Where does the budget grow, and how do you hold it?

The budget usually grows for four reasons:

  • changes after the start: moved sockets, different tiles, a new partition
  • finishing materials chosen along the way, without a specification
  • furniture ordered after the renovation: the outlets and niches were not prepared for it
  • a lump-sum estimate with no quantities in metres and units

The answer to all four is the same: a design and a specification before the start, and an estimate with quantities room by room. If CAS does the renovation, the final total does not deviate from the approved estimate by more than ±5%.

How we price and run a turnkey apartment

A phone consultation is free: from the area and condition of the apartment we will give you a guide figure. Next come the site survey (it is paid, and the fee depends on the property), the design project and an estimate with separate sections for works, rough materials and finishing materials. We fix the price, the schedule and the payment stages in the contract. You have one manager, photo reports every 1–2 weeks and certificates for each stage; the works carry a 12-month warranty. Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey apartment cost in Kyiv?

At CAS rates: a design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500), renovation from $550/m² for works and rough materials, furnishing, household appliances included, from $250/m². Finishing materials are priced separately, in the estimate. The exact sum comes from the estimate after the site survey and the design.

Are finishing materials included in the $550/m²?

No. The rate covers all works and rough materials. Tiles, flooring, doors and sanitaryware are priced separately, in the estimate; furniture and household appliances come under furnishing, from $250/m².

Can I order a renovation without a design project?

Yes, the estimate can be based on your brief or an existing design. But the more precise the drawings, the more precise the estimate and the fewer changes during the works.

Can the sum grow during the renovation?

If CAS does the renovation, the final total does not deviate from the approved estimate by more than ±5%.
