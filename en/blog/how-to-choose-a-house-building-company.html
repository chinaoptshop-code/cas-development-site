How to choose a building company for a house: contract, estimate, payment

CAS Development · 23 November 2026

You build a house once, yet you pick the contractor from a dozen similar offers with a “from” price per metre. Here is how to choose a building company for a house in Kyiv or the region: what to compare in the estimates, what the contract must contain and how to tie payment to stages.

Why do you need the design before the contractor?

The design comes before the contractor because, without it, every company prices a house of its own: one allows for a strip foundation, another for a slab, a third leaves out the floor slabs. Such offers cannot be compared. An architectural design with a structural section gives quantities: how much concrete, how many blocks, how much rebar, what kind of roof. With it you can send the same request to several companies and get estimates that can genuinely be laid side by side.

At CAS, the architectural design of a house costs from $12/m²: a concept and a detailed design with architectural and structural drawings; building services sections and the site plan are added on request. If you already have a design, the estimate is based on it. We went through the set of documents in the article on what a house design project includes. What we build and how is described on the turnkey house construction in Kyiv and the region page.

How do you compare the construction price per metre?

A price per metre can only be compared together with the list of works, because “shell” and “turnkey” mean a different scope at different companies. Ask each contractor to confirm in writing whether the price includes:

  • a ground investigation of the plot and the type of foundation
  • walls, floor slabs and roof: in which materials
  • windows, insulation and facade, that is, the thermal envelope
  • internal services: electrics, heating, water, drainage, ventilation
  • interior finishes
  • connection to external utilities, a borehole and a septic tank

As a guide: at CAS the shell costs from $600/m². That covers site preparation, the foundation, load-bearing walls and partitions, floor slabs, the roof and embedded sleeves for services. A turnkey house, with the thermal envelope, internal services and finishes, costs from $1,100/m². For a 150 m² house the lower bound is: shell — from $90,000 (150 × $600 = $90,000), turnkey — from $165,000 (150 × $1,100 = $165,000). External utilities, a borehole, a septic tank, furniture and landscaping are priced separately. More in the article on a turnkey house versus a shell. The rate also depends on the technology: in the cost calculator you can choose the house type, from brick and aerated block to timber frame, modular or log, and see the guide figure for it.

House construction from $600/m², turnkey from $1,100/m²; architectural design from $12/m². Phone consultation is free.

What should you check about a building company before the contract?

Before the contract, find out who signs it, which projects you can see, who works on site and how the company reports:

  • who signs the contract: a registered company or a sole proprietor with full legal details, not a foreman on a verbal promise
  • projects you can see: one in progress and one handed over
  • who will work on site: staff or subcontractors, and who answers for them
  • who runs the project and how to reach them
  • how they report: photos, certificates, access to the site
  • whether they are ready for technical supervision on your side

If these questions get evasive answers before signing, the answers will not become more precise during construction.

Technical supervision means an engineer on your side who accepts concealed works and checks them against the design. For a house it is especially useful at the foundation and floor-slab stages: a reinforcement mistake cannot be corrected once the concrete has been poured.

What must be written into the contract with a building company?

The contract should include the design, an estimate with quantities, a stage schedule, payment against certificates, acceptance of concealed works and a warranty:

  • the address of the plot and the design as an annex to the contract
  • an estimate with quantities, materials and prices item by item
  • a stage schedule with start and handover dates
  • payment tied to certificates for completed stages
  • acceptance of concealed works before they are covered: reinforcement, foundation waterproofing, embedded sleeves
  • the warranty and the procedure for remedying defects
  • what happens if the design changes: a supplementary agreement with a new price and deadline
  • the parties’ liability for missed deadlines and late payment

Construction is formalised with a construction contract. Everything agreed verbally — deadlines, substituted materials, extra charges — should be moved into the text of the contract or a supplementary agreement.

How do you pay for building a house?

A house is paid for in parts, and each payment is tied to a stage: foundation, walls and floor slabs, roof, thermal envelope, services, finishes. At CAS the client chooses the schedule: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10, or a stage-by-stage split starting from a 10% advance. A demand to pay everything upfront, or a schedule in which payments do not depend on results, should put you on your guard.

Which warning signs should put you on guard?

A price without a list of works, a refusal to work from a design and a contract without an estimate should all put you on guard:

  • a price per metre with no list of what it includes
  • “you don’t need a design, we’ll build from a sketch”
  • a foundation priced without a ground investigation of the plot
  • a price noticeably lower than other offers, with no explanation of how
  • no projects they can show you
  • a one-page contract with no estimate and no schedule

Questions and answers

How do I choose a building company for a house?

Compare estimates prepared from the same design, not prices per metre. Check who signs the contract, which projects you can see, who works on site and whether payment is tied to stage certificates.

Can construction start without a design?

It is not a good idea. Without a design there are no quantities, so the estimate is approximate and decisions are made on site. An architectural design at CAS costs from $12/m².

How much does it cost to build a house with CAS?

The shell — from $600/m², a turnkey house with finishes — from $1,100/m². External utilities, a borehole, a septic tank, furniture and landscaping are priced separately; the exact sum comes from the estimate based on the design.

How do I pay for building a house?

In parts, under the schedule in the contract: 50/50, 50/30/20, 50/40/10, or a stage-by-stage split starting from a 10% advance. Payments are tied to stage certificates.
