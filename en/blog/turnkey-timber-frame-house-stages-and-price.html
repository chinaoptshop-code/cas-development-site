Turnkey timber frame house: stages and what the price consists of

CAS Development · 16 November 2026

A turnkey timber frame house costs from $1,100/m², and people choose it for speed, a light foundation and warmth with moderately thick walls. We explain how construction goes stage by stage, what the shell and turnkey options include, which works are costed separately and how to read an estimate to compare offers fairly.

What is a timber frame house?

A timber frame house has a load-bearing wooden frame of studs, beams and rafters. The space between the studs is filled with insulation; outside, the frame is closed with boards and a facade, inside with a vapour barrier and plasterboard. The structure is light, so a pile or strip foundation or an insulated slab without massive walls below ground is often enough.

How warm such a house is depends not on the material itself but on the quality of the junctions: gap-free insulation, a taped vapour barrier, insulated window installation. That is why we specify these details in the design and check them on site before the walls are closed. If you are still choosing the technology, see the comparison in our article timber frame, aerated block or timber.

What are the stages of building a timber frame house?

  1. Survey: mapping the terrain and setting out the house axes on the plot.
  2. Foundation: the type suits the soil and loads from the design; sleeves for services go in before concreting.
  3. Sole plate on waterproofing, frame studs and OSB boards that give the frame its rigidity.
  4. Floor structure and the second-floor frame, if there is one.
  5. Rafters, membrane, ventilated gap and roof covering.
  6. Windows and the entrance door with insulated installation.
  7. Wall and roof insulation, wind and vapour barriers.
  8. Building services: electrics, water, drainage, heating, ventilation.
  9. Service layer, plasterboard, rough and finishing works.
  10. Facade, perimeter paving, handover with certificates and as-built documents.

How do shell, thermal envelope and turnkey differ?

Contractors call the same stages by different names, so look at the scope of works rather than just the label. In our estimate a house is divided like this:

  • shell — site preparation, foundation, wall and partition framing, floors, rafters and roof, sleeves for services
  • thermal envelope — windows and entrance door, insulation of walls, roof and plinth, membranes, facade, gutters; costed as a separate stage per estimate
  • turnkey — the shell and thermal envelope plus building services, rough and finishing works, interior doors, sanitaryware, sockets and lighting

Why the thermal envelope is separate: the cost of windows and facade depends heavily on your choices — the size and number of windows, the profile, the cladding. Most builders cost it this way, and it makes offers easier to compare.

How much does a turnkey timber frame house cost?

Our rates for Kyiv (we also build in all regions of Ukraine and in Europe): shell from $600/m², a turnkey house with finishes from $1,100/m². An architectural design for the house starts from $12/m². Landscape design for the plot starts from $50 per 100 m².

Example for a 120 m² house: shell from 120 × $600 = $72,000, turnkey from 120 × $1,100 = $132,000, architectural design from 120 × $12 = $1,440. This is a guide; the exact sum for your house comes from the estimate based on the design.

The total is affected by:

  • area and number of storeys
  • the foundation type for the soil on the plot
  • insulation thickness and roof type
  • the number and size of windows
  • services: a boiler or a heat pump, ventilation with or without heat recovery
  • the finishing class

Furniture and appliances, connection to external utilities, a borehole and septic tank, and landscaping are costed separately, per estimate. A full breakdown of house costs is on the house construction cost page.

What should you check in a timber frame house estimate?

  • whether the timber section and species, moisture content and antiseptic treatment are stated
  • which insulation and what thickness is used in walls and roof
  • whether membranes and taped joints are in the estimate
  • how windows are installed — whether insulated installation is included
  • what exactly finishing includes in the turnkey option
  • which works are listed “separately” and who does them

One more point is the wall and roof build-up. Each layer has its role, and if one is skipped the house may lose heat or let the insulation get damp. We showed how the layers go together in our article on wall, floor and roof build-ups.

Which to choose: shell or turnkey?

A shell means a smaller starting budget, but you will have to organise services and finishing yourself and control the interfaces between contractors. Turnkey gives you one responsible party and a warranty on the whole scope of works. We compared the pros and risks of both in the article turnkey house or shell.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey timber frame house cost?

From $1,100/m² with finishes; the shell from $600/m². A 120 m² turnkey house starts from $132,000. The exact sum comes from the estimate based on the design.

What does the shell of a timber frame house include?

Site preparation and foundation, wall and partition framing, floors, rafters and roof, sleeves for services. The thermal envelope — windows, insulation, facade — is costed as a separate stage.

Can you live in a timber frame house in winter?

Yes. Warmth depends on the quality of insulation, vapour barrier and window installation, so we specify these details in the design and check them before the walls are closed.

Do you build to someone else's design?

Yes. We check the drawings, refine the junctions if needed and draw up the estimate. We can also do the architectural design ourselves, from $12/m².
