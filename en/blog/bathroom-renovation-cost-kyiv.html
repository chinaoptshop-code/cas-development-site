How much a bathroom renovation costs in Kyiv and what drives the price

CAS Development · 25 October 2026

A bathroom renovation price in Kyiv is not a single figure per metre: the bathroom is the most complex room in any renovation, so its cost is calculated in an estimate after a survey, and the benchmark we can confirm is a turnkey apartment renovation from $550/m². Here is what that cost is made of and how to get an honest estimate.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Kyiv?

There is no separate price “per bathroom”: the cost depends on the design, the materials and the condition of the room, and the benchmark we can confirm is a turnkey apartment renovation from $550/m². A bathroom is a small area with a large amount of work: water supply, drainage, electrics, waterproofing, tiling, ventilation. That is why the same room can cost very different amounts depending on the decisions made. If the bathroom is part of an apartment renovation, it is counted in the overall estimate. The exact figure for the bathroom itself comes from the estimate after a survey.

What makes up the cost of a bathroom renovation?

  • stripping out old tiles and sanitary ware, and removing the debris
  • water and drainage pipework, electrics, ventilation
  • waterproofing of the floor and wet areas, a screed with the correct slopes
  • tiles and tiling: layout, cutting, grouting
  • sanitary ware, mixer taps, concealed cistern frames, the shower area
  • vanity units, mirrors, lighting
  • underfloor heating, the door, the ceiling

It is also worth separating works from materials. Works are what the crew does; the materials and sanitary ware you choose yourself, and their cost can differ several times over. That is why our estimate splits these two parts, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Renovation from $550/m²: labour and rough materials; finishing materials, furniture and appliances are estimated separately. Phone consultation is free.

What makes a bathroom renovation more expensive?

  • relocating risers, floor drains and sanitary ware to new positions
  • concealed cistern frames and a shower without a tray, which requires precise slopes
  • large-format tiles or stone, a complex layout
  • backlit niches, underfloor heating, several lighting scenes
  • custom furniture and non-standard sizes
  • a poor substrate: levelling walls and floors

Each of these items is visible at the survey stage, so the extra cost never comes as a surprise halfway through the works. If the budget is tight, we suggest what can be dropped without compromising quality and what is better kept.

What should you never save on in a bathroom?

Never save on waterproofing, slopes, access hatches and ventilation: the most expensive mistakes in a bathroom are the hidden ones. Poor waterproofing, wrong slopes, missing access hatches and weak ventilation only show up once the tiles are on, and fixing them means tearing everything out. So save on decor and colour, not on the technology. We carry out these stages strictly to specification and show them in photo reports.

How do you get an honest bathroom estimate?

  1. a survey that checks the risers, the ventilation and the condition of the substrate
  2. decisions on the plan: what changes, what stays, which sanitary ware
  3. an estimate where works and materials are listed as separate items
  4. changes agreed before the start, so the crew does not decide them on the fly
  5. a contract that fixes the scope and the amount

Be wary of a single-figure estimate with no breakdown: it is hard to see what is included and what you will have to pay extra for. A good estimate can be read line by line and compared with another one.

Can sanitary ware and furniture come from China?

Yes — and it matters, because sanitary ware, mixer taps, mirrors and bathroom furniture are a noticeable part of the budget. They can be ordered directly from factories in China: delivery takes 35–70 days, so it is worth ordering in advance. Details of how we fit out a project are on our turnkey procurement page.

Questions and answers

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Kyiv?

We do not quote a separate fixed price: it depends on the area, the tiles, the sanitary ware and the scope of works. Turnkey apartment renovation starts from $550/m², and the cost of the bathroom is calculated in the estimate after a survey.
