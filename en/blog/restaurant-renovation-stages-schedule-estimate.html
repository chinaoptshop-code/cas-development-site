Restaurant renovation in Kyiv: stages, schedule to opening day and the estimate

CAS Development · 18 November 2026

A restaurant renovation runs to a fixed date: the rent is already being paid, the staff are hired and the opening is announced. We look at what to check in the premises before the start, how a restaurant renovation goes stage by stage, how to build a schedule to the opening and what makes up the estimate.

How does a restaurant renovation differ from an apartment?

In an apartment, what matters most is what you see. In a restaurant, the most important things are hidden: extraction and supply ventilation, power for cookers and refrigeration, floor drains and drainage with grease traps, kitchen surfaces that can be washed every day. A mistake here will surface after the opening, and it will have to be fixed in a working venue.

The second difference is the schedule. The renovation of a venue is tied to the opening date, and in parallel you have to order furniture, lighting and equipment, and hire and train the staff. That is why we draw up the works plan together with the launch plan.

What should you check in the premises before starting?

Some questions are worth settling before the lease is signed, because they decide whether a restaurant will work in this space at all:

  • what electrical capacity the landlord provides and whether it can be increased
  • where the kitchen extraction can be routed: a shaft, the roof or the facade, and who approves it
  • where the drainage runs, whether there is enough fall for floor drains and room for grease traps
  • ceiling height: ductwork and a suspended ceiling need a margin
  • whether there is a separate delivery entrance so that goods are not carried through the dining room
  • the condition of the screed, walls and windows, and what will have to be stripped out

What are the stages of a restaurant renovation?

  1. Site survey and 3D scan of the premises: the design is based on real dimensions.
  2. Design: kitchen workflow layout, services for the equipment, dining room, bar, guest toilets.
  3. Estimate and works schedule to the opening date.
  4. Strip-out and general building works: partitions, openings, levelling.
  5. Building services: ventilation and air conditioning, water, drainage with grease traps, a separate distribution board and circuits for the equipment.
  6. Acceptance of concealed works before they are covered by screed and walls.
  7. Waterproofing and kitchen floor and wall coverings, screeds, finishes in the dining room and toilets.
  8. Bar, display cases, installation of lighting and equipment.
  9. Furniture, terrace, facade and signage.
  10. System checks, cleaning and handover with signed certificates.

How do you schedule the works to the opening date?

The opening date is fixed in the contract, and the schedule is built backwards from it. The longest chain in a restaurant usually looks like this: building services, then the floors and kitchen coverings, then equipment installation and testing. Everything that can run in parallel is taken out of this chain:

  • furniture, lighting and textiles are ordered straight after the design is approved, so that they arrive in time for the finish
  • kitchen equipment is agreed with the supplier against the workflow layout before the services are laid
  • signage and the facade are approved while the works inside are under way
  • the terrace is prepared as a separate stage if the opening falls in the warm season

The duration of the works themselves depends on the area, the condition of the premises and the scope of the services, so we state it after the design and write it into the contract.

What does a restaurant renovation estimate consist of?

We do not quote a ready-made price per square metre for the renovation of a venue: two restaurants of the same area can differ several times over because of the ventilation and the kitchen. The estimate is drawn up from the design, and it lists as separate lines:

  • strip-out and general building works
  • ventilation, kitchen extraction and air conditioning of the dining room
  • water supply, drainage, floor drains and grease traps
  • electrics: distribution board, circuits for equipment, dining room lighting
  • finishes in the dining room, bar and guest toilets
  • coverings for the kitchen, wash-up and prep areas
  • furniture, lighting, decor, terrace, facade and signage

A design project for a venue costs from $30/m², with a minimum of $2,500. For a 200 m² restaurant that is from 200 m² × $30 = $6,000. If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the final total does not deviate from the estimate by more than ±5%. What the service includes is on the turnkey restaurant and café fit-out page.

How do you refresh a venue that is already open?

Sometimes what is needed is not a full renovation but a refresh of the dining room: new lighting, furniture, wall finishes, a bar counter. This kind of work can be split into stages — for example, the terrace first and then the dining room — so that the venue does not close completely. Whether this will work in your case depends on whether the works touch the kitchen and the ventilation. That becomes clear after a visit and the design.

Questions and answers

How much does a restaurant renovation in Kyiv cost?

We price the renovation of a venue by the project estimate: the amount depends on the ventilation, the kitchen, the number of toilets and the condition of the premises. A design project costs from $30/m², with a minimum of $2,500.

How long does a restaurant renovation take?

It depends on the area, the condition of the premises and the scope of the building services. We state the duration after the design and fix it in the contract together with the opening date.

Can you renovate to someone else's design?

Yes. We check the drawings, especially the services for the equipment, refine them where needed and prepare the estimate.

Can the restaurant stay open during the renovation?

Sometimes: a refresh of the dining room or the terrace can be split into stages. If the works touch the kitchen and the ventilation, the venue usually has to close for those stages.
