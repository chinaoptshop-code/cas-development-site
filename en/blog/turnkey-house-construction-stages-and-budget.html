Turnkey house construction in the Kyiv region: stages and budget

CAS Development · 22 October 2026

A house is the biggest investment for most families: construction costs from $600/m², and with turnkey finishing from $1,100/m². We explain how turnkey construction goes from the plot to moving in, what the budget consists of and how to stay within it.

How much does building a turnkey house cost?

At CAS Development house construction starts from $600/m², and construction with turnkey finishing from $1,100/m². For a 150 m² turnkey house that is roughly from $165,000. The exact sum is set by the design: wall and roof materials, services, finishing level.

What are the stages of building a house?

  1. plot inspection, brief and budget
  2. architectural design, layout, facades and 3D
  3. estimate and a contract with stages
  4. foundation and shell
  5. roof, windows, facade
  6. building services
  7. interior finishing, furniture and landscaping

House construction from $600/m², turnkey from $1,100/m²; architectural design from $12/m². Phone consultation is free.

What does the construction budget consist of?

  • design: architecture, structure, services
  • shell: foundation, walls, floors, roof
  • windows and facade
  • services: heating, water, drainage, electrics, ventilation
  • interior finishing
  • furniture, lighting and landscape

Why does construction start with the design?

The design gives an accurate estimate and answers the main question — how much the house will cost. Without it a price “per square metre” guarantees nothing: two shells of the same area can differ in price by a factor of two because of materials and services. An architecture and interior design project starts from $30/m², with a minimum budget of $2,500.

How do you keep construction on budget?

  • build services into the design rather than solving them during construction
  • fix the stages and price in the contract
  • agree changes in writing
  • plan the landscape together with the house — you will not have to dig up services later
  • procure furniture and lighting in parallel with finishing

We build single- and two-storey houses, cottages, A-frame houses and holiday resorts in Kyiv and the Kyiv region. One team handles design, construction, finishing and turnkey procurement.

What should you check on the plot before the design?

  • plot boundaries and permitted land use
  • access for machinery
  • terrain, soil type, groundwater level
  • the possibility of connecting electricity, gas and water
  • building restrictions and setbacks from boundaries

These data affect the foundation, the position of the house and the services budget. It is better to find them out before the sketch than to redo the design.

Which wall material should you choose for a house?

Aerated block, ceramic block, timber frame or monolithic solutions differ in price, timing and insulation requirements. We choose the structure to suit the budget, the plot and how the family will live in the house — all year round or seasonally.

What building services does a private house need?

Heating, water supply, drainage, ventilation and electrics in a private house are entirely your responsibility. We build the solutions into the design: boiler type, underfloor heating, heat recovery, backup power. Mistakes in services do not show at once, but they are the most expensive to fix.

How do you keep track of the construction?

During construction you receive photo reports from the site every 1–2 weeks, and payment is made stage by stage. Concealed works — reinforcement, waterproofing, insulation — are recorded before they are closed. This way you see what you are paying for even if you are not on the plot every week.

The warranty on the works is 12 months.

How much does a 150 m² house cost?

For a 150 m² house the guide is: shell and roof from $90,000 (150 m² × $600), a turnkey house with finishing from $165,000 (150 m² × $1,100). The exact sum depends on the design, wall materials, services and the plot, so we fix the final figure in the estimate after the design.

What should be decided at the start of construction?

  • how many storeys and bedrooms the family needs now and in 5–10 years
  • where the boiler room, wardrobes and pantries will be
  • whether you need a terrace, a carport, a pool
  • what the landscape and paths around the house will be like

Answering these questions before the design starts saves money: redoing drawings is cheaper than redoing walls. We also design the landscape around the house — from $50 per 100 m².
