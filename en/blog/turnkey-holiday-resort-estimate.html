Turnkey holiday resort: what the estimate consists of

CAS Development · 10 November 2026

A holiday resort estimate is not one price per metre but a set of buildings, each with its own rate. We look at what it consists of and how to split construction into phases. On our site you can assemble the mix yourself in the interactive “Holiday complex” master plan.

1. Why is the master plan the basis of the estimate?

The master plan determines how many buildings there are, where they stand and how guests and staff move between them. Without it the estimate is just a guess. The master plan immediately shows utilities, paths, parking and space for the next phases.

2. What share of the budget do the cabins take?

The largest part of the budget. Their number and size set the capacity: in the master plan on our site you can choose 4, 8 or 12 cabins of 30, 45 or 60 m². Building the shell starts from $600/m², turnkey from $1,100/m².

The cabin format should suit the guest: 30 m² with a terrace is enough for couples, families need 45–60 m² with two bedrooms. A mixed set lets you sell at different prices and fill the resort better on weekdays and weekends. Some cabins can be placed over the water — we covered that format separately in our article on houses on water.

3. What goes into the main building?

Reception, restaurant, kitchen, sometimes a spa or conference room — usually 150–300 m². It is the most complex building in terms of services: kitchen ventilation, drainage with grease traps, power capacity.

4. What should be budgeted for leisure and landscaping?

  • the pool and the area around it
  • pergolas and relaxation zones
  • a beach and bar, if there is water
  • paths, lighting, landscape — from $50 per 100 m²

5. What do the technical zone and utilities include?

A boiler room or heat pumps, wastewater treatment, electrics and backup power, storage, laundry, parking. Utilities often become a hidden cost item — that is why we cost them at the master-plan stage, not afterwards.

6. How much do design and furnishing cost?

Interior design starts from $30/m², furnishing from $250/m². For identical cabins the design is done once and replicated, and furniture and decor are more economical to order as a batch directly from factories.

What is often left out of the estimate?

  • roads and access for construction machinery to the site
  • connection to external utilities and approval of the power capacity
  • fencing, the entrance and a security post
  • outdoor lighting and wayfinding across the site
  • furniture for terraces, the beach and the pool
  • inventory: textiles, tableware, appliances in the cabins

These items are small on their own, but together they can increase the budget considerably. That is why they go into the estimate as separate lines, not as “other costs”.

How do you cut the budget without losing quality?

Identical cabins are cheaper than unique ones: the design, drawings and purchasing are done once. Timber frame and modular cabins are built faster, so the resort starts earning sooner. Furniture and lighting for the whole resort are more economical to order as one batch directly from factories. And some buildings — for example a second pool or a spa — can be included in the master plan but built from the first season's revenue.

How do we calculate a resort estimate?

After the brief and site inspection we draw up the master plan and break the resort down into buildings. Each has its own rate: cabins and the main building per metre, landscaping per 100 m², utilities and foundations per estimate. This shows how much each phase costs and what gives the greatest effect for the launch.

7. How do you build a resort in phases?

  1. first phase — some of the cabins, the main building, utilities for the whole resort
  2. second — the remaining cabins, pool, beach
  3. third — expansion based on the results of the first season

Utilities are best laid for the whole master plan at once: then the second phase will not require digging up an already finished site.

CAS Development takes a resort from master plan to opening with one team: design, estimate, construction, furnishing. You have one manager, photo reports every 1–2 weeks and a 12-month warranty on the works.

Questions and answers

How much does a turnkey holiday resort cost?

It depends on the mix: cabins and the main building from $1,100/m² turnkey, the shell from $600/m². The full estimate comes after the master plan.

How many cabins are needed to start?

It depends on the format and the site; resorts often start with the first phase and expand after the first season.

Can I see the resort mix before the design?

Yes, in the interactive master plan on our site you can choose the number and size of cabins, the main building, pool and beach.

Do you do design and furnishing for resorts?

Yes: design from $30/m², furnishing from $250/m², furniture and decor directly from factories.
