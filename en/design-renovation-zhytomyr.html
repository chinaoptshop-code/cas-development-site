Zhytomyr: private houses, granite riverbanks and forests

Zhytomyr has a large private housing sector, an old centre and estates of apartment blocks. Around it are forests and the rocky granite banks of the Teteriv, where many people keep a dacha or a holiday house.

Typical projects here are rebuilding an old family house, a holiday house in nature or an apartment for children who have moved to Kyiv. We can handle both the Zhytomyr and the Kyiv side if needed.

Interior design in Zhytomyr

For houses by the forest and the river we base the design on natural materials — wood, stone, linen — and warm light, as in our A-Frame House and Hoda Estate. For city apartments, a practical layout and storage. Design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500); packages are on the interior design studio page.

Turnkey renovation and house construction

We renovate houses and apartments, and convert dachas for year-round living: insulation, new services, a new roof. In the Zhytomyr region log and timber-frame houses fit well, as do classic aerated-block ones; plots by the Teteriv are often rocky, so the foundation is designed after the site survey.

Renovation is carried out by our salaried team: from $550/m² for works and rough materials, with finishing materials, furniture and appliances priced separately per estimate. Scope, schedule and payment plan (50/50, 50/30/20 or in stages, from a 10% advance) are fixed in the contract; team travel is on individual terms. Architectural design from $12/m², house shell from $600/m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100/m² — see turnkey renovation and house construction.

Logistics, control and reports

Design and approvals run online; we travel for measurements and key stages on the schedule set in the contract, and the terms and costs of travel and team accommodation are individual. Between visits you get photo reports every 1–2 weeks, and an online camera on site 24/7 is available for an extra fee.
