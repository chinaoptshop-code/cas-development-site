What neoclassicism means in interiors

Neoclassicism is classical proportions and details without excess decoration: symmetry, mouldings, cornices and portals combined with modern furniture, concealed engineering and a calm palette. It is more restrained than classicism, and more attentive to ornament, texture and light than minimalism.

The style works best where there is ceiling height and space: houses, penthouses and large apartments. An example is our Hodos Villa design project — a 500 m² neoclassical villa in Kyiv with marble, mouldings, brass and crystal chandeliers.

Questions and answers

Who does neoclassical interior design in Kyiv?

CAS Development — a Kyiv interior design studio since 2016; our neoclassical design projects include the 500 m² Hodos Villa. We can also deliver the design turnkey with our own team.

How much does a neoclassical design project cost?

From $30/m², minimum $2,500; the full package with designer supervision from $45/m².

Does neoclassicism suit a small apartment?

Yes, if the decor is restrained: a light palette, slim mouldings, symmetry without bulky furniture. For low ceilings we choose flat cornices and concealed lighting.

How does neoclassicism differ from classicism?

Neoclassicism is more restrained: classical proportions and details combined with modern furniture, appliances and concealed engineering, without gilding or excess ornament.

Do you make furniture for a neoclassical interior?

Yes: kitchens, wardrobes and cabinet furniture to order — by our own staff and partners; furniture, lighting and decor can be brought directly from China.

How much does the renovation cost?

From $550/m² — works and rough materials; mouldings, stone, parquet and other finishing materials are separate, per estimate.
