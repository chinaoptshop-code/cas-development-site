Hostomel: private houses and new districts

Hostomel is above all a town of private houses: homesteads on a few hundred square metres, new cottage communities and small complexes closer to the road to Kyiv. Many owners here do not renovate but build anew or substantially rebuild an old house.

For Hostomel plots we often do the whole package: house design, construction, systems, interior and landscaping. That way the joints between stages — foundation and floors, services and furniture — hold no surprises at the finishing stage.

Interior design in Hostomel

In a Hostomel house the design starts with the floor plans: where the bedrooms go, where the utility and boiler rooms are, how you walk from the terrace to the kitchen. Single-storey houses with a terrace and a pool, like our House 150, work especially well on small plots. Design project from $30/m² (minimum $2,500); packages are on the interior design studio page.

Turnkey renovation and house construction

We renovate houses after purchase, replan old homesteads and update apartments in complexes; with an old house we first check the roof, walls and services. We build aerated-block and brick, modular and timber-frame houses, with the house construction cost set out by stage in the contract.

The same salaried team as in Kyiv, at the same rates: renovation from $550/m² (works and rough materials), and if CAS carries out the renovation, the final total stays within ±5% of the estimate. Architectural design from $12/m², house shell from $600/m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100/m²; 12-month warranty on works. More on turnkey renovation and house construction.

Logistics, control and reports

Our site managers include visits to the town in their normal schedule, just as for Kyiv districts, and materials come from Kyiv warehouses. Between visits you get photo reports every 1–2 weeks; an online camera on site 24/7 is available for an extra fee.

Questions and answers

How much does it cost to build a house in Hostomel?

Shell from $600/m², turnkey with finishing from $1,100/m². For a 150 m² house the base figure is 150 m² × $600 for the shell or 150 m² × $1,100 turnkey; the exact price comes from the estimate.

Do you do interiors for a house built by another contractor?

Yes. Design from $30/m², renovation from $550/m²; if CAS carries out the renovation, we keep within ±5% of the estimate.

Is it better to rebuild an old house or build a new one?

It depends on the foundation and walls. After a survey we give an estimate for both options, so you decide with the numbers in hand.
