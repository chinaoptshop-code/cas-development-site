About the project

The brief

A beach resort in Bodrum (Turkey): we had to develop the concept, place the buildings on the site and design the architecture of the cabins and the reception.

The solution

The cabins stand over the water, reached by wooden walkways. The reception is a glass volume, with pools and lounge areas with soft poufs nearby. The master plan was built around guest routes: entrance, reception, then on to the beach and the pools.

The materials are natural — wood, reed and stone — alongside contemporary glass façades. In the evening the resort is carried by its lighting, so on the visualisations it stays expressive after sunset too.

The result

Design project: resort concept, master plan and zoning, architecture of the cabins and reception, 3D visualisations. The interactive scenes below show how we build properties like this.
