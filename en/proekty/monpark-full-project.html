Monpark — a full restaurant design project

Visualisations · drawings · completed venue

Project at a glance

Property: a two-storey restaurant with a main terrace, a back courtyard and a second-floor terrace

City: Kyiv, city centre

Status: completed by CAS Development — the venue is open (stills from on-site video below)

What the package includes: 3D visualisations of the interior and exterior and an album of 247 working-drawing sheets

  • measured plans before and after replanning, partition construction plan
  • electrics: sockets and outlets, lighting, lighting groups, LED strips, acoustics
  • heating, ventilation, plumbing outlets
  • floor finishes, granite «rugs», travertine, railing and balustrade
  • technical briefs for details: vestibule, stairs, pergolas, cornice, restrooms
  • drawings of custom furniture and decor: tables, sofas, bar counters, signage, light fittings
  • specifications for lighting, furniture and materials
