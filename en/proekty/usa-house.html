About the project

The brief

A 420 m² house in the USA for Ukrainian clients: they needed an interior design project with space planning, visualisations and furniture selection.

The solution

The palette is restrained: grey, beige, warm wood. Accents come from green textiles and art on the walls. The living rooms have soft sofas, and the kitchen an island with bar stools.

Wooden panels and hidden lighting recur from room to room, so a large house doesn't fall apart into separate interiors. Furniture was selected within the project, together with the space planning.

The result

Interior design project: space planning, 3D visualisations, furniture selection. CAS has had and has projects in the USA, Spain, France, Germany, Poland and Bali.

For large houses we offer the full package with design supervision, from $45/m². If another contractor does the renovation, CAS technical and design supervision is available separately; terms and cost are agreed individually.
