About the project

The brief

A light interior where storage had to be carefully thought out: a separate walk-in wardrobe, plus space planning, 3D visualisations and furniture selection.

The solution

The kitchen, living room and dining area were combined into one space. The palette is restrained and beige, so character comes from textures and details: graphic art on the walls and thin lines of light.

In the bedrooms the walls behind the beds are finished with textile panels that look and feel soft. The walk-in wardrobe has glass fronts. Furniture was chosen in the same palette so the rooms read as one apartment.

The result

Design project: space planning, 3D visualisations, walk-in wardrobe and furniture selection. You can walk through the property in the 360° virtual tour below. CAS can take on the full implementation of a project like this: renovation from $550/m², furnishing from $250/m².

We work on apartments like this from a 3D survey: our specialist scans the space, and the walk-in wardrobe or built-in cabinets are designed to real dimensions. Custom kitchens and cabinets are made by our joiners and partners.
