About the project

The brief

A 210 m² penthouse in Kyiv where everything, from the layout to the stone, wood and sanitaryware, had to be done as one project, with well-planned storage and a workspace.

The solution

The whole interior rests on the contrast of warm wood and natural stone. Vertical timber slats divide the space instead of solid walls. The light stone of the floor continues up the bathroom walls, and the kitchen island is made of boldly veined marble.

For storage there is a walk-in wardrobe with glass fronts and lighting, plus cabinets built into the timber panelling. The workspace sits by the window. There are no heavy chandeliers: even light comes from track lights and linear fittings in the ceiling.

The result

Interior design project: space planning and zoning, 3D visualisations of every room, selection of stone, wood and sanitaryware, walk-in wardrobe and built-in furniture. CAS prices renovation to this design from $550/m²; finishing materials and furniture by cost estimate.
