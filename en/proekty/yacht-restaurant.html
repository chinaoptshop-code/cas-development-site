About the project

The brief

A premium restaurant with a view of the water: it needed a venue concept, zoning of the dining hall, bar and fireplace area, and a selection of furniture and lighting.

The solution

The concept is the atmosphere of a yacht club. Timber arches, light fabric ceilings shaped like sails, woven pendant lights and living trees in planters. Panoramic glazing opens a view of the water from most tables.

The dining hall was divided into zones with different seating: sofa, bar and a fireplace area with a decorative relief. The bar with hanging shelves sits under rattan pendants. The light is warm and subdued, so in the evening the space stays cosy.

The result

Design project: venue concept, zoning of the dining hall, bar and fireplace area, 3D visualisations, furniture and lighting selection. Restaurant design at CAS is from $30/m², minimum $2,500; renovation and kitchen equipment by cost estimate.

For restaurants we run the project from the measured survey to custom furniture. Ventilation, drainage with grease traps and kitchen electrics are built into the design before the dining hall gets its finishes, so the ceiling and floor don't have to be redone after the equipment is installed.
