About the project

The brief

A premium residence with an inner courtyard: it needed a concept, the design of the interior and the courtyard, and 3D visualisations.

The solution

The interior is built on natural materials: wood, plaster, stone. The ceilings are wooden, the pendant lights woven, and the fireplace is cone-shaped.

The inner courtyard with an olive tree got a skylight, so daylight reaches the middle of the house. Soft seating areas are arranged so the house feels like a country retreat.

The result

Design project: concept, interior design, inner courtyard, 3D visualisations. For properties of this level CAS offers the full package with design supervision — from $45/m².

We work on houses like this from a 3D survey: our specialist scans the space, and the fireplace, ceilings and built-in furniture are designed to real dimensions. During the build the designer visits the site as part of design supervision.
