About the project

The brief

A villa in Dubai: the architecture of the house, terraces with a pool and landscaping of the plot for a hot climate, where much of life happens outdoors.

The solution

The house is composed of white volumes in a contemporary minimalist style. Between them are inner courtyards with palms, and aluminium pergolas with louvres shade the terraces. A terrace with lounge sofas extends the living room outdoors, with the pool right next to it.

The evening was thought through separately: lighting for the façade, the canopied entrance with a sculpture and the greenery by the door. The visualisations show how the house looks by day and after sunset.

The result

This is a CAS design project: villa architecture, terraces and pool, landscaping and 3D visualisations.

CAS's architectural design for a house covers the concept and working stages of the architectural and structural sections; engineering sections and the site plan are added as required. The landscape for a villa like this (terraces, courtyards, planting) is designed together with the house; landscape design is from $50 per 100 m².
