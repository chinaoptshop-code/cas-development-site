About the project

The brief

An apartment that needed a complete design project — space planning, 3D visualisations, furniture and lighting — for a calm, contemporary interior.

The solution

A warm beige palette became the base. The living room has soft sofas with flowing shapes, a kitchen with an island next to it and art on the walls. There are hardly any chandeliers: the light is linear and hidden, washing the wall and ceiling planes.

The bedrooms have soft headboards and a textured rug; in the corridors the doors are concealed flush with the wall, so the circulation areas aren't broken up by openings. Furniture and lighting were selected within the same project.

The result

A finished apartment design project: space planning, 3D visualisations, furniture and lighting selection. CAS can carry it out: renovation from $550/m² (labour and rough materials), with the designer providing design supervision.

For a similar apartment we start with a survey: our specialist scans the space with a 3D scanner, and the layout is built on real dimensions. You can then stop at a space-planning package (from $6/m²) or order the full project with design supervision (from $45/m²), where the designer visits the site during renovation.
