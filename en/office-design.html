Office design built around the team's processes

An office starts not with the colour of the walls but with how the team works: how many people need focus, how many are on calls, how often clients visit, where meetings happen. This defines the balance of open space and private offices, the number of meeting rooms, acoustics and where the kitchen goes.

Then come lighting at workstations, ventilation, power and low-voltage cabling for equipment, and materials that stand up to daily wear. We put all of this into the drawings so the renovation runs without improvisation.

Open space, private offices or a mixed layout

Open space gives flexibility and fast communication; private offices give quiet for focused work and confidential conversations. Most often we design a mixed layout: open workstations, phone booths, several meeting rooms of different sizes and a quiet zone.

Office Chicago: an office without solid walls

One of our turnkey projects is Office Chicago: zoning without solid walls, green walls and an island of live plants between desks, linear lighting over workstations. The space is open, but every zone has its own function.

We work the same way with other commercial spaces: the Dolls Salon beauty salon was delivered turnkey — from the facade and entrance to the stylists' workstations.

How much office design and renovation cost

A design project costs from $30/m², minimum $2,500. Office renovation costs from $350/m² (works and rough materials). For a 400 m² office the base calculation is 400 m² × $30 for design and 400 m² × $350 for renovation.

We sign contracts with companies, invoice companies registered in Ukraine and provide closing documents. If CAS carries out the renovation to our design, the total stays within ±5% of the estimate.

Office furniture

After the design we furnish the office: workstations, ergonomic chairs, meeting tables, acoustic booths, reception desks and lighting. Items are made to order at factories in China, delivery 35–70 days.

Questions and answers

How much does office design cost?

From $30/m², minimum project fee $2,500. Office renovation from $350/m² (works and rough materials). The exact figure comes from the estimate after the site survey.

Can I order only the office design?

Yes. But the risks are lowest when design, renovation and furniture are handled by one team — then decisions from the drawings are not lost on site.

What is better for an office — open space or private offices?

It depends on the team's processes: how many people work in quiet, how many are on calls, how often clients visit. Most often we design a mixed layout — open seats, phone booths, meeting rooms and a quiet zone.

Do you work with companies?

Yes: a contract with your company, invoicing for companies registered in Ukraine and closing documents. Invoicing for EU companies is being set up.

Can you finish before our move-in date?

The schedule and handover date are fixed in the contract. Furniture from China is ordered as soon as the specification is approved — delivery takes 35–70 days, in parallel with the renovation.

Can you source office furniture?

Yes. Workstations, chairs, storage, lighting and decor, directly from factories in China.

What warranty do you give?

12 months on completed works; photo reports from site every 1–2 weeks.
