Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026 · Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CAS Development ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our content-publishing application ("the App"), including its integration with TikTok and other social media platforms.

In short: the App is an internal marketing-automation tool. It publishes content that we create to our own social media accounts. We do not collect personal data from the general public, we do not sell data, and we do not use data for advertising profiles.

1. Who we are

CAS Development is an architecture, interior design, and product-sourcing business operating the brand "China Opt Shop". Contact: china.opt.shop@gmail.com, phone/WhatsApp +380 97 866 7755, Kyiv, Ukraine.

2. What the App does

The App is a content-scheduling and publishing tool. It prepares posts (images, video, and captions) and publishes them to social media accounts that we own and operate, including our TikTok account, via the official platform APIs.

3. Information we collect

  • Platform access credentials. OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms when we authorize the App on our own accounts. Tokens are used solely to publish and manage our own content.
  • Basic account information. Account identifiers, display name, and avatar of our own connected accounts, as returned by the platform APIs.
  • Content data. The media files and captions that we create for publication.
  • Technical logs. Standard execution logs (timestamps, status codes, error messages) generated by our automation server for reliability and debugging.

The App does not collect data about TikTok users other than our own account, does not read direct messages, does not track viewers, and does not collect data from minors.

4. How we use information

  • To publish and manage content on our own social media accounts;
  • To monitor whether a publication succeeded or failed;
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the App.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Data is shared only with the platforms themselves (TikTok, Meta, Telegram) through their official APIs as required to publish content, and with infrastructure providers that host our automation server. These providers process data solely on our instructions.

6. Data storage and security

Access tokens and logs are stored on access-controlled servers. Tokens are kept in encrypted credential storage, are never published, and are rotated or revoked if compromise is suspected. We retain logs only as long as needed for troubleshooting, after which they are deleted.

7. Your rights

If you believe we hold any personal data about you, you may contact us at china.opt.shop@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion. We respond within 30 days.

8. Data deletion

Platform access can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's security settings, which immediately invalidates the App's tokens. You may also email us to request deletion of any stored data related to you.

9. Children's privacy

The App is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
